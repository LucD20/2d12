# The cleanup audit: what the applied answers changed

Written by `node` from `applied-B1.json`, `applied-B2.json` and `applied-B3.json` (2026-11-26). 241 entries applied to the book (238 in full, 3 partly); 65 change a rule or settle which of two readings holds; the rest are wording, cuts of repeats and leftovers.

## Rule changes

- **B1-006:** Saves: a Save Action's DC is 11 + the Modifier or Attribute its traits name; any other Save uses the DC its effect writes, or 13 if none.
- **B1-022:** The Notation row for Critical names all three causes, including three matching dice on a Favored roll.
- **B1-023:** Up to: a maximum you can go below, even to none, except targets: 'up to three creatures' is one, two or three.
- **B1-025:** An encounter can't end while a creature is Dying or Shattered until each stabilizes, gets its damage below its Health Pool, or dies; the tip says an ally can easily get them back up.
- **B1-026:** A creature that becomes Dying or Shattered outside an encounter rolls on its Table, and that is its roll for the encounter that follows.
- **B1-028:** Swapping Initiative: the AP you had left are frozen and pay for nothing, not even a Reaction (your Reaction Point still can); on your new turn you carry on with them and none becomes Guard.
- **B1-029:** The Reaction Point pays for Reactions and for the few cards that say you spend it (Smite, Steady Draw); the Glossary row says the same.
- **B1-030:** The Guard from unspent AP lasts until you regain AP at the start of your next turn; Chapter 4 says it lasts until you regain AP again; Rounds says nothing else lasts until the start of your next turn.
- **B1-033:** An Interrupt happens before the moment it names (example: entering a square adjacent to you); a Trigger after the moment it names, which can be mid-Action (after the first farther square while still moving).
- **B1-037:** Disrupting Actions: a Reaction disrupts only when its card says so; an Interrupt's disruption means the Action never starts, a Trigger's stops it where it is. Both Stand-then-Advance examples are removed.
- **B1-038:** Moving closer / away is judged square by square: entering a square nearer (farther) than the one it came from; the repeated sentence is cut.
- **B1-039:** Moving Closer ends: Evade, Forced Movement and Reposition don't trigger Reactions from movement, so they are never moving closer or moving away; the bullet in Moving Away is removed.
- **B1-044:** Linked Actions: a required one you can't make stops the Action there (what happened stays, cost paid); an optional one is skipped. The separate 'no valid target' sentence is removed.
- **B1-055:** EoT: one gained on your own turn ends at its end; one gained on another creature's turn lasts until the end of your next turn.
- **B1-058:** Momentum gaining: +1 after each encounter overcome; until non-fight scenes have rules the GM says which count.
- **B1-061:** Squeezing: while a creature stands where it doesn't fit and is not mid-movement, its Rolls are Doomed and it is Vulnerable half its level.
- **B1-062:** Chapter 7, Move: you can't end in another creature's space nor enter an unwilling creature's space; a willing creature's space is Difficult Terrain.
- **B1-063:** Creatures in your way: an Unconscious creature and a dead one are Difficult Terrain; a creature that is Down and still fighting holds its space like any other.
- **B1-065:** Pulled: the cone is measured from the square the creature starts in; every path square lies less than 45° off the line from that square to the origin.
- **B1-074:** Senses table, last column: Obscured '1 Field Boon, if you are Clear to it'; Hidden and Unsensed 'Favored, if you are Clear or Obscured to it'.
- **B1-077:** The Snake example: its Favored is 5 Boons, less its 1 Field Bane: it rolls normally and adds 4.
- **B1-082:** Rally Points: 'While Down or Unconscious, you can't use Steel Yourself'; the Unconscious bullet 'You can't wake up while Dying...' is removed. The Chapter 3 text was not written: B1-063's text for the same sentence was applied instead.
- **B1-089:** The Dying Table's Tier 3: 'You stabilize: you are no longer Dying, but you stay Unconscious.' (no roll); Shattered now uses Dying's tables (B1-090).
- **B1-091:** Unconscious: damage while both Unconscious and Down makes you roll on the Dying or Shattered Table (becoming one first if needed); an NPC doesn't roll, NPCs Going Down decides again.
- **B2-005:** Distinct Choices now reads: each of your class's Attribute increases goes to a different Attribute; a species' and a class's increase can go to the same Attribute (the old example was replaced with the section).
- **B2-010:** Chapter 1's Check row: one Attribute Modifier against a DC (a "Body Check"); a card can call for one inside an Action (Break Free, Tumble, Aid), and that roll is a Check, not an Attribute Action.
- **B2-012:** Chapter 1: a Tier of a Weapon Action that writes no [W] deals its damage as the weapon's damage too, with the weapon's type and subtype.
- **B2-014:** Charge stops on the first square from which the target is within the normal Range of the weapon you attack with.
- **B2-016:** Difficult Terrain: a movement of at least 1 square can always enter one square, whatever it costs, with an example (Mark Crawling into rubble while Prone, then Advancing with Speed 1).
- **B2-019:** Feint's Critical: until the end of your turn, the target can't use a Reaction for which you are the triggering creature.
- **B2-020:** Gear: Fists are used with the Fists Basic Action, and with a Weapon Action that isn't the Basic Attack.
- **B2-022:** Haul has a Special line: you can't choose your own square as the destination.
- **B2-024:** Hide has a Special line: Senses don't change this roll: you don't need to perceive the targets.
- **B2-030:** Point Out's Tiers 2 and 3: the ally perceives the target 1 step better: Unsensed becomes Hidden, whatever the reason; Hidden from Hide goes back to what its own senses give; never better than you perceive it.
- **B2-031:** Point Out's Target: one creature you perceive (no Line of Effect needed).
- **B2-032:** Chapter 1: unless its card names another DC, an Action that rolls an Attribute against a creature rolls against that creature's DC of the same Attribute.
- **B2-036:** Reload has a Special above its roll: with a ranged weapon Reload is a [Ranged] Action, so its roll is Doomed while you are Engaged.

- **B2-046:** An Action with more than one Attribute trait: you choose which of them to roll.
- **B2-058:** Blinded: your Sight perceives nothing and no Sight Sense helps; you perceive creatures only with your other senses; every square is Difficult Terrain for you; any Roll that needs sight is Doomed.

- **B2-069:** Human: both choices are made when you build the character and can't be changed later.
- **B2-075:** Two-handed: [Strike, Weapon] Actions with it held in one hand take 3 Banes, and the off-hand Banes too if that hand is your off hand.
- **B2-081:** An open box under Armor: Wards and their Ward values are not written yet; a character's Ward value is 0 for now.

- **B3-073:** Shriek and Scatter has a Distance of 6 sq., a Target of one ally with the Goblin Tag and "Trigger: the target goes Down." (chapter and card); Backhand's stat block names its target, the triggering creature.
