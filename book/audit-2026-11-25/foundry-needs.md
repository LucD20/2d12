## Built in v0.0.520

- **B1-006 (rule):** a Save a card asks for outside a [Save] Action uses DC 13 when it writes none: `module/inline.mjs` `saveDcFor` (a chip on an item that isn't a Save Action: 13) and `module/actions.mjs` (the chat card's `saveDc` flag: 13 unless the Action is a Save Action). No card has such a chip today (checked: no `@Save[...]` in the compendium), so nothing that plays changes.
- **B1-026 (rule):** a Dying or Shattered Table rolled outside an encounter is that creature's roll for the encounter that follows: `module/down.mjs` `carriedTable` (pure) and `becomeDying` (the mark "none" moves to the encounter, no second roll).
- **B1-038 (rule):** moving closer is judged square by square (a square nearer than the one it came from): `module/moment.mjs` `closerStep` (pure), `moveMoment`'s `closer` and the Move back square (`nearer`: against the square before, not where the movement started).
- **B1-063 (rule):** only an Unconscious or a dead creature is Difficult Terrain in your way; one Down and still fighting (Desperate, Dying or Shattered, awake) blocks like any other: `module/move.mjs` `downTok` (an NPC at 0 stays passable: it is out of the fight).
- **B1-089 (rule):** a collapsed creature's Tier 3 stabilizes with no roll on the Table: `module/down.mjs` `endOfTurn`.
- **B1-091 (rule), the PC part:** damage while both Unconscious and Down rolls on the Dying or Shattered Table, becoming one first by the Dominant Damage if it was neither: `module/down.mjs` `afterDamage`. A Desperate (awake) creature doesn't roll by this rule (its own branch comes first). The NPC part is already so: `afterDamage` runs for characters only, NPCs Going Down is the GM's.
- **B2-009:** Advance's `foundry.sub` follows the new text ("Advance your Speed" is the Move button): `book/cards/basic-actions.json`; and a book link in a movement-only Version no longer stops it moving at once (`module/actions.mjs` `pureMove`: Advance's "see Advance" is an `@Book` link).
- **B2-010 (rule):** Break Free's roll (its "Check" line, vs. the Action DC) is a Check for every effect that names Checks (`__check`, not `__attr`): `module/rollsource.mjs` (`check` on the roll source, `rollTraits`). Tumble's and Aid's Checks were already Check buttons (`inline.mjs` `doCheck`).

- **B2-019 (rule):** Feint's Critical blocks only the Reactions for which the feinter is the triggering creature (the mover, or the source of the Strike or damage): `module/moment.mjs` `collectReactions` (it also blocked a Reaction whose target or subject was the feinter).
- **B2-020 (rule):** the Basic Attack never offers the Fists (`module/fists.mjs` `weaponsFor`); with no weapon in hand it is refused with a note to use the Fists Basic Action (`module/actions.mjs` `useAction`). Other Weapon Actions still can use them.

## Open

- **B1-023 (rule):** an "up to N targets" Action needs at least one. The system lets every Action roll with no target (the roll dialog shows "No target" with DC 13: Lucas, v0.0.393). A warning for an "Up to" Target would be small, but it reverses his ruling: his call.
- **B1-033 (rule):** a Reaction comes at the moment it names (a movement Trigger mid-move). The movement moments are read after the drag (`module/moment.mjs` `moveMoment`), with Move back to the square where it would have come. Stopping a drag at that square is a rework of the movement Reactions: large.
- **B2-014 (rule):** Charge stops on the first square from which the target is within the normal Range of the weapon you attack with. `module/move.mjs` (the Charge picker, `computeTarget`) stops at the creature's Reach. Same for a melee weapon; different for a ranged, thrown or Long one. Needs the Basic Attack's weapon and its normal Range in the picker: small to medium.
- **B2-015 (Shake Off's face):** the book's card has a Save line and three Tiers; Foundry's is one Effect (the Save button and the three results in its text), because its roll is made by the Lingering creature through its button. t260 prints this one difference. Drawing the book's lines needs a roll line that a button makes (`cardmodel.mjs`, `cardlines.mjs`, the chat card): small to medium.
- **B2-016 (rule):** a movement of at least 1 square can always enter one square, whatever it costs (Crawl 1 into Difficult Terrain). The movement budget (`module/move.mjs`) charges 2 for it, so Crawl 1 or Speed 1 can't enter. A floor of one square in the cost check: small to medium, in the movement code.
- **B2-030 / B2-031 (rule), the Unsensed part:** Point Out improves Unsensed to Hidden "whatever the reason", never better than you perceive it. `module/hide.mjs` `improve` changes only a Hide state (Unsensed or Hidden from Hide); a creature Unsensed by darkness or walls isn't changed, and nothing caps it at your own perception. Needs a per-observer "perceived as Hidden" in `senses.mjs perceive`: medium. (No Line of Effect needed is already so: see below.)
- **B2-061 (rule):** Empowered's die takes a pool and subtype you choose among the Action's damages. Foundry gives it the first damage's (`module/explode.mjs` `empoweredComponent`). A choice (the pick bar) when an Action deals two kinds: small to medium.
- **B2-069 (rule):** Human's choices can't be changed after the character is built. The sheet's Species window (`module/specieswindow.mjs` `editSpecies`, the sheet's Choices link) changes them at any time. Locking it needs a moment when creation ends (the first class level confirmed?): small, Lucas to say when.
- **B3-077 (rule):** a thick wall blocks Hearing. Hearing passes every wall (`module/senses.mjs` `hears`, the Hearing detection mode `walls: false`). Needs a way to mark a thick wall (a wall that blocks Sound?) and a wall test on the line: small to medium.
- **B3-026 (rule):** Shifting Aim on another creature's turn counts a move made during that turn. Foundry reads the Archer's own turn only (`module/hooks.mjs` `strikeHookChipsFor`: no Boon off its turn). Needs the moved flag keyed to the turn in progress for this hook (the "left its square" of other hooks stays the Archer's turn): small.
- **B3-030 / B3-031 (rule):** Hit and Run: enemies adjacent before the first square, once each per weapon, the bow's target can take the knife; one 3d12 roll, each hit worked out on its own. Bronze: by hand (its `automationNote` now says the book's way). Automating it is a new mechanism: large.
- **B3-022 (rule):** the free re-Judge is a Reaction (0R; Staggered stops it). Judge's 0 AP use from its "went Down" message is used as an Action (`module/mark.mjs` messages, `actions.mjs`): Staggered doesn't stop it. Its card says "the 0 AP use ... as a 0R: by hand". Making that use a Reaction (its moment, Staggered): small to medium.
