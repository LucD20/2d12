The book is built from `rules-src/` with `build_rules.py` (assets in `rules-assets/`). How the files are written is in `rules-src/README.md`; `rules-src/INDEX.md` (generated) is a map of every chapter; `python3 build_rules.py --where Term` lists every mention of a term. ## Round 9 (applied to the book and to Foundry v0.0.105; the list below is what was decided)

- **Movement words.** Only Move Actions are "Move" (an Action with the Move trait: Advance, Charge, Crawl, Stand); never a bare capital "Move" as a verb. "You haven't moved" is your own movement (Advance, Charge, Crawl, Evade; not Stand; not Forced Movement). - **Disrupting part of an Action.** Disrupting an Action that is part of a larger one stops the larger one only if that part is necessary for it. What already happened stays, the AP stays spent, and a disrupted movement stops in the square the creature was in.
