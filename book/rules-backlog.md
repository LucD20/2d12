# Core Rules: changes applied to the book, and what is open

The book is built from `rules-src/` with `build_rules.py` (assets in `rules-assets/`). How the files are written is in `rules-src/README.md`; `rules-src/INDEX.md` (generated) is a map of every chapter; `python3 build_rules.py --where Term` lists every mention of a term. The full earlier version of this list was in `archive/old-notes/rules-backlog-history.md` (not in this bundle).

## Round 9 (applied to the book and to Foundry v0.0.105; the list below is what was decided)
- **Dark fights.** Under the Senses table: "You gain no Favored or boon from a creature's poor perception of you if you perceive that creature as Hidden." Two creatures that are Hidden to each other are Doomed only (the old sentence "Hidden to each other: Favored and Doomed, normal" goes). Asymmetric cases are unchanged. Dim light is unchanged (mutual Obscured: 1 bane against 1 boon cancels). Foundry: the Favored from the target's perception needs the attacker not to perceive the target as Hidden.
- **Movement words.** Only Move Actions are "Move" (an Action with the Move trait: Advance, Charge, Crawl, Stand); never a bare capital "Move" as a verb. "You haven't moved" is your own movement (Advance, Charge, Crawl, Evade; not Stand; not Forced Movement). "You haven't left your square" is any change of your square, Forced Movement included (a Large creature: any change of the squares it occupies, "space"). A small box in Advance and Evade with this table, and matching glossary entries: Advance/Charge/Crawl = Move Action, moved, left your square; Evade = not a Move Action, moved, left your square; Stand = Move Action, not moved, not left; Forced Movement (Pushed, Reposition...) = not a Move Action, not moved, left your square. Features say which "since": "since your last turn" is since the end of your last turn, "this turn" is since the start of your turn. Foundry: store the square at the start and the end of each turn; a flag set only by your own movement.
- **Reactions: Interrupt and Trigger.** The card's line says the kind: "Interrupt: ..." resolves before the Action that triggers it; "Trigger: ..." resolves after it. Both cost 1R, once per triggering Action, and several creatures can react. Only Interrupts can disrupt. Foundry: store the kind; no automation (used by hand).
- **Opportunity Attack** becomes an Interrupt: "A creature within 3 squares leaves a square during a Move Action or uses the Stand Action." Once per triggering Action (a multi-square Advance is one trigger).
- **Disrupting part of an Action.** Disrupting an Action that is part of a larger one stops the larger one only if that part is necessary for it. What already happened stays, the AP stays spent, and a disrupted movement stops in the square the creature was in. A Charge disrupted before its 3 necessary squares is cancelled whole (no Weapon Attack); disrupted after them, it ends there and the attack happens if there is a valid target.
- **"Moves closer to you."** Interrupts: per step (a square it enters is nearer to you, 1-2-1-2 count, than the one it left); Triggers: the whole movement may be compared. Lateral steps that don't change the count don't count. Forced Movement doesn't trigger Reactions; Evade doesn't either.
- **Movement cap.** When you move with an Action you can't move more than your current Speed (when the movement starts), per movement and not per turn; Forced Movement is not capped; abilities that go beyond it say so or change Speed (Battering Charge: "Speed counts as 2 higher").
- **Stacking.** The same effect never stacks unless it says so; a repeated one keeps the higher value and the longer duration; "increase your ... value by 1" stacks; boons and banes are the exception. Applies to all named conditions.
- **Terms.** Creatures get **Tags** (TBD; not "traits", which belong to Actions). New trait **Fear** (no rule of its own; other features reference it). Cut Through's Setup: "Evade 2" (not "Evade up to 2").
- **Empowered / Enfeebled: decided (A).** Empowered N adds N to every damage die; Enfeebled N subtracts N from every die, each die at least 1; no hard caps yet. (Before: being redesigned.) Decided so far: both have a value N; the same effect doesn't stack; Empowered and Enfeebled net (Empowered minus Enfeebled) and the result applies once to each damage instance; flat numbers are untouched. The ladder (1d4 ... 2d12, then +1, +2...) with Enfeebled's floor at 1d4 is not final; options are being compared.
