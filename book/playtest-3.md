## Against playtest 2 (first look; the ruler checks it in pack R's prep)
- **4 rounds** again (the book's target is 3½). **The PCs dealt 249 (62 a round), the same as playtest 2 (63); the foes dealt 143 (36 a round,
  playtest 2: 47).** Taking NPC Weapon Attack away cut the NPCs' damage by about a quarter, as expected.
- **One PC Down (DP, round 1, up in round 3), not three.** Rally Points: HP 4, HA 1, EA 1 (playtest 2: DP used all 8).
- **HP still spent most turns healing** (LoH 3A twice on itself): the heal loop is smaller but there.
- **Reactions:** one SoF and Mark Quarry swaps; still few (pack R's Reaction AP is for this).
