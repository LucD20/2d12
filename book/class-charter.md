# The class charter (2026-11-14, for the class redesign)

A prep document: Claude's defaults, **Lucas answers by exception** ("all defaults except 3 and 6"). Nothing here is in the book. It replaces the charter of 2026-10-29 (`archive/book-drafts/class-charter-2026-10-29.md`), written for 5 AP, spent Escalation and Talents. What stands behind it: rules-backlog.md "THE CLASS REDESIGN: DECIDED", `book/cards/paladin-analyses.md`, `book/research/` (`ability-scope.md` and the five notes of 2026-11-14).

**Status (Lucas, 2026-11-14): all nine questions answered.**

## 1. The kinds of Action

A kind is a design label, not a trait: it is not printed on the card. Every card has one main kind. A class's Stage 0 list must hold every kind (section 4).

| Kind | What it is | The test | Examples (sketches, not cards) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strike** | Damage and at most one rider. | It rolls damage against an enemy. | A Smite cut to its core. |
| **Help** | One thing for an ally: a Rally Point, Guard, a step, a condition ended. | Its target is an ally and it has no damage. | Lay On Hands; Shield of Faith. |
| **Compression** | Two things you could already do, for less AP or at a time you couldn't. No new number. | Remove the card and you can still do both, slower. | "Advance, then Shove"; "Stand and Advance"; the touch as you pass. |
| **Rule-bender** | It changes a rule, not a number. Usually a Feature or a Passive. | Its text has no dice and no "+". | Creatures in your light can't Hide; you carry a Down ally at full Speed. |
| **Art** (the utility category; name: question 1) | A small tool for the board: position, light, senses, objects, a condition named by the situation. **No damage.** One card, several small uses. | It is the card you look at when attacking isn't the answer. | A light you place; a challenge that moves an enemy's attention; a ward on a doorway. |
| **Ribbon** | Flavor with a small or no effect in a fight. | Remove it and no fight changes. | Your holy symbol is a light source; you sense the Profane nearby. |

**What an Art is for (Lucas, 2026-11-15, on the first one):** "roleplay focused abilities that aren't strong by themselves, but in the right situation, it is thematic, cinematic and cool." An Art that is empty in half the fights and the best AP of the turn in the right one is doing its job.

Ribbons and Arts **outside combat** (exploration, social) wait for the Skills design (D3): until then a class has at most one Ribbon, inside a Feature.

## 2. The complexity budget

The cards met most are the simplest (research: `ability-scope.md`, takeaway 1). Words are counted on the effect text, without the Flavor and the Tier dice.

| Card | Versions | It does | Conditionals ("if the target is...") | Words, at most |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Feature | none | one sentence each | 0 | 30 |
| An At-will or Round Action (the Hallmark Actions) | 1 or 2 | one main thing, at most one rider | 0 | 35 a Version |
| A Reaction (the Hallmark Reactions) | 1 | one main thing, at most one rider; one moment the rules define | 0 | 35 |
| An Art | up to 3 | each Version one thing, no rider, no damage | 0 | 20 a Version |
| An Escalation Passive | none | one sentence | 0 | 30 |
| An Encounter Action | 1 or 2 | one main thing, up to two riders | 1 | 50 |
| The Apex (level 4, not written now) | 1 | as much as it needs | free | 70 |

Rules that go with it:
1. **Two Versions are two uses, not two sizes.** The second Version adds a different thing (a move, a second target, a rider), never only bigger dice. A card that only scales has one Version.
2. **Each Tier's line holds its whole result** (Lucas, 2026-11-14; Draw Steel and 4e write it so): the damage, then a semicolon, then what else that Tier does ("[V] 1[W] + [Mod] + [R] 1d6 Ho; Immobilized (1 round)"). At the table you roll, find your Tier and read one line. The Effects row is only for what happens whatever the Tier. **This is a rule for the whole book (Lucas, 2026-11-15: "Yes"): "Effects happen on Tier 2+" is removed.** An Effects entry is an extra thing the Action does, whatever the roll; what happens only on some Tiers is written on those Tiers' lines (or as "Tier 1 or 2: ..."). It replaces the book's convention (Effects = Tier 2 or 3; on a Save Action, Tier 1 and 2) **in one pass over every card when the redesigned classes go in, never card by card** (a book where Effects means two things is worse than either). A Tier 1 that does something stays a choice per card, not a default.
2c. **One way to write "only on some Tiers": a Tier line** ("**Tier 1 or 2:** Prone"; a Tier that isn't written does nothing). The book's parentheses ("Immobilized (T3)", "(T2/T3)": Chapter 2, Effect Timing) are retired in the Effects pass: they save two words and pile onto the duration's parentheses (Lucas asked whether the space was worth it, 2026-11-15; Claude's default, his to revert).
2d. **Durations: only EoT and rounds** (Lucas, 2026-11-15), counted as the book counts them today; a thing a creature holds up is written "(1 round)"; no "until your next turn starts", and **no "save ends"**: where a condition should outlast a turn without a count, the creature pays to end it (Break Free, Stand, Shake Off).
2b. **A Tier 3 visibly beats a Tier 2** (Lucas, 2026-11-14: "Rolling a tier 3 and seeing it does the same as tier 2 isnt great"). The weapon's own Tier 3 is already bigger, but "1[W]" reads the same on both lines: the Tier 3 line shows a bigger die or one more thing.
3. **A card names at most one condition** (Frightened, Prone...), the Apex excepted.
4. **A Passive changes a decision or fires on a moment the table sees.** Never a silent bonus ("+2 damage", "+1 Defense"). The Escalation 6 Passive is a "when your Escalation becomes 6" moment (it is seldom reached: `escalation-and-guard.md`).
5. **Every level 1 Action works in round 1.** Only the Passives and the Apex read Escalation.
6. **A number on a card is a die or a Modifier already on the sheet**, never a new counter, unless Lucas decides a class gets one.
7. **A class grants an Action directly** (Lucas, 2026-11-14): no Feature that only says "you gain the X Action". The Action's title bar says "Paladin Feature".
8. **A later Feature or Passive can expand an Action you already have** (Lucas, 2026-11-14): the added clause is written into the Action's own text, at most one a Stage, and it is a standing effect or uses the trigger the card already watches, never a new trigger to track.
10. **A Setup is optional by default** (Lucas, 2026-11-14): the card writes "You Advance...", and "must" when it isn't optional. **A move toward a creature is a straight line to it:** closer, never around.
11. **A card that counts "your Reactions" or "your Actions" names a trait, never all of them** (Lucas, 2026-11-15: the build is more Talents than class, so "when you use a Reaction" is too broad). The Paladin's is [Divine]; a later Talent can carry the trait to work with the class's cards.
12. **A Passive is a standing sentence; a "moment" ability is an Action** (Lucas and Claude, 2026-11-15, with Reckoning). An Escalation ability that has a Setup, traits, an Area or a Save is written as a 0 AP Action with an Escalation line (Chapter 2 already has it: the Apex), so the Actions' rules apply with no extra note. A rung gives "an Escalation ability": a Passive, or such an Action.
9. **Flavor describes the Action** (Lucas, 2026-11-14: the first drafts "read too much like AI text"): one sentence, "You..." or "Your...", present tense: what the character does, what is seen, a hint of the effect. Models: Draw Steel ("You utter a prayer that outlines your foe in holy energy."), 4e ("Your weapon fills with divine radiant energy, which bursts forth as you strike your enemy.").

## 3. The budget

Unchanged from the PC budget (`book/cards/README.md`): a plain PC's three Weapon Attacks are 100 points, a class is about **130 / 140 / 150** at levels 1 / 2 / 3. Anchors: a Weapon Attack 25, 1 Health 12, 1 Guard 6, an unused AP 12, an Encounter Action double its AP (the top of the one designer's range found: 13th Age's targets are 1.5 times for a per-battle attack, 2 times for a daily; `nova-burst.md`, takeaway 4), a 1R Reaction 10 to 15 a turn, a Passive about +10 a level. **Rough anchors only** (the SIM is a white room); table feel and the research decide.

- **An Art, a Rule-bender and a Ribbon cost nothing of the budget** unless they heal, give Guard or deal damage. They are where a class gets wider without getting stronger.
- A Compression is priced as the AP it saves on the turns it would be used (about 12 for an AP).

## 4. The level lists

The book's shape for levels 1 to 3 stays (Chapter 5); the redesign adds the Arts and says how many cards each slot offers. **In bold: what is new.**

| Level | You gain | The class offers |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | 2 Features · a Hallmark Action · a Hallmark Reaction · **an Art** · the Escalation 2 Passive · a Skill Talent (empty for now) | 2 Features (fixed) · 2 Hallmark Actions · 2 Hallmark Reactions · **3 Arts** · 2 Passives |
| **2** | the Escalation hook · an Encounter Pick · the Escalation 4 Passive · a Talent (empty) | 1 hook (fixed) · 3 Encounter Actions · 2 Passives |
| **3** | **a second Art** · the Escalation 6 Passive · a Skill Talent (empty) | the same 3 Arts · 2 Passives |
| 4 (not now) | the Apex, the Stage 1 numbers | |

- **18 cards a class** for Stage 0: 2 Features, 2 Hallmark Actions, 2 Hallmark Reactions, 3 Arts, 6 Passives, 3 Encounter Actions. (Today's Paladin: 25, with the Dailies and the Apexes.)
- **The two Features are the class in one line each, and every character of the class has them.** One is its identity mechanic (the Archer: Mark Quarry). The other is its Rule-bender. Either can hold a 0 AP everyday Action.
- **The kinds a class must cover by level 3:** a Strike and a Help or Compression among the two Hallmark Actions (so the choice is a style, not a role); a Rule-bender among the Features; a Compression somewhere; no kind more than half the list.
- **The two Hallmark Reactions share a trigger and a payload where they can, and differ by the rider** (the Pathfinder 2e Champion's causes: `paladins-other-games.md`, takeaway 2). Both aim at firing in about half the rounds (decided 2026-11-12).
- Swapping on a Rest stays: every choice above can be changed on a Rest.

## 5. Questions (my default first)

1. **DECIDED: "Art".** The utility category's name: "Art" ("a Paladin Art", "an Archer Art"; short, serious, no clash with a trait or with Weapon Techniques). Others: Craft, Knack, Gift, Discipline. A class can give its Arts its own word in the heading ("Paladin Arts: Prayers").
2. **DECIDED: one at level 1, another at level 3.** An Art at level 1 and a second at level 3, from 3 offered. Or: both at level 1; or one only.
3. **DECIDED: try 3 ("if it feels too much, we remove one").** An Art has up to 3 Versions, each one thing, no damage: a small menu of different uses, all yours when you pick the Art. Or: 2 uses at most; or each use is its own Art. Two sketches to show the shape (not proposals): *Lantern of Faith*: 1 AP, you shed Bright light in a 3 Emanation until the encounter ends · 1 AP, one creature within 5 squares that you see stops being Hidden and can't Hide for 1 round · 2 AP, a 2 Burst within 10 squares is Bright light for 3 rounds. *Hold the Line*: 1 AP, until your next turn the squares adjacent to you are Difficult Terrain for enemies · 1 AP, you swap places with a willing adjacent ally, and neither movement triggers Reactions · 2 AP, you Advance, and a Prone or Down ally you pass moves with you.
4. **DECIDED ("makes sense").** Two Versions must be two uses, not two sizes (section 2, rule 1). This ends the 1 / 2 / 3 AP ladders on Smite, Lay On Hands, Haul, Shove and the like for class cards (the Basic Actions keep theirs).
5. **DECIDED ("good").** Passives are never a silent bonus (rule 4). It retires Radiant Zeal, Searing Blows, Font of Mercy and Hallowed Stance as written.
6. **DECIDED: A, nothing new to track** ("While I like the pool of points, I dont think the game needs more tracking"). He asked whether Escalation could be the pool, spent on abilities as before pack R; Claude's answer: it would hurt (spending turns the Escalation Passives off, as the old charter found; every class and the Elites share that ladder; Momentum would become a starting purse); abilities can **read** Escalation without spending it. **Does a class track a number of its own?** Three ways to build the Paladin's burst. **A, nothing new to track (the default):** the burst spends what is already on the sheet ("3 AP; you can also spend your Reaction AP: 2d8 more", or "1d8 more for each Escalation above 1"). Nothing to remember; the cost is real (no Reaction that round). **B, one charge, on or off:** "When you use a Reaction to protect an ally, you are Charged. Your next divine Strike spends it: 2d8 more." One token, never two (Nimble's paladin works this way; its designer moved to it from a stack of dice). Protecting visibly loads the burst. **C, a pool of points:** "Wrath: 2 at the start of each turn, 1 more when the enemy you judged damages you; 1 Wrath protects, 5 pays the big Strike" (Draw Steel's Censor). The deepest decision each round, a number to track every round, and a slow first round (a guide: "Not enough to do anything powerful"). Default: A, with B allowed where a card needs it; C only if Lucas asks for it.
7. **DECIDED, as a try ("we can try that. Need to be careful to not lose the identity").** The pieces that make the class are Features every character has; the Hallmark choice is a style. For the Paladin it means the mark, the Rally Point touch and the burst are not behind an either / or (`paladins-other-games.md`, takeaway 8). Or: keep today's "Smite or Lay On Hands" split.
8. **DECIDED ("makes sense").** 3 Encounter Actions offered (4 today), 2 Passives a rung (2 or 3 today).
9. **DECIDED: test both.** Where a level 1 character's burst lives. The Encounter Pick comes at level 2 and the Apex at level 4, so a level 1 Paladin has no "explode". Default: **a class whose brief has a burst gets it small at level 1, inside a Feature**, and the Encounter Pick is the bigger one. Or: that class takes its Encounter Pick at level 1 (the old charter's answer).

## Rules learned in the two redesigns (the Paladin and the Archer, 2026-11-14 to 16; Lucas's, for every later class and for the weapons)

**How to work a class.**
1. **His brief first, in his words;** a table of each of its lines against the cards it asks for. Research on the brief before any card (`book/research/`), then one decision at a time: the numbers, the mechanic, the two Features, the Hallmark Actions, the Hallmark Reactions, the Arts (themes first), the Escalation abilities (the ladder first) and the hook, the Encounter Picks (themes first), then the whole class on one page.
2. **A record and a reading page a class:** `book/cards/<class>-redesign.md` (every decision with his words) and `book/cards/<class>-desk/index.html` (an artifact: "What I need from you now" on top, the cards drawn in the Structure, the price tables). Both updated with every decision.
3. **When he asks about a mechanic: the rules and `book/research/` first, a search only for what they lack.** Before pricing how often something happens, look at what the ruler has measured (`book/ruler/reactions.md`: a Reaction on "moves closer" fires in 56 to 73% of rounds).
4. **A structural choice is offered three ways;** a card's choices list every candidate the rules offer, the rejected ones with their reason.
5. **He answers "Proposed" for yes.** A change of his is applied and the card is then decided; nothing goes into the book until he says so.

**How a card is written** (the Structure: `book/cards/card-structure.md`).
6. **Don't invent a word the book already has** ("arrows" were [W]: "2[W]" is the weapon twice).
7. **Every Tier's line holds its whole result; a Tier 3 adds something beyond the weapon's own Tier 3 damage** (a rider, one more [W]); a Tier 1 can deal [Mod] ("[V] [Mod]", no subtype: the Modifier takes the weapon's). **The exception** (Lucas, 2026-11-18, the book audit): an Art's Mode is a small effect, so its Tier 3 can do what its Tier 2 does ("Tier 2 or 3": Trick Shots' Pin and Startle).
8. **Fixed dice are written on the Tiers; a named value ("Smite dice") only when the count changes.**
9. **A few cards get their own Critical entry, to be unique; no Critical line a class.** A Critical is about 3% of rolls.
10. **A second Version only when it does a different thing, never a bigger one** (`book/research/versions-criticals.md`). The Arts' Modes are that idea at one cost.
11. **A Passive's sentence has no "While your Escalation is N or more":** the bar says it.
12. **Durations are EoT or rounds only;** no "until your next turn", no "save ends".
13. **A weapon property is written before the word "weapon":** "a Light weapon", capitalized, no brackets.
14. **A lasting area names the side it affects in its sentence** ("Difficult Terrain for enemies") and has no Target line; the Target line is for whom the Action affects when it is used.
15. **Line of Effect is needed for everything a card does to a creature,** on the Target line or in the text; "(no Line of Effect needed)" is the exception. An effect on one creature that others gain from (Vulnerable, Call Out) needs no line to the others.
16. **A Reaction has a Target line and its Interrupt or Trigger is written about "the target".** A trigger that fires most rounds is disruptive (an extra roll outside anyone's turn every round): choose a rarer moment.
17. **Flavor describes what is done and seen** ("You..."), never a mood line.

**What a class should be.**
18. **No one best button:** the turn is built from several Round cards; one roll, several hits beats several rolls (his playtest: Double Shot was "quite boring").
19. **A pair to choose from differs in shape, not in role** (depth against width; flee or shoot), and the two are the same size.
20. **Teamwork even in a class that stands alone** (the Archer's Clear Lines, Opening Shot, Call Out).
21. **No silent bonuses, no tracking across turns** if a "this turn" or "once a round" will do.
22. **A cost of the class's range or role is never erased for free** (Cover, Doomed while Engaged): a card lifts it for a price.
