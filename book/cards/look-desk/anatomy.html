Card Anatomy

Every kind of card the two classes and the weapons have, in the decided look (the panel, the bronze accent, Source Serif 4 and Source Sans 3), with the proposed anatomy written out first. The cards are drawn from the card files as they stand.

  1. The panel: a flat tint of the page, square corners, a 2 px bronze rule above and below. It is never split across a printed page.
  2. The name line: the name in bold, in bronze, at the left; at the right, in bold black, the Escalation it needs, the frequency and the cost ("Escalation 2 · Passive", "Round · 1 or 2 AP").
  3. The Flavor: one line in the text face's italic, a shade lighter.
  4. The traits: bold, black, in group order, joined by a middle dot; a thin bronze rule under them. A card with no traits keeps the rule.
  5. The grid: Distance and Area at the left, Target at the right, the labels bold.
  6. The body: one line each, the label bold in a fixed column (the same width on every card, wide enough for "Tier 1 or 2" and "Requirement"), the text beside it.
  7. A Version or a Mode: its heading in bold bronze over a thin grey rule; a Mode's own traits after its name in black; its own Distance and Target under it.
  8. Inside the text: game terms in the text's color; a link underlined thinly; [V] and [R] damage in their pool colors; brackets ([Strike]) plain.
  9. The card's text in Source Sans 3; the page in Source Serif 4.
