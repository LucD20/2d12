Book Text Faces

Your pick, four mid-way options between Source Serif 4 and Alegreya, and Alegreya for reference. Each sets the same slice of the Paladin's page in the decided look: the bronze accent, the traits in black. Only the text face changes; the cards stay in the same sans (Source Sans 3), with their Flavor in the text face's italic. Switches to dark with your system.

Source Serif 4

Your pick. Frank Grießhammer for Adobe, after Fournier's 18th-century types: large x-height (0.475 of the em), optical sizes, the same family as the cards' sans. The clearest on screen.

Hallmark Action Selection

Choose one of the following Hallmark Actions. Both are Round Actions: each can be used once a round. Binding Light roots a foe for 1 round on a Tier 2 or 3; Lay On Hands gives an ally Guard equal to 2 + your Power Modifier, and at 2 AP lets them use 1 Rally Point.

Example. Lonny's Paladin has Power Modifier +2 and 20 Vigor. On round 3 he uses Binding Light against a goblin and rolls 17: a Tier 2. The goblin takes 1[W] + [Mod] and 1d6 Holy, and is Immobilized until the end of its next turn.

Piazzolla

Mid-way. Huerta Tipográfica, the foundry of Alegreya's designer (Juan Pablo del Peral), 2020: Alegreya's calligraphic family made sturdier and more open for compact reading, with optical sizes. X-height 0.478, as large as Source Serif 4's. The closest to "Alegreya, easier to read".
