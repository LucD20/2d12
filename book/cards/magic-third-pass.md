# Magic, the third pass: nineteen new spells under magic's own charter

**2026-11-18, asked by Lucas:** "Class charter isn't the magic charter, they are different so they are balanced differently; none is supposed to be stronger than the other, what makes something strong is the situation it is used in and how it is used. The limits we have been putting should be the only limiters/charter for now, nothing from the class. With this in mind, read the research and give me the summary, plus new cards if you don't have questions."

**A test, not a spell list. Nothing here is decided, nothing is in the book. Every number is a placeholder** [not priced: the SIM has no caster].

## What changed in how these are written

**Magic's charter is the list of limits in `book/magic-design.md` 5m and nothing else** (decision 47). So these cards drop what the first two passes took from the class charter:

| Dropped (a class rule) | So a spell may |
|---|---|
| One condition a card | Grade its result: a full effect on the worst Tier, a lighter one on the next |
| 35 words a Version; one main thing and one rider | Carry its own rules, as long as each is written |
| "Never only bigger"; no tracking across turns | Count things, fade, or grow a number when that is the spell |
| A Passive must be a visible moment; no silent bonus | Lay a standing effect on a field |

**Kept, because they are the book's own rules and not the class's:** Chapter 1 (Power Actions add no Modifier to damage; a Save's Tier 1 is the worst for the target; Several Targets), the card Structure's labels, Line of Effect, durations in EoT or rounds, and every decision of the magic record: the minimums, the riders, Overcast's ladders and small rungs, [Woven] and [Sustained], Overreach, Guard instead of Health, no whole turn taken from a major enemy or a PC, a [Spell] is Doomed while Engaged unless it is [Melee].

**Read with** the header of `magic-second-pass.md` (the bar, Overcast, the riders in full, Charmed and Confused). Merge and Overreach are on every spell and not repeated. [Focus] is on every spell and left off the trait lines.

**These nineteen were chosen from the research for what the first 26 lacked:** a second spell in each opening cell; each Path doing a job its own way; the things Dominions' magic is known for and no card had yet tried (weather laid on both sides, the elements answering each other, false damage, poison that works slowly, linked casters, a touch spell cast in melee).

## Fire

### 1. Burning Hands
*Dominions: Fire 1 / Evocation 1. "A melee-ranged square roast."*

> **Burning Hands** · Fire 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You spread your fingers and a sheet of flame washes over whoever stands before you.*
> [Power, Spell, Fire, Evocation, Melee, Save]
> Distance: Reach · Target: Up to two creatures adjacent to each other
> **Save:** Instinct
> **Tier 1:** [V] 2d6 Fi; Lingering 1d4 Fi (Bo/In).
> **Tier 2:** [V] 1d6 Fi.
> **Overcast:** *Breadth.* *Fire:* the flames find cloth: a target on a Tier 2 also takes the Lingering.

The second spell of the Fire 1 • Evocation 1 cell (a level 1 caster's two spells). **[Melee, Spell]:** cast while Engaged, it pays nothing (decision 46), which is the reason to stand in front with it.

### 2. Battle Fury
*Dominions: Fire 2 / Thaumaturgy 2. +2 attack, +2 morale. Fire's side of the mind is rage.*

> **Battle Fury** · Fire 1 • Thaumaturgy 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You lay a hot hand on your ally's shoulder, and the fear burns out of them.*
> [Power, Spell, Fire, Thaumaturgy]
> Distance: Reach · Target: One willing creature other than you
> **Effect:** For 2 rounds, the target is Empowered and ignores the Frightened condition, and it can't use Reactions.
> **Overcast:** *Breadth, Hold.* *Fire:* the rage wants blood: once on each of its turns, its first [Melee, Strike] Action that gets a Tier 3 lets it Advance 2.

A buff with a price, as Stoneskin is: rage gives and takes. Empowered and "ignores a condition" are Chapter 8's words.

## Air

### 3. Gust
*Dominions: Gust of Winds, Air 1 / Evocation 1. It stuns the small and the weak.*

> **Gust** · Air 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You throw your arm wide and a wall of wind goes with it.*
> [Power, Spell, Air, Evocation, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** Pushed 3; Prone.
> **Tier 2:** Pushed 1.
> **Special:** A Minor enemy is Pushed 2 more.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth.* *Air:* the wind carries: a creature Pushed into another creature's square stops before it, and both are Prone.

No damage at all: Air's rider is the whole spell (decision 24). Forced Movement and Steadiness already say how far it really goes.

### 4. Storm
*Dominions: Air 4 / Alteration 5, a battlefield enchantment on both sides. Precision halved, half of all missiles lost, nobody flies, burning put out; and the Air mage's own spells grow in it.*

> **Storm** · Air 2 • Enchantment 2 · Encounter · 2 AP
> *You raise both hands and the sky answers: wind, rain, and the dark under the cloud.*
> [Power, Spell, Air, Enchantment, Woven, Area]
> Area: every square within 10 squares of you when you cast (it doesn't move with you)
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, for every creature: a [Ranged, Strike] Action whose origin or target is in the Area takes 2 Pressure Banes; and a Lingering of Fire damage ends when its creature starts its turn in the Area.
> **Special:** While you are in the Area, your [Air] spells count your Escalation as 1 higher.
> **Overcast:** *Air:* you are the eye of it: the Banes don't apply to your own Actions.

The first card that touches **everyone**, allies included: your own Archer pays for it. That makes it a decision, where a one-sided aura would be a free bonus. Its Special is Dominions' Storm Power in 2d12's terms, and uses the fight's clock.

## Water

### 5. Freeze
*Dominions: Water 2 / Alteration 3. Slowed, and tiring every round until it thaws; heat thaws it.*

> **Freeze** · Water 1 • Alteration 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *Frost races up your foe's limbs and stiffens them.*
> [Power, Spell, Water, Alteration, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** Slowed 1 and Hindered (2 rounds).
> **Tier 2:** Hindered (1 round).
> **Special:** These end at once if the target takes Fire damage.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth, Hold.* *Water:* the ice bites: at the end of each of its turns while it is Slowed by this spell, it takes [V] 1d4 Co.

The graded result the class rule forbade: the full effect on a Tier 1, Water's rider alone on a Tier 2. Its Special is the elements answering each other, written on the card: an ally's fire undoes your ice.

### 6. Rain
*Dominions: Water 3 / Evocation 3, a battlefield enchantment. It puts out the burning and makes Fire spells cost half again.*

> **Rain** · Water 1 • Enchantment 2 · Round · 2 AP
> *You open your hand to the sky, and it lets go.*
> [Power, Spell, Water, Enchantment, Woven, Area]
> Area: every square within 10 squares of you when you cast (it doesn't move with you)
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, for every creature in the Area: a Lingering of Fire damage ends; and a [Fire] spell that would affect a square of the Area takes no Overcast steps.
> **Overcast:** *Water:* the ground turns to mud: the Area is also Difficult Terrain for every creature that isn't you.

**A spell that asks more of the School than of the Path** (Water 1 • Enchantment 2): a technique. It limits another caster through the rules magic already has (Overcast), with no number.

## Earth

### 7. Flying Shards
*Dominions: Earth 1, no research. "A blunderbuss of small rocks": four ordinary projectiles, two more a level, against full armor.*

> **Flying Shards** · Earth 1 • Evocation 1 · At-will · 1 AP
> *You stamp, and splinters of stone leap from the ground at your foes.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Evocation, Ranged, Strike]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Armor Defense
> **Tier 2:** [V] 1d6 Pi.
> **Tier 3:** [V] 1d6 Pi; and one more creature within 2 squares of the target takes [V] 1d6 Pi.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth.* *Earth:* a heavy stone among them: on a Tier 3, the target is also Prone.

Earth's cantrip against Fire's: the same job (the At-will Strike), another way. Fire Darts hits harder; the Shards spread on a Tier 3 with no Overcast and deal a weapon's damage type, which a Resist to Fire doesn't stop.

### 8. Sunder Armor
*Dominions: Armor of Achilles, Earth 1 / Alteration 2. It destroys armor.*

> **Sunder Armor** · Earth 2 • Alteration 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *You close your fist, and the metal your foe wears groans and splits along its seams.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Alteration, Ranged, Strike]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Armor Defense
> **Tier 2:** The target's Armor Defense is 1 lower until the encounter ends.
> **Tier 3:** The target's Armor Defense is 2 lower until the encounter ends.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Earth:* the shards fly inward: the target also takes [V] 1d8 Sl.

It hurts nobody: it makes every ally's weapon better against one enemy. The Stacking rule already says two castings don't add (the highest penalty counts).

### 9. Earthquake
*Dominions: Earth 4 / Evocation 5, the whole battlefield, both sides; those who fly or float are spared.*

> **Earthquake** · Earth 2 • Evocation 2 · Encounter · 3 AP
> *You drive both fists into the ground and it bucks like a beast.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Evocation, Area, Save, Self]
> Area: 3 Emanation · Target: All creatures in the Area but you
> **Save:** Instinct
> **Tier 1:** [V] 2d6 Bl; Prone.
> **Tier 2:** Prone.
> **Effect:** After the Save, the Area is Difficult Terrain for every creature until the encounter ends.
> **Overcast:** *Earth:* you ride it: the Area isn't Difficult Terrain for you, and you can Stand at no cost while in it.

Allies in it save too: the caster has to walk into the enemy to use it well. It costs 3 AP, which no class card does.

### 10. Stone Ward
*From Dominions' Earth protections; a Reaction, where Abjuration lives.*

> **Stone Ward** · Earth 1 • Abjuration 1 · Turn · 1R
> *You point, and the ground rises a hand's width to brace your ally's feet.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Abjuration]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: You or one ally
> **Interrupt:** The target would be Pushed, Pulled, Slid or knocked Prone.
> **Effect:** It isn't, and it gains Guard equal to your Power Modifier.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Earth:* the ground bites back: the creature that would have moved or felled the target is Hindered (EoT).

Against Air's Gust it is the elements answering each other again, here as a Reaction.

## Astral

### 11. Mind Burn
*Dominions: Astral 2 / Thaumaturgy 2. 12 damage, unlimited range, it can't miss; resisted; the mindless are immune.*

> **Mind Burn** · Astral 1 • Thaumaturgy 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You look at your foe, and think of fire, and it screams.*
> [Power, Spell, Astral, Thaumaturgy, Save]
> Distance: 10 sq. · Target: One creature you can see (no Line of Effect needed)
> **Save:** Mind
> **Tier 1:** [R] 2d6 Fo.
> **Tier 2:** [R] 1d6 Fo.
> **Tier 3:** [R] 1d4 Fo.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Astral:* the thought stays: on a Tier 1, the target also takes Astral's rider.

Two things no other card has: it **needs sight, not Line of Effect** (through a window, a portcullis, a wall of force), and it **always does something**, even on a Tier 3. Dominions' "sure hit" in 2d12's Tiers.

### 12. Circle
*Dominions: the Communion, Astral 1 / Thaumaturgy 1. Linked mages: the master's levels rise, the cost is shared, and the slaves suffer if the master falls.*

> **Circle** · Astral 1 • Enchantment 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You and your ally touch palms, and for as long as it lasts you think with two minds.*
> [Power, Spell, Astral, Enchantment, Woven]
> Distance: Reach · Target: One willing creature other than you
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, you and the target are **linked**:
> - When either of you casts a spell, they can use the other's level in its Path or in its School in place of their own, for its minimums and for Overcast.
> - When either of you Overreaches, the other can take the damage in their place.
> - A [Self] spell cast by either of you also affects the other.
> **Special:** This spell ends when either of you goes Down or becomes Bloodied or Strained. When it ends that way, the other is Dazed 1.
> **Overcast:** *Astral:* a wider circle: a second willing creature is linked.

Dominions' boldest mechanic, without anyone sitting out: both go on acting, and what is shared is knowledge (levels) and cost (Overreach). A fighter with no magic at all can still take a caster's Overreach for them. The backlash is Dominions' too. **It is long;** that is what "rules inside themselves" allows, and whether it is too long is for the table.

## Death

### 13. Hand of Death
*Dominions: Death 2 / Alteration 1. A touch: 40 damage, armor-negating, for 5 fatigue. The strongest cheap attack in the game, and it has to touch.*

> **Hand of Death** · Death 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *You lay your palm flat on your foe's chest, and something in there stops.*
> [Power, Spell, Death, Evocation, Melee, Strike]
> Distance: Reach · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Ward Defense
> **Tier 1:** [R] 1d6 Un.
> **Tier 2:** [R] 3d6 Un.
> **Tier 3:** [R] 4d6 Un.
> **Overcast:** *Death:* the cold spreads: on a Tier 3, the target also takes Death's rider.

The biggest dice of any Round spell here, and no Reach ladder: the price is standing next to the thing. [Melee, Spell], so being Engaged costs nothing.

### 14. Wither
*Dominions: Weakness, Death 1 / Alteration 2. Strength lost, so every blow lands softer.*

> **Wither** · Death 1 • Alteration 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You point, and the strength runs out of your foe's arm like sand.*
> [Power, Spell, Death, Alteration, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** For 2 rounds, each [Strike] Action the target uses deals 1d6 less damage to each creature (to a minimum of 0).
> **Tier 2:** The same, for 1 round.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth, Hold.* *Death:* it knows it is failing: on a Tier 1, the target is also Frightened of you (1 round).

No condition word at all: the effect is written out, and a die is rolled each time (tracking the class rule would have refused).

### 15. Darkness
*Dominions: Death 4 / Alteration 6. The battlefield goes dark, for everyone.*

> **Darkness** · Death 2 • Enchantment 1 · Encounter · 2 AP
> *You breathe out, and the light goes with your breath.*
> [Power, Spell, Death, Enchantment, Woven, Area]
> Distance: 10 sq. · Area: 3 Emanation
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, the Area is Darkness, and no light source lights a square of it.
> **Special:** You see in this Darkness as if it were Dim light.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Death:* your dead see too: your summons see in it as you do.

Darkness is Chapter 3's word (what is in it is Hidden or Unsensed; its squares are Difficult Terrain). **It needs the rule the backlog already asks for:** "Light never makes a square darker" has no opposite yet.

## Nature

### 16. Tangle Vines
*Dominions: Nature 1 / Conjuration 1. Entangled: Strength to break out, a little easier or harder by the land.*

> **Tangle Vines** · Nature 1 • Alteration 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *You scatter a handful of seed, and it is a thicket before it lands.*
> [Power, Spell, Nature, Alteration, Area, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Area: 1 Burst · Target: All creatures in the Area
> **Effect:** For 2 rounds, the Area is Difficult Terrain for every creature.
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** Immobilized. Its Break Free needs a Body Check against your Power DC.
> **Tier 2:** Hindered (1 round).
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Hold.* *Nature:* thorns among the vines: a creature that uses Break Free in the Area takes [V] 1d4 Pi.

Nature's hold against Earth's (Earth Grip): Earth grips one creature hard; Nature fills a patch of ground that slows everyone who comes after.

### 17. Venom
*Dominions: poison. "It acts slowly but surely": the damage is taken over the following rounds, a tenth of what remains each round.*

> **Venom** · Nature 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *A thorn the length of your finger grows from your palm, green at the tip.*
> [Power, Spell, Nature, Evocation, Ranged, Strike]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Armor Defense
> **Tier 2:** [V] 1d4 Pi; Lingering 1d8 Po (Bo/In).
> **Tier 3:** [V] 1d4 Pi; Lingering 2d6 Po (Bo/In).
> **Special:** Each time this Lingering deals its damage, it loses one size of die (2d6, 1d8, 1d6, 1d4), and it ends after the 1d4.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth.* *Nature:* it reaches the heart: Shake Off's Save against this Lingering takes 1 Bane.

Poison as its own thing, not Fire's Lingering with another name: almost nothing now, a lot over the next rounds, and it runs out by itself. The fading die is tracking; here it is the spell.

## Glamour

### 18. Phantom Blade
*Dominions: False Damage. It kills only together with real damage, leaves no lasting harm, and fades once no enemy Glamour mage is left.*

> **Phantom Blade** · Glamour 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *A sword that isn't there opens a wound that isn't there, and your foe bleeds belief.*
> [Power, Spell, Glamour, Evocation, Ranged, Strike]
> Distance: 10 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Ward Defense
> **Tier 1:** [R] 1d6 Ps.
> **Tier 2:** [R] 2d8 Ps.
> **Tier 3:** [R] 3d8 Ps.
> **Special:** This damage is **false**: it is Nonlethal, and all false damage you have dealt is removed when you go Down.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Glamour:* it believes the wound: on a Tier 3, the target also takes Glamour's rider.

Bigger dice than any real bolt at 1 AP, because the damage is a loan: drop the illusionist and their victims get it all back. Nonlethal is Chapter 4's word (a creature taken Down by it falls Unconscious, not dead). One new word, false.

### 19. Luck
*Dominions: Glamour 1 / Thaumaturgy 1. A blow that would kill misses three times in four.*

> **Luck** · Glamour 1 • Enchantment 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You whisper a small, true wish over your ally, and the world agrees to it once.*
> [Power, Spell, Glamour, Enchantment, Woven]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: You or one willing creature
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, the first time a [Strike] Action gets a Tier 3 or a Critical against the target, it is a Tier 2 instead. Then this spell ends.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Glamour:* luck turns: the creature whose Strike was lessened is Pressed 1 (1 round).

A ward that is a count, as Twist Fate is, and different in kind: Astral takes dice off the next blow, whatever it is; Glamour waits for the worst one.

## What the third pass showed

### What the freedom bought
1. **Graded results.** Freeze, Gust, Tangle Vines and Burning Hands each give a full effect on a Tier 1 and a lighter one on a Tier 2. In the second pass those Tier 2 lines were empty or repeated the Tier 1.
2. **Spells that are only their own rule:** Storm, Rain, Circle, Venom, Phantom Blade, Wither. None is "damage and a rider".
3. **Fields laid on both sides** (Storm, Rain, Darkness, Earthquake): the caster's allies pay too, so using one is a decision about where everyone stands.
4. **The elements answer each other on the cards themselves:** fire thaws Freeze; Rain strips Fire spells of Overcast and Storm puts out burning; Stone Ward stops Gust.
5. **One job, two Paths, two cards:** the At-will Strike (Fire Darts, Flying Shards); the hold (Earth Grip, Tangle Vines); the counted ward (Twist Fate, Luck); the buff with a price (Stoneskin, Battle Fury).

### What it cost or left open
| # | Finding | Seen on | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Length.** Circle is seven lines of rules; Storm and Venom four each | 4, 12, 17 | Lucas's eye: complex is allowed, "but it needs to be intuitive" |
| 2 | **"Every square within 10 squares of you when you cast"** is a new kind of Area (the book has Burst, Emanation, Line) | Storm, Rain | A 10 Emanation says the same and is a word the book has; or a new one for fields |
| 3 | **A spell that limits another spell** (no Overcast steps for [Fire] in the Rain) | Rain | Nothing, if accepted; it is a new kind of interaction |
| 4 | **Levels lent between two characters** | Circle | A check against decision 18 (a level is never a number on a roll): it isn't one here, but it is the first time a level leaves its owner |
| 5 | **Sight without Line of Effect** | Mind Burn | The backlog's entry on perception and Line of Effect allows the exception "(no Line of Effect needed)"; this is its first use on a damaging spell |
| 6 | **A Tier 3 that still hurts on a Save** | Mind Burn | A spell that can't be fully resisted: fine once, a problem if common |
| 7 | **A lowered Armor Defense until the encounter ends** | Sunder Armor | Pricing: it multiplies a whole party's damage against one enemy |
| 8 | **New words:** linked, false (damage) | 12, 18 | Their sentences |
| 9 | **Darkness as a zone** | Darkness | The rule the backlog asks for |
| 10 | **A 3 AP spell** | Earthquake | Nothing; noted as a first |
| 11 | **Every number is unpriced, and these are freer than the last 26** | all | A caster in the SIM matters more now |

### Assumptions I made (Lucas can revert any)
- "Nothing from the class" drops the class charter's design rules (one condition, the word budget, "never only bigger", no tracking, no silent bonus). It does **not** drop the book's own rules or the card Structure's labels.
- Durations stay EoT, rounds, or "until the encounter ends" (that rule is written in the charter but it is how the whole book counts time).
- A Tier 3 that beats the Tier 2 is kept where the spell rolls an Attack.
- Flavor lines are written as before.
