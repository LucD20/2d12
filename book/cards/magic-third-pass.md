# Magic, the third pass: nineteen new spells under magic's own charter

**2026-11-18, asked by Lucas:** "Class charter isn't the magic charter, they are different so they are balanced differently; none is supposed to be stronger than the other, what makes something strong is the situation it is used in and how it is used. The limits we have been putting should be the only limiters/charter for now, nothing from the class. With this in mind, read the research and give me the summary, plus new cards if you don't have questions."

**A test, not a spell list. Nothing here is decided, nothing is in the book. Every number is a placeholder** [not priced: the SIM has no caster].

**Audited.** An independent reader checked the first version against the record and the rulebook (42 findings); the main ones were verified in the rules and the cards corrected. This is the corrected version; what changed is the last table.

## What changed in how these are written

**Magic's charter is the list of limits in `book/magic-design.md` 5m and nothing else** (decision 47). So these cards drop what the first two passes took from the class charter:

| Dropped (a class rule) | So a spell may |
|---|---|
| One condition a card | Grade its result: a full effect on the worst Tier, a lighter one on the next |
| 35 words a Version; one main thing and one rider | Carry its own rules, as long as each is written |
| "Never only bigger"; no tracking across turns | Count things, fade, or grow a number when that is the spell |
| A Passive must be a visible moment; no silent bonus | Lay a standing effect on a field |

**Kept, because they are the book's own rules and not the class's:** Chapter 1 (Power Actions add no Modifier to damage; a Save's Tier 1 is the worst for the target; Several Targets), the card Structure's labels, Line of Effect, durations in EoT or rounds, and every decision of the magic record: the minimums, the riders, Overcast's ladders and small rungs, [Woven] and [Sustained], Overreach, Guard instead of Health, no whole turn taken from a major enemy or a PC, a [Spell] is Doomed while Engaged unless it is [Melee].

**Read with** the header of `magic-second-pass.md` (the bar, Overcast, the riders in full, Charmed and Confused). Merge and Overreach are on every spell and not repeated. [Focus] is on every spell and left off the trait lines.

**These nineteen were chosen from the research for what the first 26 lacked:** a second spell in each opening cell; each Path doing a job its own way; the things Dominions' magic is known for and no card had yet tried (weather laid on both sides, the elements answering each other, false damage, poison that works slowly, linked casters, a touch spell cast in melee).

## Fire

### 1. Burning Hands
*Dominions: Fire 1 / Evocation 1. "A melee-ranged square roast."*

> **Burning Hands** · Fire 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You spread your fingers and a sheet of flame washes over whoever stands before you.*
> [Power, Spell, Fire, Evocation, Melee, Save]
> Distance: Reach · Target: Up to two creatures within your Reach that are adjacent to each other
> **Save:** Instinct
> **Tier 1:** [V] 2d6 Fi; Lingering 1d4 Fi (Bo/In).
> **Tier 2:** [V] 1d6 Fi.
> **Overcast:** *Breadth.* *Fire:* the flames find cloth: a target on a Tier 2 also takes the Lingering.

The second spell of the Fire 1 • Evocation 1 cell (a level 1 caster's two spells). **[Melee, Spell]:** cast while Engaged, it pays nothing (decision 46), which is the reason to stand in front with it. **It leans on a rule not yet written:** Chapter 7's [Melee] says "these Actions need a melee weapon used in hand"; a spell needs its own sentence there.

### 2. Battle Fury
*Dominions: Fire 2 / Thaumaturgy 2. +2 attack, +2 morale. Fire's side of the mind is rage.*

> **Battle Fury** · Fire 1 • Thaumaturgy 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You lay a hot hand on your ally's shoulder, and the fear burns out of them.*
> [Power, Spell, Fire, Thaumaturgy]
> Distance: Reach · Target: One willing creature other than you
> **Effect:** For 2 rounds, the target is Empowered, ignores the Frightened condition, and is Staggered.
> **Overcast:** *Breadth, Hold.* *Fire:* the rage wants blood: the first time on each of its turns that one of its [Melee, Strike] Actions gets a Tier 3, it can Advance 2.

A buff with a price, as Stoneskin is: Staggered is Chapter 8's word for "can't use Reactions". **Noted by the audit:** the price is nothing on a creature that has no Reaction, such as a summon.

## Air

### 3. Gust
*Dominions: Gust of Winds, Air 1 / Evocation 1. It stuns the small and the weak.*

> **Gust** · Air 1 • Alteration 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You throw your arm wide and a wall of wind goes with it.*
> [Power, Spell, Air, Alteration, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** Pushed 3; Prone.
> **Tier 2:** Pushed 1.
> **Special:** On a Tier 1 or 2, a Minor enemy is Pushed 2 more.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth.* *Air:* the wind keeps blowing: until the end of the target's next turn, every square that brings it closer to you is Difficult Terrain for it.

No damage at all: Air's rider is the whole spell (decision 24). Forced Movement, Steadiness and Collision already say how far it really goes and what happens when something is in the way. **Changed after the audit:** it is an Alteration (a spell with no damage isn't "the Path released to harm"), and its own step no longer knocks a bystander Prone with no Save.

### 4. Storm
*Dominions: Air 4 / Alteration 5, a battlefield enchantment on both sides. Precision halved, half of all missiles lost, nobody flies, burning put out; and the Air mage's own spells grow in it.*

> **Storm** · Air 2 • Enchantment 2 · Round · 2 AP
> *You raise both hands and the sky answers: wind, rain, and the dark under the cloud.*
> [Power, Spell, Air, Enchantment, Woven, Area, Self]
> Area: 10 Emanation (it stays where you cast it)
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, for every creature: a [Ranged, Strike] Action whose origin or target is in the Area takes 2 Banes; and a Lingering of Fire damage on a creature ends when that creature starts its turn in the Area.
> **Special:** While you are in the Area, your [Air] spells count your Escalation as 1 higher, to at most 6.
> **Overcast:** *Air:* you are the eye of it: the Banes don't apply to your own Actions.

The first card that touches **everyone**, allies included: your own Archer pays for it, and so do you until you take its own step. Its Special is Dominions' Storm Power in 2d12's terms. **Changed after the audit:** a 10 Emanation with [Self] (a shape the book has); plain Banes with no name (Pressure is for what another creature puts on you, and Field shares its limit of 2 with Cover); Round, not Encounter, so it can be cast again after it breaks.

## Water

### 5. Freeze
*Dominions: Water 2 / Alteration 3. Slowed, and tiring every round until it thaws; heat thaws it.*

> **Freeze** · Water 1 • Alteration 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *Frost races up your foe's limbs and stiffens them.*
> [Power, Spell, Water, Alteration, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** Slowed 1 (2 rounds) and Hindered (2 rounds).
> **Tier 2:** Hindered (1 round).
> **Special:** These end at once if the target takes Fire damage.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth, Hold.* *Water:* the ice bites: at the end of each of its turns while it is Slowed by this spell, it takes [V] 1d4 Co.

The graded result the class rule forbade: the full effect on a Tier 1, Water's rider alone on a Tier 2. Its Special is the elements answering each other, written on the card: an ally's fire undoes your ice.

### 6. Rain
*Dominions: Water 3 / Evocation 3, a battlefield enchantment. It puts out the burning and weakens Fire spells.*

> **Rain** · Water 1 • Enchantment 2 · Round · 2 AP
> *You open your hand to the sky, and it lets go.*
> [Power, Spell, Water, Enchantment, Woven, Area, Self]
> Area: 10 Emanation (it stays where you cast it)
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, every creature in the Area has Resist 2 Fire, and a Lingering of Fire damage on a creature ends when that creature starts its turn in the Area.
> **Overcast:** *Water:* soaked through: every creature in the Area also has Weak 2 Cold and Weak 2 Electricity.

**A spell that asks more of the School than of the Path** (Water 1 • Enchantment 2): a technique. **Rewritten after the audit:** the first version stripped Overcast steps from Fire spells, which did nothing to NPCs (they have no Escalation) and so limited only the caster's own allies. Resist and Weak are Chapter 1's words and work on everyone. Its own step sets up Water's and Air's spells for the whole table, friend and foe.

## Earth

### 7. Flying Shards
*Dominions: Earth 1, no research. "A blunderbuss of small rocks": four ordinary projectiles, two more a level, against full armor.*

> **Flying Shards** · Earth 1 • Evocation 1 · At-will · 1 AP
> *You stamp, and splinters of stone leap from the ground at your foes.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Evocation, Ranged, Strike]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Armor Defense
> **Tier 2:** [V] 1d6 Pi.
> **Tier 3:** [V] 1d6 Pi; and one creature you choose within 2 squares of the target, not a target of this casting, takes [V] 1d6 Pi.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth.* *Earth:* a heavy stone among them: on a Tier 3, the target is also Prone.

Earth's cantrip against Fire's: the same job (the At-will Strike), another way. Fire Darts hits harder; the Shards spread on a Tier 3 with no Overcast and deal a weapon's damage type, which a Resist to Fire doesn't stop.

### 8. Sunder Armor
*Dominions: Armor of Achilles, Earth 1 / Alteration 2. It destroys armor.*

> **Sunder Armor** · Earth 2 • Alteration 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *You close your fist, and the metal your foe wears groans and splits along its seams.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Alteration, Ranged, Strike]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Armor Defense
> **Tier 2:** The target's Armor Defense is 1 lower (2 rounds).
> **Tier 3:** The target's Armor Defense is 2 lower (2 rounds).
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Hold.* *Earth:* the shards fly inward: on a Tier 3, the target also takes [V] 1d8 Sl.

It hurts nobody: it makes every ally's weapon better against one enemy. **Changed after the audit:** 2 rounds, not the whole encounter (a magic that lasts the fight must be an Enchantment, decision 10). **Still to settle:** a lowered Defense and Vulnerable are two names for nearly the same thing, and by the Stacking rule they add.

### 9. Earthquake
*Dominions: Earth 4 / Evocation 5, the whole battlefield, both sides; those who fly or float are spared.*

> **Earthquake** · Earth 2 • Evocation 2 · Encounter · 3 AP
> *You drive both fists into the ground and it bucks like a beast.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Evocation, Area, Save, Self]
> Area: 3 Emanation · Target: All creatures in the Area but you
> **Save:** Instinct
> **Tier 1:** [V] 2d6 Bl; Prone.
> **Tier 2:** Prone.
> **Effect:** After the Save, the Area is Difficult Terrain for every creature (3 rounds).
> **Overcast:** *Earth:* you choose where it breaks: up to two creatures you choose in the Area aren't targets.

Allies in it save too: the caster has to walk into the enemy to use it well, or spend its own step to spare two friends. It costs 3 AP, which no class card does.

### 10. Stone Ward
*From Dominions' Earth protections; a Reaction, where Abjuration lives.*

> **Stone Ward** · Earth 1 • Abjuration 1 · Turn · 1R
> *You point, and the ground rises a hand's width to brace your ally's feet.*
> [Power, Spell, Earth, Abjuration]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: You or one ally
> **Interrupt:** An Action's result would Push, Pull or Slide the target, or make it Prone.
> **Effect:** The target isn't moved or made Prone by that Action, and gains Guard equal to your Power Modifier.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Earth:* the ground bites back: if that Action was an enemy's, that enemy is Hindered (EoT).

Against Air's Gust it is the elements answering each other again, here as a Reaction. **A point of timing for the rules:** Chapter 2 places an Interrupt before the Action, with one exception ("would damage", after the roll). This needs the same exception: it answers a result, after the roll or the Save.

## Astral

### 11. Mind Burn
*Dominions: Astral 2 / Thaumaturgy 2. 12 damage, unlimited range, it can't miss; resisted; the mindless are immune.*

> **Mind Burn** · Astral 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You reach for your foe's mind, and think of fire, and it screams.*
> [Power, Spell, Astral, Evocation, Save]
> Distance: 10 sq. · Target: One creature you perceive (no Line of Effect needed)
> **Save:** Mind
> **Tier 1:** [R] 2d6 Ps.
> **Tier 2:** [R] 1d6 Ps.
> **Tier 3:** [R] 1d4 Ps.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Astral:* the thought stays: on a Tier 1, the target also takes Astral's rider.

**Psychic, not Force (Lucas, 2026-11-18: "wouldn't psychic damage fit best in such a spell?"):** a Path's subtype is its usual one, not its only one; the subtype follows what the spell does. Two things no other card has: it **needs no Line of Effect** (a creature you hear behind a wall is Hidden to you, and that is enough), and it **always does something**, even on a Tier 3. **Corrected after the audit:** the first version said "a creature you can see", which can never be true without Line of Effect (Chapter 3: "You can't see [...] creatures you have no Line of Effect to"); and it is an Evocation, since all it does is damage.

### 12. Circle
*Dominions: the Communion, Astral 1 / Thaumaturgy 1. Linked mages: the master's levels rise, the cost is shared, and the slaves suffer if the master falls.*

> **Circle** · Astral 1 • Enchantment 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You and your ally touch palms, and for as long as it lasts you think with two minds.*
> [Power, Spell, Astral, Enchantment, Woven]
> Distance: Reach · Target: One willing creature other than you (not a summon)
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, you and the target are **joined**:
> - When either of you casts a spell, they count the other's level in its Path or in its School as their own, if it is higher, for Overcast.
> - When either of you Overreaches, the other can take the damage in their place.
> **Special:** This spell ends when either of you goes Down or becomes Bloodied or Strained. When it ends that way, the other is Dazed 1.
> **Overcast:** *Breadth.* *Astral:* shared senses: each of you perceives every creature the other perceives, as the other does.

Dominions' boldest mechanic, without anyone sitting out: both go on acting, and what is shared is mastery (spare levels) and cost (Overreach). A fighter with no magic can still take a caster's Overreach for them. The backlash is Dominions' too. **Cut down after the audit:** lending levels for a spell's minimums could never matter (you only hold spells you can cast); the line sharing [Self] spells is gone (most are [Woven], and casting one would end the Circle); a summon can't be joined (it made Overreach free); "linked" became "joined" (the book has Linked Actions); a second partner is a Breadth step, not Astral's. **It does lift one of magic's limits on purpose** (a spare level for each step): that is what the spell is.

## Death

### 13. Hand of Death
*Dominions: Death 2 / Alteration 1. A touch: 40 damage, armor-negating, for 5 fatigue. The strongest cheap attack in the game, and it has to touch.*

> **Hand of Death** · Death 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *You lay your palm flat on your foe's chest, and something in there stops.*
> [Power, Spell, Death, Evocation, Melee, Strike]
> Distance: Reach · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Ward Defense
> **Tier 1:** [R] 1d6 Un.
> **Tier 2:** [R] 3d6 Un.
> **Tier 3:** [R] 4d6 Un.
> **Overcast:** *Death:* the cold spreads: on a Tier 3, the target also takes Death's rider.

The biggest real dice of any Round spell here, and no Reach ladder: the price is standing next to the thing. [Melee, Spell], so being Engaged costs nothing (with the same unwritten sentence as Burning Hands).

### 14. Wither
*Dominions: Weakness, Death 1 / Alteration 2. Strength lost, so every blow lands softer.*

> **Wither** · Death 1 • Alteration 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You point, and the strength runs out of your foe's arm like sand.*
> [Power, Spell, Death, Alteration, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** For 2 rounds, each [Strike] Action the target uses deals 1d6 less damage to each creature (to a minimum of 0).
> **Tier 2:** The same, for 1 round.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth, Hold.* *Death:* it knows it is failing: on a Tier 1, the target is also Frightened of you (1 round).

No condition word at all: the effect is written out, and a die is rolled each time (tracking the class rule would have refused).

### 15. Darkness
*Dominions: Death 4 / Alteration 6. The battlefield goes dark, for everyone.*

> **Darkness** · Death 2 • Enchantment 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *You breathe out, and the light goes with your breath.*
> [Power, Spell, Death, Enchantment, Woven, Area]
> Distance: 10 sq. · Area: 3 Emanation
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, the Area is Darkness, no light source lights a square of it, and a creature in the Area perceives every creature outside it as Obscured at best.
> **Special:** You have Dark Sight in the Area.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Death:* your dead see too: your summons have Dark Sight in the Area.

**The audit showed the first version worked backwards.** In 2d12 light matters where the *target* is: a creature in Darkness is Hidden to those who can't see in the dark, so Strikes against it are Doomed, while it strikes out normally. Dark laid on your enemies shields them; laid on your own side it is a refuge. So this is written as what the rules make it: **a place to hide in**, which also dims what its occupants see outside. Dark Sight is Chapter 3's sense (it also removes the Difficult Terrain of Darkness). Two things to know: standing in it stops enemies being Frightened of you (they can't see you), and it still needs the backlog's rule for darkness against light.

## Nature

### 16. Tangle Vines
*Dominions: Nature 1 / Conjuration 1. Entangled: Strength to break out, a little easier or harder by the land.*

> **Tangle Vines** · Nature 1 • Alteration 1 · Round · 2 AP
> *You scatter a handful of seed, and it is a thicket before it lands.*
> [Power, Spell, Nature, Alteration, Area, Save]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Area: 1 Burst · Target: All creatures in the Area
> **Effect:** For 2 rounds, the Area is Difficult Terrain for every creature.
> **Save:** Body
> **Tier 1:** Immobilized (2 rounds). Its Break Free needs a Body or Instinct Check (the higher) against the Immobilized DC.
> **Tier 2:** Hindered (1 round).
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Hold (the Area and the Immobilized).* *Nature:* thorns among the vines: a creature that uses Break Free in the Area takes [V] 1d4 Pi.

Nature's hold against Earth's (Earth Grip): Earth grips one creature hard; Nature fills a patch of ground that slows everyone who comes after. The Break Free line is now the book's own form (Chapter 8, Grabbed and Immobilized).

### 17. Venom
*Dominions: poison. "It acts slowly but surely": the damage is taken over the following rounds, a tenth of what remains each round.*

> **Venom** · Nature 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *A thorn the length of your finger grows from your palm, green at the tip.*
> [Power, Spell, Nature, Evocation, Ranged, Strike]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Armor Defense
> **Tier 2:** [V] 1d4 Pi; Lingering 1d8 Po (Bo/In).
> **Tier 3:** [V] 1d4 Pi; Lingering 2d6 Po (Bo/In).
> **Special:** Each time this Lingering deals its damage, it loses one size of die (2d6, 1d8, 1d6, 1d4), and it ends after the 1d4. It is compared with another Lingering by the die it has now.
> **Overcast:** *Reach, Breadth.* *Nature:* it reaches the heart: Shake Off's Save against this Lingering takes 1 Bane.

Poison as its own thing, not Fire's Lingering with another name: almost nothing now, a lot over the next rounds, and it runs out by itself. **A cost the audit found:** a creature keeps only its strongest Lingering (Chapter 8), so Venom can push out an ally's small fire, or be blocked by a bigger one.

## Glamour

### 18. Phantom Blade
*Dominions: False Damage. It kills only together with real damage, leaves no lasting harm, and fades once no enemy Glamour mage is left.*

> **Phantom Blade** · Glamour 1 • Evocation 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *A sword that isn't there opens a wound that isn't there, and your foe bleeds belief.*
> [Power, Spell, Glamour, Evocation, Ranged, Strike, Nonlethal]
> Distance: 10 sq. · Target: One creature
> **Attack:** Power vs. Ward Defense
> **Tier 1:** [R] 1d6 Ps.
> **Tier 2:** [R] 2d8 Ps.
> **Tier 3:** [R] 2d10 Ps.
> **Special:** This damage is **false**, dice added to it included: all false damage you have dealt is removed when you go Down or become Bloodied or Strained. It can't be made Lethal.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Glamour:* it believes the wound: on a Tier 3, the target also takes Glamour's rider.

Bigger dice than a real bolt at 1 AP, because the damage is a loan. **Changed after the audit:** the loan now falls due when the illusionist is hurt, not only when they go Down (a caster at 10 squares rarely goes Down, so the first version was simply the best bolt). It follows the same break as held magic. **Corrected:** false damage is Nonlethal, but that doesn't spare a creature by itself: an NPC that goes Down dies if it has more Lethal damage than Nonlethal (Chapter 4), so false damage kills together with the party's real blows, as in Dominions.

### 19. Luck
*Dominions: Glamour 1 / Thaumaturgy 1. A blow that would kill misses three times in four.*

> **Luck** · Glamour 1 • Enchantment 1 · Round · 1 AP
> *You whisper a small, true wish over your ally, and the world agrees to it once.*
> [Power, Spell, Glamour, Enchantment, Woven]
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: You or one willing creature
> **Effect:** While this spell is woven, the first time a [Strike] Action gets a Tier 3 or a Critical against the target, it is a Tier 2 instead, for everything. Then this spell ends.
> **Overcast:** *Reach.* *Glamour:* luck holds: it ends after the second such Strike.

A ward that is a count, as Twist Fate is, and different in kind: Astral takes dice off the next blow, whatever it is; Glamour waits for the worst one.

## What the third pass showed

### What the freedom bought
1. **Graded results.** Freeze, Gust, Tangle Vines and Burning Hands each give a full effect on a Tier 1 and a lighter one on a Tier 2. In the second pass those Tier 2 lines were empty or repeated the Tier 1.
2. **Spells that are their own rule:** Storm, Rain, Circle, Wither, Darkness, and the Special lines of Venom and Phantom Blade.
3. **Fields laid on both sides** (Storm, Rain, Earthquake): the caster's allies pay too, and the caster is spared only by spending the spell's own step.
4. **The elements answer each other on the cards themselves:** fire thaws Freeze; Rain and Storm put out burning and Rain blunts fire; Rain's step opens everyone to cold and lightning; Stone Ward stops Gust.
5. **One job, two Paths, two cards:** the At-will Strike (Fire Darts, Flying Shards); the hold (Earth Grip, Tangle Vines); the counted ward (Twist Fate, Luck); the buff with a price (Stoneskin, Battle Fury).

### Assumptions I made (Lucas can revert any)
- "Nothing from the class" drops the class charter's design rules (one condition, the word budget, "never only bigger", no tracking, no silent bonus). It does **not** drop the book's own rules or the card Structure's labels.
- Durations stay EoT, rounds, or "while this spell is woven" (that rule is written in the charter but it is how the whole book counts time).
- A Tier 3 that beats the Tier 2 is kept where the spell rolls an Attack.
- Flavor lines are written as before.
- Storm and Darkness became Round spells (they were Encounter): a held spell is limited by being held and by its break, and an Encounter one could not be cast again after breaking.
- Mind Burn and Gust changed School (to Evocation and to Alteration) by the sorting rule as it stands.
