# Magic: first Overreach lines (six drafts)

> **Status (2026-11-20): PLACEHOLDER spell cards (Lucas, 2026-11-19: reference only, "they aren't set in any shape or form"). Never argue from one as settled.**

**2026-11-18.** Decisions 57 and 58 of `book/magic-design.md`: Overreach is a line only some spells print; the line gives what it buys and the whole price. Shared by every line: once a casting, chosen when you cast and before any roll; paid even if the spell misses or is resisted; the usual price is Nonlethal Resolve damage that Guard doesn't take and nothing reduces.

**A test. Nothing is decided, nothing is in the book. Every number is a placeholder** [not priced: the SIM has no caster]. The six spells are from `magic-third-pass.md`; only the new line is shown.

## What the six show

- **Three of the six buy a way past a limiter** (a Frequency, the one-[Woven] limit, a spell's own drawback). That is the strongest use of the line and the one to decide on purpose: 5m's limits are magic's only charter.
- **Circle** (third pass) lets a joined ally take "the damage" of an Overreach: it does nothing for a price that isn't damage (Mind Burn).
- With decision 55 (decided, not in the book yet), each line's damage stays until a Sleep, a Rally Point or a heal.
- **A price that isn't damage is weaker than it looks:** it costs nothing outside a fight or in a fight's last round, and Circle can't share it. Damage has neither gap.

## The audit

An independent reader checked the first version against the rulebook and the record. What changed:

| Spell | Finding | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Darkness | "Isn't [Woven]" switched the spell off (its Effect reads "while this spell is woven"), dropped the Bloodied and Strained break (decision 28), and could be cast again every round | It stays [Woven] in everything but the count, and only one such casting can be held |
| Mind Burn | "Dazed 1 at the start of your next turn" is Slowed's rule under Dazed's name (Chapter 8), and was never paid if the fight ended first | Staggered for 1 round, paid at once |
| Earthquake | Each later casting could be Overreached again: the Frequency stopped limiting | "A second time": once an encounter |
| Burning Hands | "Every creature within your Reach" was an area without the [Area] trait (decision 43: one creature to an area is a different spell), unclear for Unsensed creatures, and made Breadth useless | Up to four chosen targets |
| Hand of Death | The order against Doubles was unstated (Chapter 1), and it lacked Luck's "for everything" | Both written |
| Phantom Blade | The card's "it can't be made Lethal" lost its referent once the damage isn't false | Restated in the line |
