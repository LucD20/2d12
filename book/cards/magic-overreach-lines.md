# Magic: first Overreach lines (six drafts)

**2026-11-18.** Decisions 57 and 58 of `book/magic-design.md`: Overreach is a line only some spells print; the line gives what it buys and the whole price. Shared by every line: once a casting, chosen when you cast and before any roll; paid even if the spell misses or is resisted; the usual price is Nonlethal Resolve damage that Guard doesn't take and nothing reduces.

**A test. Nothing is decided, nothing is in the book. Every number is a placeholder** [not priced: the SIM has no caster]. The six spells are from `magic-third-pass.md`; only the new line is shown.

| Spell | The line | What it tries |
|---|---|---|
| **Burning Hands** (Fire 1 • Evocation 1) | **Overreach (1d6!):** The targets are every creature within your Reach but you, allies included. | A bigger form that can't be aimed: the price is partly your friends |
| **Earthquake** (Earth 2 • Evocation 2, Encounter) | **Overreach (3d6!):** You can cast this spell though you have already used it this encounter. | Overreach against a Frequency |
| **Hand of Death** (Death 1 • Evocation 1) | **Overreach (2d6!):** A Tier 1 of this casting is a Tier 2 instead. | Buying certainty for a spell that risks standing in melee |
| **Darkness** (Death 2 • Enchantment 1, Woven) | **Overreach (1d6!):** This casting isn't [Woven]: it lasts 3 rounds and ends early only if you go Down. | Overreach against the one-[Woven] limit |
| **Mind Burn** (Astral 1 • Evocation 1) | **Overreach (you are Dazed 1 at the start of your next turn):** The target's Save takes 1 Bane. | A price that isn't damage |
| **Phantom Blade** (Glamour 1 • Evocation 1) | **Overreach (2d6!):** This casting's damage isn't false. | The spell's own drawback bought off |

## What the six show

- **Three of the six buy a way past a limiter** (a Frequency, the one-[Woven] limit, a spell's own drawback). That is the strongest use of the line and the one to decide on purpose: 5m's limits are magic's only charter.
- **Circle** (third pass) lets a joined ally take "the damage" of an Overreach: it does nothing for a price that isn't damage (Mind Burn).
- With decision 55, each line's damage stays until a Sleep, a Rally Point or a heal.
