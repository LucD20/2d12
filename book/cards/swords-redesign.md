## What the swords inherit (decided with the bows, in the book since 2026-11-16)

- **Mastery:** a Mastery for each weapon; +1 with one weapon for a won encounter that was normal or harder; never lower than half your best in the group; every weapon's unlocks at 10 (its Technique), 20, 30, 45, 60; Strikes with an Advanced weapon Doomed below 10 with it, an Exotic one below 20.
- **A row** is a flat number plus one exploding die; melee is +2 over ranged in one hand and +4 in two (2026-11-12). Today's placeholders: Longsword 0 / 2 + 1d6! / 4 + 1d6!, Greatsword (Two-handed) 0 / 5 + 1d6! / 8 + 1d8!.
- **Hands:** the off-hand Banes (2; a Light weapon 1); Two-handed (3 Banes on its Strikes in one hand). **Parked for this group** (decision 14 of the bows): what pays for fighting with two weapons, and what removes the off-hand Banes.
- **A Technique** is a Round card in the Structure, one roll, the weapon's own thing seen on most uses, a choice attached to using it; it writes 1[W]; a Tier 3 adds something; no second Version that is only bigger; no copy of a class card or its name.
- **The class it meets first is the Paladin** (Weapon +1, Power +2; Heavy armor): Judge, Smite, Binding Light (Immobilized), Lay On Hands, Shield of Faith (Taunted), Rebuke (Hindered, a Move disrupted), the Arts (Lantern of Faith, Hold the Line, Commandment), Armor of Faith and Roving Judgment, Bulwark of Faith, Consecrated Ground, Word of Radiance (Pushed), Weight of Judgment, Tireless Vigil, Reckoning (Pushed, Frightened), Hallowed Stance. And the Archer's Darting Blade and Hit and Run need a Light weapon, which no sword of this group is.
- **Today's placeholder Techniques** (not design, his word): Guarded Cut (Longsword: a Tier 3 gives +1 Armor Defense for a round, a silent bonus) and Reaping Sweep (Greatsword: Cleave 1 / 2).
