# The new Basic Actions: the record (the core rework, batch 3; started 2026-11-22)

Every creature's Basic Actions, reworked one at a time with Lucas (`book/core-rework.md` B5 to B9). Nothing is in the book until he says so.

## Decided
- **Haul is Body only** (Lucas, 2026-11-22; it was Appeal or Body).
- **The order** (Lucas, 2026-11-22: "start with demoralize"): Demoralize, Aid, Feint, Tumble, Heal (its playtest version is decided), Trip and Shove; a Mind-damage Basic attack dropped (each class's Power attack deals Resolve damage).

## Demoralize: decided (Lucas, 2026-11-22: "Its good")

> **Demoralize** · Round · 1 AP · [Appeal]
> *You fix them with a look and a promise, and their guard slips.*
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature that can see or hear you
> Attack: Appeal vs. Appeal DC
> Tier 1: No effect. · Tier 2: Vulnerable 1 (1 round). · Tier 3: Vulnerable 1 (1 round), and Daunted of you (EoT).

Lucas's idea (Vulnerable 1 on Tier 2, Daunted added on Tier 3); Claude's change he accepted: Vulnerable for 1 round, not EoT (the target's EoT often comes right after your turn: only your own attacks would use it; for 1 round every ally's do). About 5 points a use against a Basic Attack's 6.6 [judgment, the Bestiary's rider prices]. Not [Strike]. PF2e's Demoralize was the model (Frightened 1: worse checks and DCs).

## Aid: decided (Lucas, 2026-11-22: "Good")

> **Aid** · Round · 1 AP · [Appeal, Focus, Instinct]
> *You call the opening, and a friend takes it.*
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One ally that can hear you
> Effect: The target is Quickened 1 on its next turn.
> Special: If the target is Daunted, you also make this Check. · Check: Appeal or Instinct vs. Action DC · Tier 2 or 3: The target is no longer Daunted.

Shape (A), handing an AP over (Claude's pick: Boons are too small for 1 AP, and rolling to help has a poor record, `research/helping.md`); Lucas added the Focus and Instinct traits and the Check against Daunted's Action DC (Chapter 1, Action DC), made only when the ally is Daunted.

## Feint: decided (Lucas, 2026-11-22: "A but Tier 3 I think it should be a critical. Leave t2 and t3 equal"; "reach is fine")

> **Feint** · Round · 1 AP · [Focus, Mind]
> *You show one blow and land another.*
> Distance: Reach · Target: One creature
> Attack: Mind vs. Mind DC
> Tier 1: No effect. · Tier 2 or 3: Your next [Strike] Action against the target this turn is Favored.
> Critical: The target can't use Reactions against you until the end of your turn.

What a setup is worth on the next Strike (+2 vs 13) [calc]: Basic Attack with a bow 6.6 plain, 9.1 Favored; with a Longsword 11.2 plain, 15.7 Favored. Asked by Lucas ("curiosity"): raising the next Strike's Tier by one would be 9.7 (bow) and 17.4 (Longsword): a little more than Favored, because a Tier 1 becomes a hit every time.
