# The new Basic Actions: the record (the core rework, batch 3; started 2026-11-22)

Every creature's Basic Actions, reworked one at a time with Lucas (`book/core-rework.md` B5 to B9). Nothing is in the book until he says so.

## Decided
- **Haul is Body only** (Lucas, 2026-11-22; it was Appeal or Body).
- **The order** (Lucas, 2026-11-22: "start with demoralize"): Demoralize, Aid, Feint, Tumble, Heal (its playtest version is decided), Trip and Shove; a Mind-damage Basic attack dropped (each class's Power attack deals Resolve damage).

## Demoralize: decided (Lucas, 2026-11-22: "Its good")

> **Demoralize** · Round · 1 AP · [Appeal]
> *You fix them with a look and a promise, and their guard slips.*
> Distance: 5 sq. · Target: One creature that can see or hear you
> Attack: Appeal vs. Appeal DC
> Tier 1: No effect. · Tier 2: Vulnerable 1 (1 round). · Tier 3: Vulnerable 1 (1 round), and Daunted of you (EoT).

Lucas's idea (Vulnerable 1 on Tier 2, Daunted added on Tier 3); Claude's change he accepted: Vulnerable for 1 round, not EoT (the target's EoT often comes right after your turn: only your own attacks would use it; for 1 round every ally's do). About 5 points a use against a Basic Attack's 6.6 [judgment, the Bestiary's rider prices]. Not [Strike]. PF2e's Demoralize was the model (Frightened 1: worse checks and DCs).
