# Archer, level 1 (book/rules-src/13-archer.md). A Human Archer: Body 1, Mind 1, Instinct 2, Medium armor, a Crossbow.
# Level 1: Double Shot, Precise Aim, Marked Quarry, Bow Mastery (Lock & Loose, the Crossbow's technique); the Encounter Pick: Marksman's Shot;
# Escalation: Killer's Focus (passive) and Twin Shot (active).
class Archer
role ranged damage
level 1
attributes body 1, mind 1, instinct 2, appeal 0
health body x3 +3, mind x2.5 +4
armor 2
weapon-mod 2
power-mod 2
rally 5
targeted 0.75         # at range, behind the tank: chosen less often

weapon Crossbow (ranged, projectile, reload, two-handed, range 10/20/40): 0 / 1d4+2 / 1d10+2
speed 6               # Human 5, Archer +1 (the map model)

action Steel Yourself 2 | ap 2 | round | traits self | heal 1 self     # Basic Action: you use 1 Rally Point
action Weapon Attack | ap 1 | at-will | traits strike weapon | vs ad | tiers 0 / 1[W] + [Mod] / 1[W] + [Mod]
action Reload | ap 1 | at-will | traits weapon handle | vs dc 13 | on 1: boon 2 next @Reload; on 2+: reload
action Double Shot 2 | ap 2 | round | traits shot strike | does Weapon Attack x2 | different
action Double Shot 3 | ap 3 | round | traits shot strike | does Weapon Attack x2 | each boon 1 | different
action Precise Aim 1 | ap 1 | round | traits focus self | always: boon 1 next ranged shot strike weapon
action Precise Aim 2 | ap 2 | round | traits focus self | always: boon 2 next ranged shot strike weapon
action Lock & Loose | ap 1 | round | traits ranged shot strike weapon | vs ad | tiers [Mod] / 1d4 + 2 + [Mod] / 1d10 + [Mod] | on 2: debuff Hindered (until it moves); on 3: debuff Immobilized
action Marksman's Shot | ap 2 | encounter | traits ranged shot strike weapon | vs ad | tiers 1[W] / 1[W] + [Mod] / 1[W] + [Mod] | on 2+: debuff Bane on its next Save; on 3: vulnerable 2
action Twin Shot | ap 1 | round | traits ranged shot strike weapon | escalation 2 | does Weapon Attack x2 | each extra 1d6 | different     # one roll against two creatures in the book; here two rolls

passive Marked Quarry: boon 1 shot strike quarry
passive Killer's Focus: escalation 2: extra 1d6 first shot strike weapon

turn Double Shot + Lock & Loose + attack: Double Shot 3, Lock & Loose, Weapon Attack
turn Aimed Marksman: Precise Aim 1, Marksman's Shot, Double Shot 2
turn Twin: Double Shot 3, Twin Shot, Lock & Loose
turn Reload Shot: Double Shot 3, Weapon Attack, Lock & Loose (reload shot)
turn Reload and shoot: Reload, Double Shot 3, Lock & Loose (reload shot)

turn Steel: Steel Yourself 2, Double Shot 3

plan Aimed Marksman, Double Shot + Lock & Loose + attack, Twin
turn Kite: advance, Double Shot 3, Lock & Loose
when self below 35%: Steel
when engaged: Kite     # the map model: it starts its turn next to an enemy, so it steps away first (Advance: 1 AP, Speed 6), then shoots with no Engaged Banes
