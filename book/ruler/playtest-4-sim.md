Until 2026-11-12 the engine marked an enemy out only at the end of the turn that brought it to 0 Health, so the rest of that turn went on hitting it, and "damage dealt" counted it: SIM read 46% above the table. With the enemy out at once (`RULES.outAtOnce`) and the damage that counted beside the damage rolled, the two can be compared. What is left is a decision, not a rule: the ruler's builds take the larger Versions and their Escalation Passives every turn (Rolling Shot at 2 AP with its extra die where Kaela used the 1 AP one), and the table's PCs moved almost every turn and used Actions that deal no damage (Covering Fire, Lay On Hands, Beacon, a Shove, Leaning). 34 rolls are few: one roll's damage varies by about 6, so the table's average is known to about 1 either way.
