# Schools of magic: how games sort spells, and what makes a sorting hold

**Date:** 2026-11-18. **Asked by Lucas:** in the Magic brainstorm (Paths + Schools; a School is the spell's form, with rules attached), he corrected Claude's first School list: [Focus] is concentration and applies to all spells, so it can't define Enchantment; a Fireball is an Evocation and a Save, so Evocation can't mean "a Strike". "Feel free to suggest more, after making a small research on the magic school topic in TTRPGs and magical TTRPG systems (and even Dominions if necessary)."

**What was read.** A small research; most of it is thin.
- **[read]** Dominions 6's manual: the seven schools' descriptions (in `dominions-magic.md`), and a count of its spells by Path and School made from the appendix by pattern-matching (rough: battle spells and rituals together, each under its first Path).
- **[checked]** EN World, "why are spell schools and what should they be?" (page 2 of 9 only); a fan wiki's copy of Savage Worlds' trappings rules (the general rules, not the list of trappings).
- **[lead]** (search summaries only, pages not opened or refused): Pathfinder 2e Remaster's removal of schools (Paizo's blog returned 403); other EN World and Giant in the Playground threads on D&D's schools; Mage: the Ascension's sphere ranks (fan wikis); Magic: the Gathering's mechanical color pie (wiki summaries of Mark Rosewater's articles); Shadow of the Demon Lord's traditions (names only).
- **[memory]** D&D's eight schools and what each covers, and 4e's keywords, are from Claude's training, not from a page opened today.

**Other games' sortings and opinions, never 2d12's rules. Nothing here is decided.**

## 1. Takeaways

1. **D&D's eight schools mix two questions, and that is why they blur.** Five are verbs: Evocation (release energy), Conjuration (bring or create), Transmutation (change), Abjuration (protect, block, banish), Divination (reveal). Three are nouns: Necromancy (life and death), Illusion (the senses), Enchantment (the mind) [memory]. A poster on EN World puts the finger on it: some schools say what a spell does to things, Necromancy says which things (DEFCON 1) [checked]. The classic misfiles follow: is Cause Fear Enchantment (the mind) or Necromancy? Why is Shield Abjuration and not Evocation or Conjuration (Undrave)? Healing has sat in three different schools across editions [memory].
2. **In a Paths × Schools grid the nouns already have a home.** Dominions' Paths are the nouns: Death is the necromancy, Glamour the illusion, Astral and Glamour the mind. So **a 2d12 School must be a verb, and only a verb**: one question an axis.
3. **A school with no rules gets cut.** Pathfinder 2e's Remaster removed the eight schools; the search's reading is that licensing drove it, that only Illusion survives (as a trait), and that the wizard's replacement lists were criticized as "somewhat arbitrary" [lead]. On EN World the schools are called "a Legacy Sacred Cow" kept for the old Illusionist, "almost completely flavorless", "too narrow (Abjuration) or too broad (Transmutation)" [checked / lead]. Lucas's choice (rules attached) is the answer these threads ask for.
4. **Uneven schools are a known failure:** one school that holds half the spells and one that holds five. Dominions' own counts lean the same way (Alteration and Enchantment are the fattest, Conjuration's battle half is thin). Each School should have enough to do in a fight.
5. **Savage Worlds is the opposite extreme, and a warning.** Its powers are pure forms (bolt, blast, barrier); the "trapping" (fire, ice) is appearance, with a game effect only when a target has a weakness or the GM grants a +2 / -2 "when logic supports it" [checked]. Two warnings for 2d12: if a Path only renames the damage, the grid is one axis wearing costumes; and "when logic supports it" is exactly the ambiguity Lucas wants out. **The Path must change the card by a written rule** (its rider, its damage type, what it is allowed to do).
6. **Tags instead of one box.** Level Up (A5e) reportedly gives each spell several tags from 53 schools ("Fireball is an Evocation, Arcane, Fire spell", Corinnguard) [checked as a poster's report]; D&D 4e replaced schools with keywords on every power [memory]. Lucas's "we will create more traits" goes this way: one School for each spell (it carries the rules), and traits for the rest (what it targets, how it lasts).
7. **A pie: what each color can't do.** Magic: the Gathering assigns every effect to its colors as primary, secondary or tertiary, and the design rule is said to be about what a color can't do (blue has no direct damage, green no counterspells) [lead]. Dominions' counts show the same leaning without saying so: Fire is thick in Evocation, Earth and Water in Alteration, Nature in Enchantment, Death and Glamour in Thaumaturgy; Astral barely conjures. **A grid doesn't need every cell full: a Path's identity is which Schools it is strong in, and which it is refused.**
8. **A level that means the same thing everywhere.** Mage: the Ascension's spheres are reported to share a ladder: rank 1 perceive, rank 2 small manipulation, rank 3 control, and up [lead]. For 2d12: a School level could have one meaning across every Path (level 1: one target, close; level 2: an area or far; level 3: the battlefield), which is what "excess School level grows the form" already implies.

## 2. Applied to 2d12: the Schools as verbs

Lucas's two corrections and takeaway 2 give the rule: **the School is what the spell's main effect does to the world.** Not how it is resolved (a Strike or a Save is the card's business, as on weapon cards), not what it targets, not whether it needs concentration (all spells do).

Since an Action does one main thing and at most one rider (the charter), "the main effect" is not a matter of taste: it is the card's main line. A rider never changes the School.

**Draft (Dominions' names; nothing decided):**

| School | Its verb | Covers | Dominions' own sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Evocation** | **Release.** The Path let loose, raw, and gone at once. | Bolts and blasts, as a Strike or as a Save; one target or an area | "Projects arcane power into the physical world [...] fireballs and lightning bolts" |
| **Alteration** | **Change.** A creature or a piece of ground gains or loses a property for a set time. | Buffs, conditions, terrain; ends by itself (EoT, rounds) | "Changes the physical world [...] increased strength, resistance to fire" |
| **Enchantment** | **Lay.** A magic that stands on the map or on a creature as a thing of its own. | Walls of fire, auras, wards, a blessing on a weapon; it has an owner, it can be dispelled, it ends when its caster goes Down | "Grants magical properties to men, items or the land" |
| **Conjuration** | **Bring.** A creature or an object that wasn't there, with its own stat block. | Summons, created objects | "Summons powers and beings from other worlds" |
| **Thaumaturgy** | **Command.** Your will imposed on a mind, on magic itself, or on space. | Charm, fear, sleep; dispel and counter; teleport; scrying | "Manipulates the arcane world in ways traditionally associated with sorcery" |

**The Alteration / Enchantment edge** (the two that blur in Dominions): an Alteration is done when it is cast and runs out by itself; an Enchantment is a standing thing with an owner. That needs a new trait (a working name: **[Standing]**): "it lasts until you go Down, you end it, or it is dispelled; you hold one at a time". [Focus] stays what Lucas says it is, concentration, on every spell.

### Where the draft creaks: twelve classic spells

| Classic spell | Path • School under the draft | Clean? |
|---|---|---|
| Fireball | Fire • Evocation (a Save, an area) | Yes |
| Lightning Bolt, Magic Missile | Air / Astral • Evocation (a Strike) | Yes |
| Haste, Stoneskin (a few rounds) | Water / Earth • Alteration | Yes |
| Entangle | Nature • Alteration (the ground changes) | Yes, if "Bring" needs a stat block |
| Wall of Fire | Fire • Enchantment | Yes |
| Invisibility | Glamour • Alteration | Yes |
| Charm, Fear, Sleep | Glamour / Death / Astral • Thaumaturgy | Yes |
| Summon Elemental | (its Path) • Conjuration | Yes; summons have no rules yet |
| Counterspell, Dispel | Astral • Thaumaturgy | Works; see candidate 1 |
| **Shield** (a Reaction, one hit) | Alteration? Enchantment? | **Creaks**: protection has no home |
| **Cure Wounds** | Nature • Alteration? Evocation? | **Creaks**: the old D&D problem |
| **Teleport, Misty Step** | Astral • Thaumaturgy (Dominions files Blink there) | **Creaks**: "command space" is a stretch |

### Candidates to add or change (each with its reason, the ones Claude would reject included)

1. **A protection School (D&D's Abjuration; a 2d12 name such as Warding): the verb "Stop".** Shields, counterspells, dispels, banishment. *For:* it fixes Shield, takes Counterspell and Dispel out of the vague half of Thaumaturgy, and gives a caster a defender's job, which a tactical game wants. *Against:* Dominions has no such school (its protections sit in Alteration and Enchantment), and six Schools is more grid. **Claude would add it.**
2. **Healing under Evocation: "Release" is whatever the Path's raw power is.** Fire's is burning; Nature's (and Holy's) is life. Then Evocation isn't "harm", it is "the Path, raw", and a Death Evocation and a Nature Evocation are mirror images. D&D 5e files healing in Evocation for this reason [memory]. *Against:* a School that both harms and heals is harder to hang rules on. **Claude leans to it;** the alternative is healing as an Alteration (a body changed).
3. **Movement and teleport under Conjuration** (D&D's choice: you "bring" yourself). *Against:* Bring then needs two definitions. Alternative: leave it in Thaumaturgy as "command over space". **Open;** few battle spells are at stake.
4. **A Divination School (the verb "Reveal").** Dominions has none and Ars Magica's version is almost unused in battle. **Reject for battle;** rituals and Thaumaturgy hold it.
5. **Illusion and Necromancy as Schools.** They are nouns: here they are the Glamour and Death Paths. **Reject** (takeaway 2).
6. **Split Evocation into a Strike School and a Save School** (Lucas's question, "new school or merge?"). **Merge:** the roll is how a card is resolved, and weapon cards already carry both kinds without a category for each. A School split by the roll would be the "mixed questions" mistake of takeaway 1.
7. **Merge Alteration and Enchantment** into one "Change" School, the standing ones marked by the trait alone. *For:* removes the blurriest edge. *Against:* it would be the fattest School by far (takeaway 4) and Dominions' flavor loses a name. **Claude would keep them apart, on the [Standing] edge.**
8. **Construction** (Dominions' item school). Out of battle; with items and rituals later.

### A pie for the Paths (takeaway 7)

Worth a table before spells are written: for each Path, the Schools it is strong in, the ones it touches, and the ones it is refused. Dominions' counts suggest a first cut (Fire: Evocation strong, Conjuration weak; Earth: Alteration and Enchantment; Nature: Enchantment and Alteration; Death and Glamour: Thaumaturgy; Astral: Thaumaturgy and Evocation, almost no Conjuration). Not drafted here.
