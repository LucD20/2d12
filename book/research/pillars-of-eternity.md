# Pillars of Eternity (1 and Deadfire): what 2d12 could borrow

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Asked by Lucas,** with his playtesters' ideas for the Paladin and the Archer (`talents-design.md` section 9; one player: "Pillars of Eternity has something a tiny bit similar, where the paladin order you pick modifies your smite and lay on hands"). The question is the same as `divinity-original-sin-2.md`: the mechanics players love, what fits a tabletop, what could fit 2d12.

**What was read, and how far to trust it.** One independent researcher; the full report is `raw/2026-11-20-pillars-of-eternity.md` (eight topics, sources with URLs). Rules [read] mostly from the Fextralife Deadfire wiki, Shacknews, GameBanshee and Obsidian's Kickstarter updates; Josh Sawyer's views through RPG Codex and Shacknews reporting. **Blocked:** the fandom wiki, the Obsidian forums, Reddit. **Player opinion is the weakest part** (one Beamdog thread, three Steam threads, RPG Codex, many forum titles and snippets, marked [lead]). Every page went through a summarizing reader: **quotes need a second look before they are shown as verbatim.** Sources disagree on some numbers (the graze band, the armor penalty); the report states each. Obsidian's own pen-and-paper RPG doesn't use the video game's rules [lead]; Obsidian calls the d100 formulas "hard to calculate at a physical table" [read]. This file is Claude's summary.

**Other games' opinions, never 2d12's rules. Nothing here is decided.**

## The most borrowable ideas (ranked by the researcher; the mappings are suggestions)

| # | Idea | In Pillars | Evidence | How it could map onto 2d12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Conditions as families with steps; Resist lowers a step instead of giving immunity** | Deadfire: six families of three levels; Resistance moves a condition one level down and blocks level 1, weakness one up, the matching buff cancels it | rules [read]; Sawyer: "not immune to all... just a subset" [checked]; the names confused players [lead] | A boss's Resist turns a hard condition into a softer one of its family: no single effect shuts it down (the boss rule) |
| 2 | **A god or order rewrites a button the class already has** | Bleak Walkers: Flames of Devotion adds corrode and Sickened; Kind Wayfarers: it heals nearby allies; Darcozzi: Lay on Hands adds a Flame Shield; Shieldbearers: its target can't die for 4 s; Goldpact: Sworn Enemy gives armor. PoE1 priests: each god gives its favored weapons and one themed spell | [read]; the favorite orders are the ones that change Flames, shown on every use [lead]; one-tweak orders (Goldpact) "don't seem great" [lead] | **The players' Paladin idea:** the god sets Smite's subtype and Tier 3 rider, the stance's mode and Lay On Hands' rider |
| 3 | **An aura with three modes, one at a time** | PoE1: Zealous Focus (attack), Endurance (defense), Charge (movement), each with a forked upgrade | [read] | The Paladin's stance (Hallowed Stance, Lantern of Faith) with modes; Talents fork them |
| 4 | **"Move one Tier" effects** | 15% of grazes become hits; Aware; Paralyzed targets take crits | [read]; Sawyer: grazes made damage "feel much better" [checked] | A Talent raises or lowers a Tier on a set trigger: never a percentage or a number (T1) |
| 5 | **More against a conditioned target, more again against two** | The rogue's Sneak Attack and Deathblows | [lead] | Damage or a rider keyed on the condition list: teamwork |
| 6 | **Pools fed by an event; Talents change how they fill, not their size** | Cipher Focus from hits, Monk Wounds from damage taken; Keen Mind, Imagined Pain change the gain; a 5e fan cipher does it on paper with a die per hit | [checked] / [read]; a pool that runs itself (the Chanter) was called "boring" [lead] | Martial pools as class Features (Escalation and Surge already work this way) |
| 7 | **An engagement limit a front-liner can widen** | One foe by default; Defender +2, a spear or a shield +1; Escape and Swift counter it | rules [read]; players split; unclear triggers [checked / lead] | A hook on Engaged for Talents and Techniques: write what starts and ends it; approaching never provokes |
| 8 | **A weapon's stance with a cost** | Half-Sword, Body Blows, Engaging Thrust and other modals | the modals that change the target liked, plain Penetration ones "useless" [lead]; PoE1's Weapon Focus a tax on odd weapon groups [checked] | Mastery Techniques as stances |
| 9 | **A subclass that pairs a gain with a cost on one feature** | Sharpshooter (crits at long range, Penetration up close), Berserker, Skald | [lead] | Archer options; Driving Flight (a hit carries on to the next foe in line) as a Trick Shot |
| 10 | **Per encounter over per rest** | Sawyer: "most players will not take per-rest abilities"; Deadfire moved almost everything to per encounter | [checked] | 2d12's Frequencies already agree |
| 11 | **Soulbound weapons unlocked by story beats, not kill counts** | The kill-count grind was the main complaint | [lead] | A magic item that grows with the story |

**Traps it shows:** a penalty for losing your own pet (Bonded Grief); kill-triggered talents (they need the killing blow); flat passives; **Faith and Conviction** (a paladin's defenses scale with how well its behavior matches its order: the GM must judge behavior, and players were divided) [lead].

## How it applies to 2d12

- **The players' Paladin idea has its model here** (idea 2): the god rewrites the buttons the Paladin already has, and the favorite orders are those whose change shows on every use. Their three steps (a blessing on the weapon, an aura, an inward empowerment) map onto Smite, the stance and a Talent rank.
- **The boss rule** gets two candidate shapes (with Divinity's round of immunity): a condition stepped down by Resist (idea 1).
- **What players avoid:** per-rest abilities, kill triggers, behavior-judged power.
