# Dominions 6 manual: Claude's reading log (2026-11-20)

The notes Claude took while reading the Dominions 6 manual (https://www.illwinter.com/dom6/dom6manual.pdf, revision 2, 449 pages) page by page, extracted column by column with pdftotext. Dominions' rules only, in the manual's order, with page numbers; nothing here is a judgment for 2d12 (that is `research/dominions-mechanics.md`). Pages read in full: 5-117 and 208-240; pages 118-207 and 241-436 (spell and unit tables) scanned only for ability names.

## p12 Major changes
- Battlefield terrain obstacles (bushes, rocks) with own stats, affected by AoE, crushed by large monsters, small ones go around.
- Mounts have own stats; hit rider or mount or both with AoE; mount killed, rider fights on.
- Assassination happens in a confined location fitting the target (mage in library, commander in tavern); bystanders dragged in.
- Hidden map explored. Extreme scales (4,5) bring special effects "not necessarily good". Legendary spells: one at a time at research 9.
## p19 Hall of Fame: commanders with most fights survived / kills gain a Heroic Ability that keeps improving while they stay listed (a ranking; can be pushed off).
## p21-22 Province: terrain sets population/resources/magic sites; several terrains add up. Unrest divides income (1+unrest*0.02) and resources (1+unrest*0.01); unrest 100 = no recruiting. Income needs an unbroken chain of friendly provinces to a fort ("taxation requires communication").
## p23-26 Province detail
- Dominion = religious dominance, one god per province; white/black candles; a priest present tells you WHOSE hostile dominion it is.
- Unrest raised by events, spies, blood hunting, sites, spells; lowered by patrolling, province defense, Order scale. Unrest also lowers blood-slave capture and finding stealthy units by patrol. Max 500 or pop/10.
- Supply & starvation: units eat by size; starving: -4 morale, 5% disease (50% if starving again); "survival" skill: 50% unaffected + 50% no disease. Disease stays after starvation ends.
- Province defense: cost = the new level in gold (incremental); refilled after each battle while held.
- CORPSES: count of unburied dead in a province, used by death spells and raising undead; visible only to a death mage or an undead priest. (Location resource for necromancy; knowledge gated by Path.)
- Upkeep = gold cost/15 a turn; sacred units and slaves half.
- Forts: levels replace (don't stack); admin draws resources and spreads supply (admin*6/(dist+1)); siege supply = storage / length of siege so far. Mason trait builds one level higher.
- Wall defenders: never run out of ammo; +20% missile range; protection like a tower shield, Protection 30, use the better of own shield or wall.
## p27-30
- Forts can't draw from neighbours that also have forts (spacing rule). Temples: one per province, razed when the province is captured; give priests a preaching bonus. Labs: research, gem transfer, rituals only where there's a lab.
- MAGIC SITES: hidden places in a province; found only by SEARCHING; four difficulties; a mage finds a site only if their level in the site's Path >= its difficulty (max 4 ever needed). Some spells reveal all sites of a Path (Haruspex: Nature) or all (Akashic Knowledge). Sites give gems, unique recruits, entry to other places (Void Gate), or need a building first (Library needs a lab: "the role-playing reasons should be obvious"). A site may be useless to you (a cave for Blood mages). Harmful sites act whether found or not.
- Province defense: 10 PD = -1 unrest a turn; from PD 15 it detects stealthy units (patrol strength = PD-15).
- Unrest causes: ruler's nation differs from the god whose dominion holds the land ("the people worship one god but are ruled by another"); blood hunting; spies and bards; ill omens.
- Mercenaries: hired 3 turns; when a contract ends, the last employer's bid counts double that turn only.
- SCOUTING & SCRYING: a table of what each source reveals (scout: owner, military, forts, history, temperature; priest: + dominion strength and owner; spy: + income, supply, sites, unrest, PD, better military; dominion: owner, income, temperature, neighbours' dominion; scrying: nearly everything, very accurate military; owning: everything + names 1-3 steps away by era). One table, each source named, no argument.
- AI bonus table (money). Pretender = the nation's spirit; a commander on the map and battlefield.
## p31-34 Pretender creation
- 450 design points split: physical form (a chassis with its own cost, attributes and starting paths), magic, dominion (candles + scales); The Awakening trades a late arrival for more points.
- Chassis attributes are inherent, "cannot be conferred or purchased separately".
- IMMORTALITY two kinds: dominion immortality (killed in a province with friendly dominion -> re-forms at home; outside -> needs Call God); full immortality (re-forms anywhere). Re-forming takes ~3 months by monster type; SOUL SLAIN never re-forms; re-forming removes most afflictions; dying on a remote plane (Void, Inferno) blocks immortality.
- Immobile pretenders: can't move; teleport rituals can carry most; some too big even for that; "Wind Ride isn't true teleportation and requires the caster to be mobile".
- DRAGON pretenders appear as a wizard until wounded or "change shape" order ("easier to cast spells with hands") -> form change when hurt.
- TRINITIES: one being as three bodies, sharing part of their magic; two together lose some power, one alone loses much; each can research at once; a dead one is called back in half the time.
- Magic purchase: the Nth point YOU add in a path costs 8*N (cumulative: 1->4 = 72); counted from what you add, not the total, so a chassis that already has a path is a saving; the first point in a NEW path costs the chassis's own "New Path Cost" (e.g. 80).
- BLESS: sacred units only (recruited at the home site; a captor can't recruit them). Blessing = level 1 holy spell, cast in battle only: +1 morale + the pretender's bless effects + Thrones' blesses. Bless points: each path level above 1 = 1 point (Air2/Death4/Nature6 = 1+3+5); a nation can add bonus points. An effect costs points equal to the sum of its path requirements (Astral 3 & Fire 1 = 4); some also need a SCALE (Cold 1, Magic 1, Misfortune 1, Death 1...) at no cost. PASSIVE blesses (*) are always on (survival in a terrain, unaging, larger, swimming...). "Incarnate only" effects need the god awake AND alive. Pretender and disciples are sacred: auto-blessed in their dominion, can't be blessed outside it.
- The full bless list (p33-34) by path: e.g. Fire4 Righteous Wrath, Fire5 Death Explosion / Heat Aura, Air3 Awareness, Earth4 Unbreakable, Earth5 Reconstruction, Astral4 Far Caster / Arcane Finesse, Death1 Undying, Death6 Reforming Flesh, Nature5 Recuperation / Berserker, Glamour1 Heroism (+50% XP), Glamour2 Quiet Stride, Glamour6 Obfuscate, Blood4 Blood Surge, Blood5 Blood Bond, Blood7 Blood Vengeance. Survival blesses: Wasteland (F1+D1), Swamp (W1+N1), Mountain (E1), Forest (N2), Winter's Gift (W1 + Cold scale).
- Dominion candles: the Nth added candle costs 7*N; min 1, max 10.
## p35-37 Scales, awakening, death of a god
- SCALES: 6 two-sided scales (Order/Turmoil, Productivity/Sloth, Heat/Cold, Growth/Death, Fortune/Misfortune, Magic/Drain). Tipping toward the bad side GIVES 40 design points, toward the good side costs 40 (a flaw that pays). Range -5..+5, usually only +/-2; a pretender/nation "attuned" to a scale can go one step further, and its opposite becomes harder (max 1). Extreme 4-5 bring unwanted side effects. Heat/Cold: points only for moving away from the race's preferred temperature. A province's scales move toward the god's slowly, and only as far as dominion strength allows.
- Magic scale: spells harder to resist (-0.5 MR a step), 10% less fatigue a step, +1 research per mage, +50 starting research. Drain the reverse.
- Pretender and prophet in friendly dominion: per candle +1 Str, +1/2 MR, +10% HP; in enemy dominion the reverse (HP floor 10%).
- AWAKENING: start the game with the god (450), Dormant (arrives after ~1 year, +150), Imprisoned (~3 years, +350). The time is not exact. Disciples wake in half the time.
- PRETENDER DEATH: each death costs one level in one path OR one candle; chance of losing magic = 50% + 10% per Nature level; Nature most likely lost, Death least; small chance of GAINING Death (smaller: Astral or Blood) on dying: "The lessons of the Underworld are unpredictable." Blesses never change after creation. Dominion floor 1.
- Dominion-immortal dying in friendly dominion: no loss.
- CALL GOD: each priest level on the order makes 1 point a turn; at ~50 (deliberately not exact) the god returns at home. Disciple games +50%. Ur/Uruk return with no loss.
- Auto-bless: gods and disciples in their own dominion; sacred units fighting beside the god in friendly dominion.
- Units: a value's modifications are shown on click (base 12, +1 XP, +1 dominion = 14). Commanders vs units: only commanders lead, change gear, carry gems.
## p38-41 Units
- Strength also breaks free from entanglement. ENCUMBRANCE = fatigue gained on each turn the unit ATTACKS; moving alone costs none.
- AGE: max age (when old-age penalties and afflictions start) +50% per level of the creature's "own" Path: undead Death, inanimate Earth, demons Blood, everyone else Nature; -5% per Fire level for those Nature can change ("burn bright, burn fast"). A Path changes your lifespan.
- Leadership three kinds: ordinary, undead (50 per Death level, 10 per Blood level), magical (by Path, Astral double).
- Commanders only: leadership, change equipment, carry gems.
- AMBIDEXTROUS N: reduces the attack penalty of two+ weapons by N (not the encumbrance).
- BERSERKER: when wounded, a morale check vs 12 -> berserk: fights until dead, never routs; +N bonus to berserk stats; falling unconscious ends it (can go berserk again after waking and being wounded).
- Ethereal: 75% of non-magical strikes miss; passes through fort walls.
- Glamour units: Mirror Image in combat; undetectable in friendly provinces.
- RECUPERATION heals battle afflictions over time unless Old Age. REGENERATION has kind-specific twins: Reconstruction (inanimate only), Reforming Flesh (undead only).
- AWE N: attackers check morale vs 10+N or can't attack it this round. SUN AWE doesn't work underground or in the dark.
- FEAR: an aura (6 squares + the Fear bonus in squares; strength +1 per full +5); morale lowered; the whole squad checks vs 10 or routs.
- HEAT / CHILL aura: level 1-2 clouds in 3 squares; size grows with the matching scale and shrinks with the opposite; many auras spread the cloud but don't stack on one victim. Poison cloud aura likewise.
- INVISIBILITY: -9 to melee against the unseen; Spirit Sight sees; the already blind take no extra penalty.
- PETRIFICATION (Medusa): anyone ATTACKING her is petrified on a failed MR roll; after 3-6 rounds, a second MR roll: pass, back to normal; fail, dies.
- STANDARD: raises the morale of its whole squad; only the best standard in a squad counts.
- HORROR MARK: a small monthly chance a Horror attacks; several marks raise it; "there is no way of knowing how bad the mark is"; certain spells make horrors attack the marked; in battle horrors attack marked units first; stronger marks call stronger horrors.
- TRAMPLE: a large unit enters a smaller one's square and displaces everyone in it to adjacent squares; each makes Defense - fatigue/10 vs 3d6: fail = 7 + Size damage, armor halved, min 1; pass = displaced, 1 damage. Ethereal tramplers half.
- SWALLOW: after a trample, the target is removed from the battlefield until the swallower dies; some digest (damage a turn), some heal from it; being swallowed stops Regeneration, Phoenix Pyre and other life-savers.
- UNIT CLASSES: Magic being (mage leader or rout), Mindless (morale 50, never rout with a leader; dissolve without; can't share a squad with the minded), Undead (banishment; undead leadership; mixed squad -1 morale; can't have afflictions healed; disease harmless), Demon (banishment; undead leadership; disease immune), Lifeless (immune to disease, regeneration, drain, affliction healing, many combat spells; can still heal by drain attacks), Spiritform (immune to transformation buffs like Ironskin, disease, affliction healing), Animal (half supply; armless ones half strength at sieges).
- XP: 1 a month just by surviving; battle won +4, battle retreated +1 (once a month); +1 per melee strike landed (or trample). Small stat steps at 15/50/100/200/400 XP (+1 Att/Def/Prec each, Str/HP sometimes, Morale every step, MR once). Heroism bless +50% XP.
- HALL OF FAME -> heroic abilities (non-pretender commanders; unique beings can't enter).
- AFFLICTIONS: chance = the share of total HP lost to THAT strike (2 of 10 = 20%); the hit location decides which (an arm, an eye). Healed by: Recuperation (not with Old Age), re-forming after death (immortals), changing back from an involuntary shapechange (unreliable), a HEALER N (cures N afflictions a turn in the province), disease healer N, Gift of Health (global), the Chalice, an Elixir, rituals, healing sites. Undead/inanimate/spiritform can't be healed of them. An affliction from a CURSED ITEM can't be healed until the item is removed. Each affliction has a difficulty a healing check must beat. Corpse stitcher heals corporeal undead.
## p42-45 Recruiting, squads, battle orders
- Curses and horror marks can't be removed (unlike afflictions); "horror marks may be lessened by staying dead".
- Three recruitment costs, each with a meaning: gold = training, resources = equipment, recruitment points = rarity of the fit in a populated land. Sacred units also cost Holy points, limited by the home's max dominion. Some units limited per turn.
- Squads up to the commander's leadership; max 5 squads per commander, extra squads may lose morale. Undead + magic being needs undead leadership.
- BATTLE ORDERS (set before battle, the units obey): None (AI), Attack*, Fire*, Guard Commander (stays on the commander; joins the commander's ASSASSINATION fight with a chance; ranged guards fire only at enemies getting close), Hold and attack* (holds 2 rounds then advances), Hold and fire*, Fire and keep distance*, Retreat (treated as a rout).
- TARGET ORDERS: random, Archers, Cavalry (or fast), Fliers, Large monsters (size 7+, else 6), Closest, Rearmost. Units can still get stuck in an enemy's zone of control.
- "Can't my soldiers just sit back and defend indefinitely? No. Hold and Attack holds 2 rounds, then they attack."
- COMMANDER ORDERS: up to five SPECIFIC orders scripted (one a turn), then a GENERAL order for the rest of the battle; no orders = the AI decides. Scripts can be copied between commanders.
## p46-49 Commander orders, inventory, movement
- Commander SPECIFIC orders (one a round, up to 5 scripted): Hold; Hold or Fire; Hold or Cast (AI's spell); Cast a specific spell (falls back to the AI if no gems or no valid target in range); Attack one turn; Fly attack one turn. GENERAL orders for the rest: Stay behind troops; Attack; Cast spells; Advance and cast; Retreat. "Conservative gem use" box.
- ITEM SLOTS by body: head, body, hands, feet, misc; a non-human may lack a head or feet; a mounted commander's feet slot is off while mounted (boots work again when dismounted); a mount has a barding slot. A unit's standard gear can't be removed: a better item covers it, and it reappears when the item is taken off.
- Anyone carries gems (limit 40) even if they can't use them: a "gem squire" beside the mage.
- MOVEMENT on the map: half-steps (pay to leave + pay to enter), terrain costs (plains 3, forest 5, highlands 6, swamp 7...), enemy province +4 (+3 if stealthy), snow +1, roads -2 (min 2); fliers 3 almost everywhere. A SURVIVAL ability (forest, swamp, waste, mountain) = -2 in that terrain (and its cave twin). Army moves at its slowest; only commanders move armies.
- Border terrains: a RIVER can be crossed only if frozen (Cold +1 on both sides); a MOUNTAIN PASS only if open (Heat +1 on both sides); fliers/floaters/swimmers or the right survival ignore them. All the army needs the ability (a flying commander doesn't make his troops fly; exceptions: a commander who grants water-breathing; sailing commanders carry others).
- STEALTH: sneaking units don't fight where they enter; a stealthy commander with non-stealthy troops loses stealth. A stealthy PRIEST preaching gets more stealth the more populous the province ("the newly converted protect and hide their cult leaders").
- Glamour units invisible in friendly provinces. Darkness: night -3, Utterdark -6, blind -9 to Att/Def/Prec; Darkvision reduces; no help when blind.
- Combat starts only between MUTUALLY DISCOVERED forces; with 3+ sides the battles are fought one after another, the survivors defending in the next.
## p50-53 Orders
- Allied defenders attacked: the attacker fights them one after another in random order.
- A move legal when ordered but illegal at hosting (boots removed) keeps its arrow but doesn't happen (checked at resolution).
- SNEAK / PATROL / HIDING: success depends on the hider's stealth, HOW MANY try to hide, how many look. Stealth strength = leader's Stealth -1 per other stealthy unit in his army (-0.5 if that unit has 50+). Patrol strength = sum of patrollers' (Precision + Map Move, or 30 if flying)/20 (+patrol bonus; commanders x2; mindless/undisciplined x1/2) - unrest/2 (capped) + (PD-14 if PD>=15). Found if Patrol + 2d25 open > Stealth + 2d25 open. Fast and flying units find better. Each unrest point patrol removes kills 10 population ("permanent elimination of the troublemakers"); no unrest, no harm. A patrol that finds someone brings the whole defending force on them.
- Sneak and attack: from hiding, attack an adjacent province.
- Defend = patrol without searching; with a fort, defenders go inside and get besieged, patrollers fight outside.
- Movement resolves in two steps: moves into FRIENDLY provinces first, then into enemy ones (reinforcements arrive before the attack). Two armies ordered into each other's provinces: a battle in one, in the other, or they miss and swap, by army size and terrain.
- BLOOD HUNT: three gates in a row: level (10 + 30 x Blood level %), population (pop/75 %), unrest (fails on unrest/4 %). Success: d6 + level slaves, unrest +d(slaves x 3 + 4); failure: d6-1 unrest. Commoners may refuse and fight the hunter and his guards; at dominion 10 almost nobody refuses: "it is an honor to be sacrificed".
- Siege orders: only units on Maintain Siege add siege strength. Storm Castle after defenses reach 0; a relief force fights the besiegers first.
- ASSASSINATE: a random enemy commander; the fight includes guards or townsfolk nearby; each assigned BODYGUARD has a 50% chance to be present (raised by the Bodyguard ability, lowered by the assassin's PATIENCE). The victim is unprepared (ignores its scripts). The PLACE follows the victim and the buildings (a priest praying alone in the temple; a commander in the tavern); townspeople always start surprised, guards may (patience raises it). A mounted victim is usually on foot; its mount dies too. Walls of a besieged fort block it unless Scale Walls, flying or teleport (the same for seduction, lure, infiltration).
- SEDUCTION (opposite gender; morale check or switches sides; the seducer needs a friendly province adjacent, a flier carries the victim to the capital; beauty items make it harder; a bad failure can start an assassination fight). DREAM SEDUCTION (MR roll + morale). CORRUPTION (like seduction, beauty doesn't help). LURE OF THE SIRENS (coastal only; MR then morale vs 10; the victim drowns, or if it breathes water it attacks the siren in an assassination fight; failure costs the siren nothing).
- PILLAGE: unrest up, population down, supplies down, gold and food (supplies last a month); bigger forces better; fast, large, barbarians, Fear especially good. RAID: pillager with map move 20+: move then pillage at half strength; must win any fight first.
- REANIMATE (undead priests, some nations): choose the kind; Ghouls turn living people (population drops); Soulless need unburied corpses (corpses drop).
## p54-56 More orders; the turn sequence
- Reanimation by priest level: ghouls 6..10 (cost living population), soulless 8..40 (need unburied corpses, use them up), longdead 3..11 (no limit). Asphodel's manikins need living people or human corpses, else carrion beasts (always possible); much weaker in enemy dominion; a stronger priest makes more, never better ones.
- CONTACT ALLIES: a commander spends the month gathering more troops.
- Blood sacrifice: only in a temple, only by nations whose HISTORY includes it.
- BECOME PROPHET: one prophet per nation; priest level +1 or to 3; spreads dominion like a temple; HP up in friendly dominion, down in enemy; killed -> wait 6 turns for a new one.
- INSTILL UPRISING (spies, bards): unrest up.
- INFILTRATE (enemy capital only): reveals the mundane graphs; IF THE SPY IS A CASTER also the magic graphs; IF A PRIEST also the dominion graph. ~50% fails, and THEN the enemy learns someone tried.
- Hide (default for still stealthy units); found = a separate battle.
- Build: anyone builds a fort, a SACRED commander a temple, a MAGE a lab. Demolish: one month; temples can't be demolished; conquered enemy temples vanish.
- THE TURN RESOLUTION SEQUENCE: 51 numbered steps, each saying what it guarantees: messages first ("gold, gems, and items are always sent, because everything that could stop this from happening occurs later"), research (counts even if the mage is assassinated later that turn), recruitment, empowerment, forging, preaching, thrones, quick orders, rituals (random order), remote attacks, magic battles, lost in planes, site searches, prophets, Call God, awakening, blood hunting, horrors, assassinations, ..., friendly movement before other movement, battles, castle storming, globals, random events, items' effects, sneak discovery, building, special orders ("allies summoned during a turn will not be available for that turn's battles"), pillage BEFORE income, unrest, starvation (the first starving month's battles are fought unstarved, because battles come earlier), upkeep and desertion, dominion spread and effects, site effects, aging (midwinter only), heal/disease, insanity, mercenaries, new heroes. Retreats happen after all battles of a step.
## p57-60 Combat basics
- Turn sequence tail: lone units without commanders in enemy land die; aftermath validates orders and items.
- PARTIAL vs FULL ownership of a province (besieger owns the land, the besieged the fort); some events need full ownership.
- "Battles in Dominions are NOT fought to the death. They are fought until one side loses its nerve and routs." Routing from losses and morale lets small elite units hold off masses. A squad checks morale when it suffers enough casualties. Troops rout when ALL their commanders are dead or routed; the whole army routs at 75% of its total HP lost. "There is no 'attack commanders' order. You will have to figure out how best to do this yourself!"
- LEADERSHIP vs SQUADS morale table: Ldr 10: -1 for one squad, -1 more per extra squad; 50: none up to two, -1 per extra; 100: +1 up to three; 150: +2 up to four; 200: +3 for five. XP raises how many a commander leads, not the morale bonus.
- Mixing undisciplined units makes the squad undisciplined (-1 morale); undead with living -1; demons with normal -1.
- FORMATIONS: box; line, double line, sparse line (-1 morale) only for good commanders; skirmish (checkerboard, -1 morale). Undisciplined squads only skirmish; TIGHT REIN lifts that; SKIRMISHER negates the -1; FORMATION FIGHTER fits more per square.
- Battlefield: diagonal 50% more; adjacency locks units in a ZONE OF CONTROL; FLEEING units ignore it. A unit 3+ size points larger can push smaller ones out of a full square (+1 speed cost); tramplers never displace friends unless routing.
- Timing by COOLDOWN: after a strike ~a round, after a move short (faster units shorter); between two adjacent units, whoever's cooldown ends first strikes.
- Melee: attack roll vs defense roll; with a shield three outcomes: miss (beats def+parry), SHIELD HIT (between def and def+parry: shield Protection added), clean hit. Head hit: the helmet's protection replaces the body's (a shield hit still adds the shield).
## p61-65 Combat detail
- Armor defeating hits likelier with fatigue; immobilized or unconscious count as 100 fatigue.
- SHIELD DESTRUCTION: shield Resistance = its protection (+5 magic); attack Break = damage before protection (+50% slash, +25% blunt). Break >= 3x Res: DAMAGED (-20% protection); >= 5x: BROKEN (-50%); a damaged shield damaged again breaks 25%. Magic shields repair themselves after battle unless broken (then destroyed for good; the commander falls back to his standard shield); mundane ones are repaired where there are spare resources.
- HIT LOCATIONS by REACH: torso 50, arms 20, legs 20, head 10; to hit the head, attacker size + weapon length must equal target size (torso 1 less, arms 2 less): a human with a mace hits a size-7 creature only in the legs. Low-built creatures (lizards, dragons: 2 steps; quadrupeds: 1) are easier to reach. Some creatures lack locations (a dragon has no arms; a cube nothing). A creature 2+ sizes larger hits heads 20% and legs 10%. A lost limb is the one hit; losing the head kills unless you don't need it (undead, regenerators, many heads); an eye only on a head hit.
- WEAPON TYPES each with its own property: BLUNT +25% on head hits and +25% to shield break; SLASH +25% after protection and +50% to shield break, and a leg/arm/head hit costing >= 50% of HP CHOPS IT OFF; PIERCE removes 15% of protection before (Armor-Piercing 50%; both 65%). Two-handed: 125% of Strength. A weapon with two types picks one per attack at random. UNDERWATER: slash and blunt take an attack penalty equal to their length, pierce none (half if mixed), flails -1 more.
- Damage numbers colored by kind (bleeding dark red, poison green).
- HARASSED: -1 defense per attack received, decaying by a share; only directed MELEE attacks; a multi-attack weapon harasses once per attack; against a rider only the one attacked.
- Fatigue per attack = encumbrance; -1 def per 10, -1 att per 20; 100 unconscious (recover 5/turn), 200+ hurts.
- REPEL: a defender with a LONGER weapon makes a repel attack when attacked; on a hit the attacker makes a morale check (morale + DRN - length difference vs 10 + DRN + half the margin) or ABORTS; if he passes he attacks anyway but takes 1 damage if the repel did damage. Low morale troops and claws/bites (length 0) are easier to repel. Each repel gives a lingering -2 to repel again (fast attacks get through). Giants' weapons are 1 longer.
- MULTIPLE ATTACKS may hit different targets in reach; a flail's extra attacks hit the same target, wasted if the first kills.
- MULTIPLE WEAPONS: attack reduced by the SUM OF THE WEAPONS' LENGTHS (two daggers easy, two swords hard); Ambidextrous reduces it; intrinsic BONUS weapons (a bite) add no penalty; +1 encumbrance per extra wielded weapon.
- MELEE RESOLUTION ORDER, written once: target -> early strikeback (Awe, petrifying gaze; attacker immobilized = abort) -> repel -> strikeback (Slimer, Sight Vengeance, Horror Mark Attacker; immobilized = abort) -> attack/defense -> damage/protection (+shield) -> defensive abilities (Mirror Image, Protective Force, Luck, Mossbody) -> LIMB CAP (a hit on an arm or leg does at most half max HP) -> damage redistribution (Damage Reversal, Blood Bond, Blood Vengeance; killing the attacker here doesn't stop the attack) -> deal damage to target and shield.
- DANCING WEAPONS (items): float and attack nearby enemies on their own, no penalty or fatigue; harass; can't be repelled but repel for their owner; repel bonus per attack over 2.
- MOUNTED: rider and mount are two units; mount targeted 25/50/75% if 3/4/5+ sizes bigger, else missiles 50%, or if mount is 2 bigger than reach, else 30% + 10%*mount size - 10%*reach (min 10%). AoE and lightning hit both; trample hits the mount. Magic saddle/barding; some rider items extend to the mount. Two-handed weapon mounted -3 attack. SKILLED RIDER raises the mount's morale and defense (less in heavy barding). Fear hits both: mount broken = rider thrown and fights on; FALL damage = an open roll of the size difference, armor-negating (none from a chariot). Riders who lose mounts go home (morale checks on the way, may desert) and are re-recruited at half cost KEEPING their identity, XP and afflictions; Smart Mounts do the same.
- MISSILES: no defense roll; Precision picks the square; deviation beyond Precision/2 - 2; a missile hits whoever is in the square it lands on, friend or foe, the larger more often; hit roll DRN + (size points in square)/2 (+2 magic) vs DRN + shield parry x2 - fatigue/20: crowded, big and tired targets get hit. Precision above 10 counts double. Lightning armor-negating, Fire armor-piercing.
## p66-68 Special damage (mostly in dominions-rules.md; new details only)
- Resistance = flat N off, then 2N% of the rest (50 = immune); double vs fatigue damage.
- CATCHING FIRE: 4% per point of fire damage before protection (fire fatigue counts 1/3). BURNING = 1d(size)/2 a turn: BIGGER creatures burn harder; halved at FR 5. Goes out at 25% + FR + 5 x Cold scale + 100% IF RAINING; heat scale counts against. Can't burn: FR 10+, chill aura, mistform, ethereal.
- FREEZING: +2d6 fatigue a turn; thaw 25% + CR + 5 x Heat scale (cold weather slows it). Can't freeze: CR 10+, heat aura, ethereal.
- PROFUSE BLEEDING: 10 fatigue + HP/20 a round; stops 10% + regeneration (half underwater).
- POISON: total computed once, 10% of what remains each round; while it remains, attack and defense -25% per full-HP dose (max 50%), falling as it drains. Resistance only lowers the initial total.
- SHOCK stuns: 5% + half the % of HP the hit took.
- ACID -> RUST (4% per point, iron gear only): rusty armor may be DAMAGED by later hits (2% per point); rusty weapons 25% chance of being damaged on a hit (-2 damage, blunt -1).
- LIFE DRAIN: heals damage/2 and removes damage x2 fatigue; partial drain = only the first 5 points; HP can't exceed 150% max + 10; vs Lifeless only 25% damage and no healing.
- PARALYSIS: rounds = (paralysis damage - victim's size)/2; a second paralysis keeps the longer duration and deals min(old, new)/2 damage (max 5).
- False damage: purple numbers; kills when real + false reach HP; no afflictions; Damage Reversal and Blood Bond ignore it; nothing heals it; fades 2 a round once enemy Glamour mages are dead.
- Cloud tables (frost, heat, poison, disease, sulphur, rust, death; also sleep etc. elsewhere); clouds of a type don't overlap, they spread or raise a square by 1 at most; armor-negating; ~1 level a round fade.
## p69-73 MORALE AND ROUT (not read in earlier notes), afflictions, sieges, weather, retreats
- Squad morale = AVERAGE of its members. Bonuses: +1 friendly dominion, +1 home province, commander's leadership bonus (by formation and squad count), +1 per Inspirational level (can be negative), the best Standard in the squad, sacred blessed +1.
- MAGIC MORALE BONUS -10..+1: fear lowers it, Sermon of Courage / Fanaticism raise it; a negative bonus recovers +1 about every 2 rounds; a positive one lasts the battle.
- A SQUAD CHECKS MORALE when: heavy losses since its last check AND 20%+ casualties overall; 4 or fewer members left and one was damaged this round; near a Fear monster; hit by a Fear spell (not Frighten); its army has lost 50%+ of total HP (then every turn). At most ONE check a turn. "Heavy losses" = one WOUND per two members; a wound = damage that leaves a unit at 80% HP or less ("units are very enthusiastic at the beginning of battle, but less so after taking damage").
- ARMY ROUT automatically at 75% of its total HP lost; mounts count 25% of their HP, province defense 25% ("expected to run"), slaves 50%.
- MORALE CHECK: squad morale + DRN + SURVIVOR BONUS (0-5 by the share of original members alive) vs 14 + DRN.
- ROUT: routed units spend everything fleeing to their own edge, -4 defense; fast units pick them off; once off the map they appear in an adjacent province (enemy province = destroyed); bleeding/poison/burning they carry is applied in full after they escape ("expiring of its wounds before finding help"). A winner's squads can rout too.
- MINDLESS DISSOLUTION: no eligible commander left -> immobile, 33% a turn to vanish, still strike adjacent.
- Magic FEAR (morale up to -10, the whole squad checks at the round's end, a unit may break alone on an easy check) vs FRIGHTEN (up to -5, no check). Breaking alone happens only from magic fear.
- LONG BATTLES: turn 100 TWILIGHT (every enchantment and temporary magic and berserking ends; -2 prec, +1 Glamour), 150 NIGHTFALL (attackers rout; darkness -3), 170 defenders rout, 200 everyone left dies. (A night battle gets daybreak instead.)
- AFFLICTIONS: chance = % of HP lost to the blow; over 100% = one sure + the rest as a chance; MAJOR chance = chance/1.5 (max 33%); by body part: Any: Battle Fright, Profuse Bleeding (minor); Head: Eye Loss, Mute (minor) / Dementia, Feeblemindedness, Blindness (major); Chest: Chest Wound (minor) / Diseased, Never-Healing Wound (major); Arm: Weakened / Lost an arm; Leg: Limp / Crippled. Only Profuse Bleeding passes (ends during or after the battle). LIMP: commander -4 map move; a soldier whose army marches 2+ provinces in a month: 25% to become Crippled. CRIPPLED: commander -75% map move; a soldier dies 35% when marching (70% if 2+ provinces).
- SIEGES: reduction strength = unit strength squared (fliers x2); repair = squared/2 (fliers x2, mindless 1/8, animals 1/2, undisciplined 1/2); the difference wears or repairs the walls ("the enemy is repairing the walls faster than we can destroy them. We need more men"). The BESIEGER can't see how close the walls are (hints only); the defender can. Unbesieged damaged forts repair fully. Siege supply = storage / turn of siege (300, 150, 100, 75...); starving two turns -> disease. Storming happens after other battles in the province.
- WEATHER: clear by default; rain or snow at random (not in wastes or caves); snow only if Cold >= 1 (Cold x 15%), rain only if Heat >= 0 (Growth x 10% + 10%); global enchantments change it. Assassination fights are often indoors (no weather) and 50% at night.
- RETREATS: each commander 75% to retreat "smart" (into the fort, or a friendly neighbour); IN NATIVE TERRAIN a second chance at 50%; not smart = a random neighbour (an enemy province kills him). Troops follow their leader on a morale check (squad bonus doubled; undisciplined -3; skirmish -1); leaderless or failing troops check alone (50%). In a castle siege: smart retreaters hide deeper and reappear if the battle is won, all die if it is lost; commanders always smart.
- Battle summary: kills per unit type; mounted shown riders killed + riders left unmounted.
- 2d12 has NO morale or rout rule for NPCs (grep of Chapters 2 and 14: nothing).
## p74-82 Magic chapter re-read: only what earlier notes lack
- Schools "don't categorize rigorously": Akashic Knowledge is a Conjuration spell that only searches for gem sites; Will o' the Wisp is Conjuration 5 but only Fire 1.
- Two-path spells use gems of the PRIMARY path.
- Indirect magic at level 1+: leadership kinds (Fire also ordinary leadership +10/level; Nature supplies +10/level; Fire shorter life, Nature longer life); the level 3 and 4 perks as in dominions-paths 3.4.
- Battle spells needing gems: the gems must be in the mage's inventory BEFORE the battle.
- LOCAL ENCHANTMENTS (rituals on a province, e.g. Dome of Air): last until dispelled, the caster dies, or (most) the province is conquered; some have a limited duration lengthened by extra gems (1 month per gem, some 3). Rituals can be set to recast each month.
- Globals: the max gems a mage can put into a ritual = path level x 100; extra gems make it harder to dispel but "don't protect mages against assassination" ("easier to assassinate him than to try to dispel the massive global spell he had put 200 extra gems into"); an immortal's globals end when it dies; some globals are TIED TO A PROVINCE (lost when it's conquered). Replacing: same-name global contests that one; else empty slot; else a RANDOM active global, possibly your own: "You can accidentally dispel your own spells. These are unruly magicks." Mages cast in random order across all nations.
- DOMES protect a province against globals (and rituals) except their strikeback part against a global's caster. Many globals work only (or more) inside the caster's dominion: pushing back the enemy dominion protects you.
- GRAND COMMUNION: mages with the ability join a ritual or a dispel, each adding his own path level to its strength (example: 2 Theurgs at 2 + 4 Acolytes at 1 = +8).
- Communion details: slave's share x1/2 if he's stronger than the master, x2 if weaker, x4 if under half; the master's gained levels count when computing the cost; one communion of each kind at once; CHORUS slaves drop out when unconscious (no further damage); BACKLASH when all masters die: slaves stunned ~a round + 3d50 fatigue; items (Crystal Matrix...) let any mage join without casting the slave spell.
- RESEARCH: 5 + 2 x magic levels (min 1); halved by Dementia; Magic scale helps ("extra vigor to study all night"), Drain hurts ("sleepy"); PHILOSOPHERS research with no magic and like Sloth scales ("lying about and thinking"); Divine Insights monks research too, but only as many per lab as the province's dominion strength.
- LEGENDARY spells (research 9): you pick ONE to research; the others are very expensive afterwards.
- ARTIFACTS (Construction 9): unique; they YEARN to be forged once within reach (a nation reaches Construction 9, Forge of the Ancients, the Thrones of Creation or the Artificer: +50% a month each); a yearning artifact costs half. Some items are restricted to a nation, some discounted for one.
- Smite and Banishment replacement table (as dominions-paths 3.3); the pretender's highest path at 4+ decides; ties by a fixed order.
- ALCHEMY: only into Astral or out of Astral, 2:1 (so 4:1 between two non-Astral types); only where there's a lab. Earth/Fire Alteration rituals turn gems into gold.
- DOMINION is not military control: conquering doesn't spread it. "You don't know who believes what in a province unless you have someone close enough to find out."
## p83-86 DOMINION (not read in earlier notes)
- Max dominion = the god's starting dominion + 1 per (5 x players on the team) temples. Only one god's dominion per province: -3 to you is -3 to all but its owner.
- Four SOURCES radiate it: the pretender (one automatic increase + two checks), the capital (one check), each temple (one), the prophet (one); disciples and claimed Thrones too. Chance a check works = 50% + 5% x max dominion.
- A "TEMPLE CHECK": neutral land -> +1; friendly land -> +1 at 30% - 3% x current level, otherwise it passes to a random neighbour (so faith spreads far through friendly land); enemy land -> -1 to the enemy at 50% + 5% x max - 5% x the enemy's level. High-dominion provinces make a "WALL OF FAITH". Spread to or from the sea is halved.
- PROPHET: priest 3 (or +1 if already 3+), +2 Att/Def/Prec, a temple's check, HP/Str/MR by dominion like the god.
- THRONES OF ASCENSION: sites claimed only by the god, the prophet or a level 3 priest; each spreads 1-7 checks.
- Mictlan spreads dominion ONLY by blood sacrifice (one check per slave, up to the priest's level, in a temple).
- PREACHING: 30% x priest level (+1/2 level with a temple) - 5% x enemy dominion (min 5% vs enemy); can only raise dominion to TWICE the priest's level ("Preaching cannot be used to raise the dominion of this province any higher"; the game won't warn you later when a priest stops being useful). INQUISITORS count double level in enemy dominion. HERETICS lower ANY god's dominion (20% x ability), yours too; some are stealthy, sent into enemy lands.
- Effects: +1 morale in friendly dominion, -1 in enemy; the god and prophet stronger at home, weaker abroad.
- Scales drift toward the god's each turn: 5% x dominion + 10% x the gap (big gaps move fast).
- EXTREME SCALES (4+) have a price even on the "good" side: Order -> no creativity (research -2/-4); Productivity -> people get unruly (unrest); Heat/Cold -> population dies; Death -> forests and farms become plains, plains become wastes; Growth -> plains become forest or farm, seas kelp; Fortune -> income and resources DOWN; Magic -> horrors thrive, horror marks appear, unrest. Extreme scales spread into neighbours, even other gods' lands.
- SEASONS: summer one step warmer, winter one step colder; caves and other planes have no seasons; deep seas always neutral.
## p87-89 Dominion victory, special dominions
- DOMINION VICTORY, "the Tinkerbell Effect: if no one believes in you, you cease to exist": a god with no friendly dominion anywhere is eliminated; besiege his last fort and preach his faith away instead of storming it.
