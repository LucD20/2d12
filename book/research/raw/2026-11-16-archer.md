# The archer / ranged martial in other games (raw report, 2026-11-16)

**The question.** Before the Archer's redesign: what do other games, and their players, say about ranged martial classes? What makes a shooter boring or fun at a tactical table, how games make the map matter for one, the mark on one quarry, what a shooter does besides damage, the designers' positions on ranged damage and reload, and burst for a shooter.

**How far to trust this.** Other games' rules and other people's opinions, never 2d12's rules.
- **[read]** = rules text I read in full myself, in the file or on the page named.
- **[checked]** = an opinion I read on its page (poster and date given when the page shows them).
- **[lead]** = seen only in a search tool's summary; the page was not opened. Not to be quoted to anyone before it is opened.
- Not opened on purpose: Reddit, Giant in the Playground, RPG.net (they refuse automated readers).
- Already in `book/research/` and not repeated here: Favored Enemy, 5e's Hunter's Mark, "the player declares the target" (`divine-profane.md`); splitting movement and the safe step (`movement-positioning.md`); marks on a defender (`paladins-other-games.md`); nova sizes and finishers (`nova-burst.md`).

**Sources of the rules text.** Draw Steel as markdown (github.com/SteelCompendium/data-rules-md, `Kits/`, `Abilities/`, `Draw Steel Heroes.md`); Pathfinder 2e remaster data (github.com/foundryvtt/pf2e, `packs/pf2e/feats`, `class-features`, `actions`, `static/lang/en.json` for weapon traits); the 4e Compendium crawl (github.com/toddcblank/4eitems, `crawled/`, file names given); 13th Age SRD (github.com/13vaults/13vaults.com, `content/1e/classes/ranger.mdx`, `rules/combat-rules.mdx`); Lancer (github.com/massif-press/lancer-data, `lib/talents.json`, `actions.json`, `frames.json`, `tags.json`, `statuses.json`); ICON 1.5 (github.com/zengulus/icon, `src/content/generated/icon-1.5.json`, page numbers given).

## 1. What makes a ranged martial boring or fun at a tactical table

### 1.1 The complaints, in players' words

**"Stand in one spot and shoot" / a fixed rotation.**
- [checked] Paizo forum, "Keeping Archery Interesting" (Pathfinder 1), Reynard_the_fox, Oct 2, 2013: "Casters have to consider what spell to cast, and melee types have lots of positioning and potentially combat maneuvers to consider; but it seems to me that in combat, archers can simply maintain distance from the enemy and full attack nonstop. [...] I feel like combat would become somewhat repetitive pretty quickly." Whisperknives, same day: "if you are the fighter of the group you may end up just being an artillery platform." Duiker, same day: the fix is "switching from the mindset that a high level archer's job is machine gunning for massive DPS." https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2q7z6
- [checked] Paizo forum, "Are rangers weak?" (PF2e remaster), Claxon, May 29, 2024: "Honestly flurry rangers aren't fun or good unless you only want to stand in one spot and shoot arrows, which is an incredibly boring play style to me. It can potentially deal a lot of damage, but needing to do anything other than shoot arrows craters your damage output." Nicolas Paradise, same day: "The precision ranger in my game out damages everyone including the fighter because he doesn't have to close melee. I think Rangers are incredibly potent and consistent. However they are very boring and rotation dependent. Like every fight starts, Gravity Weapon > Hunt Prey > Hunted Shot Every following round is Hunt Prey(if initial Prey is dead) > Hunted Shot > Command Animal Companion or move if needed. Repeat until there are no more enemies." https://paizo.com/threads/rzs4gy6v
- [checked] Paizo forum, "How does the ranger cope with having to switch prey so much?", Gortle, Mar 20, 2020, stating the stationary play as the *right* play: "a ranged character should not be moving every round. Its one of the advantages of ranged - you pick your spot and make them move." https://paizo.com/threads/rzs42zdj&page=2

**One best button (4e Twin Strike).**
- [read] Twin Strike (power-87, PHB p.105): "At-Will · Martial, Weapon. Standard Action. Melee or Ranged weapon. Requirement: You must be wielding two melee weapons or a ranged weapon. Targets: One or two creatures. Attack: Strength vs. AC (melee; main weapon and off-hand weapon) or Dexterity vs. AC (ranged), two attacks. Hit: 1[W] damage per attack." Two rolls, no modifier to damage; each hit can still carry Hunter's Quarry and every flat bonus.
- [checked] EN World, "No other Ranged Weapon Class, or How I loathe the ranger", Rechan, Jan 10, 2009 (#1): "I have seen about seven rangers in action, in many different groups. Every. Single. Ranger. Uses. Twin-Strike. Almost. Every. Round. I hate it. I hate rangers. They're so boring. There's no variety. No choices, no varied tactics, regardless of build just 'hit it twice over and over with my one good At-Will'. The other At-Will might as well not exist." chitzk0i (#3): "even when he uses an encounter or daily, he's getting twin strike with a bonus." cjais (#9): "I agree completely that the ranger is boring to watch in play, but forcing him to use the other at-wills would make him less effective, and thus less attractive to play." Dausuul (#38): "Everything sucks compared to Twin Strike. [...] So, instead of nerfing Twin Strike, why not beef up the ranger's other at-wills to make them more attractive?" https://www.enworld.org/threads/no-other-ranged-weapon-class-or-how-i-loathe-the-ranger.248420/
- [checked] EN World, "Ranger Balance", Stalker0, Jun 27, 2009 (#3): "My group considers the Bow ranger the most powerful class in 4e by far. Its also one of the most boring in our opinion." https://www.enworld.org/threads/ranger-balance.258337/
- [checked] EN World, "Ranger At-Wills, no choice?", JesperKT, Apr 26, 2009 (#15): "My archer ranger finds Nimble Strike useful from time to time, but Twin Strike is the damage dealer, no questions asked." https://www.enworld.org/goto/post?id=4768599
- What the same posters *liked* in the 4e ranger: the move-and-shoot at-wills. Rechan (#10): "I like Hit And Run/Nimble Strike. They are basic attacks, yes, but they also let you move. Very helpful for 'get the hell out of dodge' or, 'Hit this guy and then run over there to threaten that one'." jensun (#39): "he uses Nimble Strike or Evasive strike to be able to attack without provoking an OA and then use his move action to get clear of the melee."

**"The safest and the highest damage at once" (5e 2014).**
- [checked] DM David (blog), "Sharpshooters Are the Worst Thing in D&D, but That Speaks Well of Fifth Edition" (the page read is the tag page; the post's date is not shown on it): "When you attack from a distance, melee attackers can't hit you. You can fire past obstacles that hamper movement. You can switch targets without having to move." "Ranged attacks suffer drawbacks: Targets can gain cover. If foes move next to you, then your attacks suffer disadvantage and you stop being immune to their melee attacks. Fortunately for ranged attackers, the game's two most broken feats dismantle all these disadvantages. With Sharpshooter, you ignore cover. With Crossbow Expert, you can make ranged attacks while within 5 feet of enemies without suffering disadvantage." "D&D's designers seem to think ranged and melee attacks represent two different, but mostly equal styles, when really, ranged attacks offer massive intrinsic advantages." "In practice, sharpshooters gain 10 damage for a mere -3 attack penalty." "the D&D design collapses the options for martial characters to two: (1) pick Sharpshooter or (2) pick something plainly weaker." He quotes DM Thomas Christy: "I have actually had players complain in game and out about how it seemed like they did not need to be there." https://dmdavid.com/tag/sharpshooters-are-the-worst-thing-in-dd-but-that-speaks-well-of-fifth-edition/
- [checked] EN World, "Evaluating Range versus Damage (SS vs GWM) - putting a price on range", CapnZapp, Sep 24, 2017 (#11): "The game severely, nay critically, undercosts the quality of range." Ancalagon (#12): "5e, by simplifying things, removed too many of the obstacles that make ranged fire difficult." https://www.enworld.org/goto/post?id=7234100
- [read] The 5e (2014 SRD) rules those feats switch off: "Ranged Attacks in Close Combat. [...] you have disadvantage on the attack roll if you are within 5 feet of a hostile creature who can see you and who isn't incapacitated." "Your attack roll has disadvantage when your target is beyond normal range". Half cover "+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws", three-quarters "+5". Archery fighting style: "You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with ranged weapons". (5thsrd.org: combat/making_an_attack, combat/cover, classes/fighter.)
- [checked, guide] The 2024 change (Arcane Eye's guide, quoting the 2024 feat's parts): "The 2024 Player's Handbook has removed the clause that you can take a -5 penalty to your attack roll for +10 damage." The feat keeps "Bypass Cover: Your ranged weapon attacks ignore Half Cover and Three-Quarters Cover" and "Firing in Melee" (no Disadvantage with an enemy within 5 feet) and long range without Disadvantage. So 2024 removed the damage, and kept the three erasers of the map. https://arcaneeye.com/dnd-feat-guides/sharpshooter-5e/ . The 2024 feat's own text was not read (not in the SRD 5.2.1). [lead] An EN World poster on the 2024 version: "ranged combatants continue to have an edge over melee combatants."

**The opposite complaint: ranged is weak and the map is too small (PF2e).**
- [checked] Paizo forum, "Is being an archer a trap?", May 5-6, 2021. Gortle: "Volley sucks. Another problem is all the lesser cover bonuses that the GM will hand out. [...] Personally I think Paizo have deliberately weakened all ranged attacks to encourage melee, as melee is considered more fun." Zapp: "in official material, the monsters are seldom more than one turn's movement away from reaching you. [...] Pathfinder just isn't the kind of game where you can afford the luxury of staying at range, because that means one fewer hero is there to soak the melee damage." Watery Soup (2 favorites): "How valuable ranged weapons are depends on your GM too. If your GM is constantly springing enemies in your face with lots of cover, it's different than if your GM lets you start combat at 500' across an open field." SuperBidi (5 favorites): "Another advantage of being a ranged character is that you force enemies to come to you." Exocist, with numbers: at level 8, "3d6+2 (12.5) compared to 2d12+1d6+6 (22.5) max that melee does". https://paizo.com/threads/rzs43djg
- [checked] The same thread shows the loop players *do* enjoy on a PF2e shooter: Mathmuse (2 favorites): "she Hides behind a crate, pillar, low wall, tree, or underbrush and then shoots with her shortbow. This makes her opponent flat-footed and gives her sneak attack damage on the first shot. She often Hides again as her 3rd action".

### 1.2 What each game did about it (rules text)

- **Draw Steel** [read]: there is no archer class. A shooter is a **kit** put on a martial class ([lead] the kits chapter names censors, furies, shadows, tacticians and troubadours as the kit users; I read the kits, the Shadow's and the Tactician's abilities, not that list). The ranged kits: Rapid-Fire, Sniper, Arcane Archer, and the mixed Ranger and Cloak and Dagger. Each kit gives flat bonuses and one **signature ability**; the class supplies everything else.
  - *Sniper* (`Kits/Sniper.md`): "You wear no armor and wield a bow." Speed +1, Ranged Damage +0/+0/+4, Ranged Distance +10, Disengage +1. Signature, **Patient Shot**: "Ranged, Strike, Weapon · Main action · Ranged 15 · One creature. Power Roll + Might or Agility: ≤11: 3 + M or A damage; 12-16: 6 + M or A damage; 17+: 13 + M or A damage. Effect: If you don't take a move action this turn, this strike deals extra damage equal to your Might or Agility score (your choice)." (The one kit that **pays you for standing still**.)
  - *Rapid-Fire*: Stamina +3 per echelon, Speed +1, Ranged Damage +2/+2/+2, Distance +7, Disengage +1. Signature, **Two Shot**: "Ranged 12 · Two creatures or objects. ≤11: 4 damage; 12-16: 6 damage; 17+: 8 damage."
  - *Ranger* kit, **Hamstring Shot**: "Ranged 10 · One creature. ≤11: 3 + M or A damage; A < WEAK, slowed (save ends) / 12-16: 5 + M or A damage; A < AVERAGE, slowed (save ends) / 17+: 7 + M or A damage; A < STRONG, slowed (save ends)."
  - *Cloak and Dagger*, **Fade**: "Melee 1 or ranged 10. ≤11: 3 + M or A damage; you can shift 1 square / 12-16: 6 + M or A damage; you can shift up to 2 squares / 17+: 8 + M or A damage; you can shift up to 3 squares."
  - *Arcane Archer*, **Exploding Arrow**: Ranged 15, 5/7/10 + characteristic fire damage; "Effect: One creature or object of your choice within 2 squares of the target takes fire damage equal to the characteristic score used for this ability's power roll."
  - The one general ranged rule: "If you make a ranged strike while any enemy is adjacent to you (within 1 square), you have a bane on the strike's power roll." (`Draw Steel Heroes.md`, Distance: Ranged.)
- **Pathfinder 2e** [read]: lower base damage for ranged (section 5), the volley trait, reload as actions, lesser cover from any creature in the line (section 2), and a ranger whose best ranged feats are "two shots in one action against your prey" (Hunted Shot) or "one accurate shot for two actions" (Hunter's Aim). Texts in sections 3 and 4.
- **D&D 4e** [read]: the striker ranger (Twin Strike + Hunter's Quarry + Prime Shot), then two later tries at an archer who is *not* a damage machine: the Seeker (PHB3, a primal controller with a bow) and the Essentials Hunter (a martial/primal controller whose at-wills are riders on a basic attack). Texts in section 4. Prime Shot (class-5): "If none of your allies are nearer to your target than you are, you receive a +1 bonus to ranged attack rolls against that target." (A bonus for being the *closest*, not the farthest.) The alternative feature, Running Attack: "If you use a standard action that lets you move [...] and you end that movement at least 2 squares away from where you began that move, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls made as part of that standard action."
- **13th Age** [read] (`ranger.mdx`): no grid, so no map answer. *Archery*: "Once per battle, reroll one of your missed ranged attacks." *Double Ranged Attack*: "Your weapon damage die drops one notch, usually from d8s to d6s. If your first attack is a natural even roll (hit or miss), you can make a second attack as a free action." (Epic feat: "You can also use your move action in between your two attacks if you wish.") *Lethal Hunter*: "Once per battle as a free action, choose an enemy. The crit range of your attacks against that enemy expands by 2 for the rest of the battle." *First Strike*: "The first time you attack an enemy during a battle, your crit range for that attack expands by 2". No reception was read: Pelgrane's build page refused (403) and the Chamber of Mazarbul blog was not opened; [lead] Pelgrane calls the ranger "one of the simplest classes to build and play".
- **Lancer** [read]: ranged is the default; the interest is put in the **weapon tags and the talents**, not in a class. *Ordnance* tag: "This weapon can only be fired before the user moves or takes any other actions on their turn, excepting Protocols. The user can still act and move normally after attacking. Additionally, because of its size, this weapon can't be used against targets in engagement with the user's mech, and cannot be used for Overwatch." *Loading*: "This weapon must be reloaded after each use. Mechs can reload with Stabilize and some systems." The Anti-Materiel Rifle is Range 20, 2d6 kinetic, Accurate, AP, **Loading, Ordnance** (it must be reloaded after each shot, and it is fired before anything else in the turn; then you may move). *Engaged*: "Ranged attacks made by an ENGAGED character receive +1 difficulty." Talents and frames in sections 2, 4 and 6.
- **ICON 1.5** [read]: no bow job as such. The Vagabond class (p.145) holds the shooters: "They know how to aim a crossbow bolt through the helmet slit of a knight [...] Vagabonds boast high damage and even higher mobility." Class traits: "Skirmisher: A character with this trait can move diagonally and dash at full speed. Dodge: Immune to all damage from missed attacks, successful saves, and area effects. [...] Finesse: You deal bonus damage to bloodied foes." Basic Attack: Range 4. Its gun/bow job, the **Freelancer** (p.153-155): "Freelancers are expert tempo fighters that prefer to fight around a certain range band [...] Attacking characters at exactly range 3 allows freelancers and their bound seraph to lay down withering punishment." Trait *Trigrammaton*: "Your abilities used against foes at exactly range 3 gain +1 boon on attack rolls and unerring." *Aether Shot*: "Any attack made on the third and sixth round of combat deals bonus damage and triggers all exceed effects, hit or miss." *Astral Binding*: "You can stack up to two marks on characters. As a free action, you can teleport all characters marked by you 1 space." (A **sweet-spot range** instead of a maximum range, and a way to nudge marked foes into it.)
- **Gloomhaven**: not read. [lead] The Doomstalker is a ranged class built on a mark ("Doom") with "several [cards] at range 5 and 6".

### 2.2 What players liked or found fiddly

- **Cover from allies makes the PF2e archer move, and players accept it.** [checked] Paizo forum, "Archer cover issues", Oct 9, 2020. Atalius: "One issue I had to deal with was consistent lesser cover when shooting at my target we had two front line martials always in melee with the enemy. [...] do I save a move action every round to just get into position and continue focus firing?" jdripley (1 favorite): "Moving out to the side so you have clear line of sight is good. I know it feels bad not to attack as much as possible as a flurry ranger, but sometimes one turn of setup pays dividends throughout the rest of the battle." OrochiFuror: "get some higher ground. Up a tree, on some large rocks, a balcony or anything to get a clear shot at all your targets." Tender Tendrils: "you are generally better off using 1 action to set up for your attacks and then making 2 attack actions than you are just standing and making 3 attack actions." https://paizo.com/threads/rzs437em . It works there because the third attack is nearly worthless (the multiple attack penalty), so the action spent moving costs little.
- **A penalty for being close is disliked when it reads as nonsense (volley).** [checked] Paizo playtest forum, "Why is Volley?", redpandamage, Aug 2, 2018 (15 favorites): "Volley is one of my top 5 gripes with the new rules and should be eliminated as soon as possible. It makes no sense, nerfs longbows for no reason, and makes rangers unable to fully utilize their most iconic weapon." https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2v9vo . "Volley: Help Me Understand It?", Igor Horvat, Aug 20, 2019: "Penalty to accuracy for closer target? How did they logic their way out of that one?" Unicore (5 favorites), defending: "The point of volley is to give ranged weapons more ways to be different from each other in a way to create mechanically interesting variations. I don't think reality was as important as ways to create balance." N N 959: "It creates design space for making some classes better or worse at ranged combat." https://paizo.com/threads/rzs42q16
- **No "firing into melee" penalty is welcomed as one feat tax less.** [checked] Paizo forum, Oct 31, 2024: HammerJack (2 favorites): "There is no such penalty in 2e, so you don't need anything to avoid it." Soapbox: "Excellent! No feat-tax!" Tarlane (4 favorites): "Firing through a creature's square(ally or enemy) gives anything behind it lesser cover [...] But that can be avoided by lining up your shots without something in between." https://paizo.com/threads/rzs5d2ua
- **The GM's map decides.** [checked] DM David (as above): "Start by adding total cover to your encounters, and then play creatures with the good sense to duck between their turns." "Battles with movement and cover tend to play to the strengths of melee characters." "One caution: Newer players can find foes that duck behind total cover frustrating." [checked] EN World, "Ranger Balance", Nifft, Jun 28, 2009 (#5): "If he can target everyone on the battlefield, though, it may be that you're using boring terrain. Consider using ranged enemies behind cover (ramparts, arrow slits in a wall, a thicket of trees, or whatever -- stuff you need to walk around before you can target them effectively)." [checked] Robert Babaev, "LANCER: The GM's Guide To Building Encounters", Jul 22, 2023: "Make sightlines and cover so that your sitrep, and some of your players, have a chance to shine." https://owacsender.substack.com/p/the-gms-guide-to-building-lancer
- **"Nearest enemy" needs a tie-break.** [checked] EN World, "'Nearest enemy'?", Nov 26, 2008: CapnZapp asks who is nearest when three orcs are equally near; Rafe (#6) quotes the 4e PHB p.273: "When two or more squares or creatures are equally close, you can pick either one as the nearest." https://www.enworld.org/threads/nearest-enemy.245805/ . [lead] A Wizards customer-service answer relayed on EN World says the restriction is "intended to prevent the ranger from going straight for the squishies in the back, and also to promote some tactical mobility".
- **Aim and overwatch: reception not found.** No opened page gave players' verdicts on Lancer's Overwatch, Crack Shot's "steady your aim", or XCOM-style overwatch at a table (Lancer's talk is on Reddit and Discord). See "Not found".

## 3. The mark on one quarry, for a shooter (what `divine-profane.md` lacks)

### 3.1 Rules text [read]

- **4e Hunter's Quarry** (class-5, PHB p.103, with its errata): "Once per turn, you can use your Hunter's Quarry power. At-Will · Minor Action. Effect: You can designate the nearest enemy to you that you can see as your quarry. Once per round, when you hit your quarry with an attack, the attack deals extra damage based on your level. If you can make multiple attacks in a round, you decide which attack to apply the extra damage to after all the attacks are rolled. [...] The hunter's quarry effect remains active until the end of the encounter, until the quarry is defeated, or until you designate a different target as your quarry. You can designate one enemy as your quarry at a time." Extra damage +1d6 / +2d6 / +3d6 by tier. A daily that builds on it, *Marked for Death* (Ranger 9): "Target: One creature designated as your quarry [...] Hit: 3[W] + [...] modifier damage [...] Until the end of the encounter, your Hunter's Quarry deals two extra dice of Hunter's Quarry damage against the target."
- **PF2e Hunt Prey** (Player Core): "[one-action] (concentrate, ranger). You designate a single creature as your prey and focus your attacks against that creature. You must be able to see or hear the prey, or you must be tracking the prey during exploration. ## 5. Ranged damage math and reload

### 5.1 Positions [read] and [checked]

- **PF2e, in the rules** [read]: propulsive: "You add half your Strength modifier (if positive) to damage rolls with a propulsive ranged weapon." (Melee adds all of it; a plain bow adds none.) GM Core, building creatures, Strike Damage: "A creature that's meant to be primarily a melee threat uses high damage for its melee Strikes, or moderate for melee Strikes that have the agile trait. Ranged attacks more typically use the moderate value, or even low." (Archives of Nethys, Rules ID 2897.) Weapon dice were not read from the data; the players below give d6 (shortbow), d8 (longbow, with volley) against d12.
- **No designer sentence "ranged is safer, so it deals less" was found** on the pages opened (searched: Paizo blog, Mark Seifter). Players state it as the known intent: [checked] Paizo forum, "Is being an archer a trap?", May 5-6, 2021. Kyrone (7 favorites): "Damage is indeed lower, you have the benefit of range after all". Claxon (4 favorites): "Damage is intentionally lower, because you can deal damage from range, meaning not needing to spend actions to position yourself as often, and not putting yourself in direct harms way." graystone: "Ranged PC's don't have to move as much and can target creatures melee can't [like flying]. As such, you'll often get more attacks than melee does." pauljathome: "They do less damage than a melee combatant but not that much less and they do it at range."
- **How much, in PF2e** [checked, a poster's numbers]: Exocist, same thread: at low levels "doing 1d6+0 damage is pretty bad" ("3.5 compared to 10.5"); "By level 8 [...] 3d6+2 (12.5) compared to 2d12+1d6+6 (22.5) max that melee does, you're still significantly behind"; "By level 16, I wouldn't want to be a melee character any more, so many monsters just have abilities that say 'if you're melee, get rekt loser', and the damage differential is even closer". So, on his numbers, about a third at the start, a bit over half at level 8, closing after.
- **The gap closes when a class adds a flat rider to every hit.** [checked] Paizo forum, "Post Core classes are much stronger ranged than melee", CaffeinatedNinja, Apr 30, 2022 (7 favorites): "All these classes have big damage boosters on their main attacks (Spellstrike, Strategic Strike, and Overdrive) This means that the advantage of higher hit die and +str on melee weapons is badly diluted. In other words, ranged does almost as much damage as melee." And May 2, 2022 (3 favorites): "combine that with little to no damage penalty for going ranged, why go into melee?" https://paizo.com/threads/rzs43m26 (This is the same shape as a quarry's bonus damage on a low-die bow.)
- **Paizo's other position: make ranged the norm and say so.** [checked, quoted on the Paizo forum by Hiruma Kai, Aug 5, 2024, from Starfinder 2e Field Test #5] "in Starfinder Second Edition, we're working hard to create a 'ranged' playstyle—the assumption is that most combatants rely on ranged abilities and Strikes." Guntermench quotes the Operative sidebar: "a lot of combats happen at a distance using cover and tactical positioning, rather than up close and personal like in Pathfinder Second Edition." Exocist's warning (3 favorites): "There are many things that reduce ranged damage - Cover, lack of stat, (general) lack of reaction attack access [...], no easy to inflict FF due to flanking. If you use PF2e assumptions about ranged damage, in a game where ranged combat is the expectation, without updating HP values to compensate, you're going to end up with a slog". https://paizo.com/threads/rzs4uwi7
- **4e**: the ranger is the top striker *because* Twin Strike is two rolls that each carry every flat bonus (section 1). No lower ranged damage; the price of range was the opportunity attack and cover from enemies.
- **5e 2014**: ranged was paid *more* (Archery's +2 with the -5/+10), section 1.1; 2024 removed the +10.
- **13th Age**: Double Ranged Attack pays for its second shot with a smaller die (d8 to d6).
- **Draw Steel** [read]: by kit bonus. Ranged kits: Rapid-Fire +2/+2/+2, Arcane Archer +2/+2/+2, Sniper +0/+0/+4 (only the top tier), Ranger and Cloak and Dagger +1/+1/+1; and ranged kits give little or no Stamina (Sniper and Arcane Archer: none; Rapid-Fire +3; the mixed Ranger +6). The melee kits' bonuses were not compared here.
- **ICON**: the Vagabond's stated trade: "Strengths: High mobility and damage [...] Weaknesses: Relatively low durability, relies on support" (HP 28, Defense 10).

- [read] PF2e *reload*: "This entry indicates how many Interact actions it takes to reload such weapons. This can be 0 if drawing ammunition and firing the weapon are part of the same action." 4e: bows "load free", "The crossbow is 'load minor'". 5e *Loading*: "you can fire only one piece of ammunition from it when you use an action, bonus action, or reaction to fire it, regardless of the number of attacks you can normally make." - [checked] **A reload that carries a second action is liked.** Same thread, graystone (2 favorites): "Add in running reload at 4th and you use your reload action to keep at range too." Squiggit (1 favorite): "it's not really a wasted action for the crossbow user because they have to do it anyways. Trip.H, Jul 31, 2024 (3 favorites), on a quiet upside: "Crossbows and 2-H reload weapons are not 'locked in' to only attacking like 2-H melee weapons are. - [checked] **Counting arrows**: Schreckstoff (rzs43djg, May 5, 2021): "I personally do not make my players track ammunition unless there's some special circumstances or special ammo." Hobit of Bree, the opening post, lists ammunition among the reasons archery "is a bit of a trap". Deriven Firelion (2024): "you need to carry a lot of arrows with flurry".
- [checked] exequiel759 (rzs4uwi7, Aug 5, 2024) names the two taxes side by side: "a melee character in SF2e is assumed to have a 'movement tax' to be within melee range of enemies, much like how ranged characters in PF2e are assumed to have a 'reloading tax' unless you are using something like a bow."

Draw Steel *Two Shot*; Tactician 9, *Blot Out the Sun!* (11 Focus): "Each target can make a ranged free strike that gains an edge against any enemy marked by you". Lancer Monarch, *Avenger Silos*: "1/round, on a critical hit with any ranged weapon, the Monarch may deal 3 explosive to a different character of your choice within range 15".

### 6.2 Reception [checked]

- The 5e -5/+10 was the burst that broke the table, and it was the *repeatable* part that did it (section 1.1): "Crossbow experts get one [extra attack] every damn turn" (DM David). 2024 removed it.
- 4e: gjnave on Twin Strike plus Quarry at level 1 (section 3.3): "the Ranger is dominating the battlefield taking over 80% of the kills." Rapid Shot: [lead] a handbook calls it "good for minion-clearance and not a lot else"; [checked] Neonchameleon: "The Rapid Shot feature worked fine for me".
- PF2e: the crossbow as "a one-hit-wonder" (Pumpkinhead11) is the praised form of burst; Hunted Shot's two rolls are the default.
- Called shots (5e has none): [checked] DM David, "What to Do When a D&D Player Wants to Be Ready, Call a Shot, or Delay", quoting the 2e DMG: "Against a creature, a called shot will only cause the normal amount of damage allowed by the weapon. Attempts to blind, cripple, or maim will not succeed." And: "Such a limit quashes most interest in called shots, so the designers opted for rules economy over adding rules for called shots. Still, players may want to temporarily impose a condition like Blinded, Deafened, or Prone." He advises a flat penalty over disadvantage: "Don't impose disadvantage, because that creates an incentive to call a shot whenever an attack would suffer disadvantage anyway." https://dmdavid.com/tag/what-to-do-when-a-dd-player-wants-to-be-ready-call-a-shot-or-delay/ . The games read here that do have a called shot make it a **rider chosen after a strong roll** (Lancer's Watch This, on a critical hit) or a **choice of rider on a hit** (4e's Clever Shot), never extra damage for a penalty.
- No reception was found for Lancer's Mark for Death, Draw Steel's Patient Shot or ICON's Freelancer.

## Patterns

1. **The archer's boredom is one best button repeated, and it appears wherever multi-shot is the best use of every action.** 4e (Twin Strike: "Every. Single. Ranger. Uses. Twin-Strike. Almost. Every. Round."), PF2e (flurry ranger: "stand in one spot and shoot arrows"; "Gravity Weapon > Hunt Prey > Hunted Shot [...] Repeat until there are no more enemies"), PF1 ("an artillery platform"). Evidence: strong (three games, many posters, 2009 to 2024). The 4e posters' own fix: make the other buttons worth pressing, not the best one worse.
2. **Range is an advantage the rules must price, and the price is paid in one of three coins: lower damage (PF2e), exposure when an enemy closes (4e's opportunity attack, Draw Steel's bane, 5e's disadvantage, Lancer's Engaged), or obstacles on the line (cover).** When a game sells the erasers of all three (5e 2014: Sharpshooter + Crossbow Expert + Archery), the shooter is "the safest and highest damage at once" and other players feel they "did not need to be there". Evidence: strong for 5e and PF2e ([checked] blog and several threads); the PF2e "damage is intentionally lower" line is players' reading plus the GM Core creature table, not a designer's sentence I could open.
3. **Lower damage alone does not make the shooter interesting, and it can swing to "is archery a trap?".** PF2e posters split on whether the price is too high, and agree the answer depends on the map ("If your GM is constantly springing enemies in your face with lots of cover, it's different than if your GM lets you start combat at 500' across an open field"). Evidence: strong (one long thread, many voices). And a flat bonus on every hit (a mark's damage) erases the die gap: "ranged does almost as much damage as melee."
4. **What makes a shooter move is a reason on the line of fire, not a rule that says "move": cover from creatures in the way, total cover to walk around, high ground to climb to, a sweet-spot distance.** PF2e (lesser cover from allies: "one turn of setup pays dividends"), ICON (exactly range 3; height ignores cover), Lancer (Solar Backdrop, Flank needs "doesn't have cover from your position"), 4e (Prime Shot: be the nearest), DM David and Nifft for the GM's side. It works in PF2e because the action spent moving replaces the worst attack. Evidence: medium (rules in five games; players' approval read only for PF2e and 5e).
5. **A penalty for being close is accepted when it is a danger (an enemy next to you) and resented when it is an accuracy rule that reads as nonsense (PF2e's volley: "It makes no sense"; 15 favorites).** A penalty to shoot into a melee is seen as a feat tax ("Excellent! No feat-tax!"). Evidence: medium (PF2e threads only).
6. **Standing still can be a choice with a price instead of the default: the game pays for not moving and takes something away.** Draw Steel's Patient Shot (extra damage if you take no move action), Lancer's Crack Shot (Immobilized for +1 Accuracy), Mark for Death (Immobilized, no reactions, target beyond 5 and out of cover), Ordnance (fire first, then move), Spotter II (did not move). The opposite kits pay for moving (Fade, Skirmisher, Nimble Strike, Skirmish Strike, Running Attack). Evidence: rules strong (three games each way); reception not found.
7. **A mark that costs an action each time the quarry dies is called cumbersome; every game that kept the mark found a way to refund it.** PF2e (Hunted Shot's two shots for one action, Favored Prey's free mark at initiative, hunting before the fight; the playtest's level-19 free mark), 4e (a minor action, ends on defeat, damage once a round chosen after the rolls), 13th Age (a free action, once a battle), Draw Steel ("When a creature marked by you is reduced to 0 Stamina, you can use a free triggered action to mark a new target"). Side effects players report: the ranger stops focus-firing with the party to avoid re-marking, skips the mark on weak enemies, and suffers in fights with many small foes. Evidence: strong (designer blogs and two long threads).
8. **Of the kinds of mark, players take damage and accuracy and leave defense: PF2e's Outwit is "infamously bad"; once-a-round damage (Precision, Hunter's Quarry) is valued because it "doesn't lock you into trying to only attack", while a per-attack benefit (Flurry) is strongest and the most monotonous.** A mark the whole party uses (Draw Steel's Mark, Lancer's Lock On, Warden's Boon, Assisting Shot) turns the shooter into a spotter. Evidence: medium (one PF2e thread for the edges; the party marks are rules text only).
9. **The praised non-damage tools are riders on the normal shot, available every turn, with a choice: Clever Shot's slide / prone / slowed ("The star of the show"), crits that pin, Hamstring Shot, Crack Shot's headshot / leg shot / body shot.** The two cautionary cases: an archer who *only* controls (4e Seeker: "way too full of 'soft' control", remembered as the weakest class) and trick arrows rationed to two a rest (5e Arcane Archer: "nowhere near often enough to make you feel like you're getting the fantasy"). Evidence: medium-strong for 4e and 5e; Lancer and Draw Steel are rules only.
10. **Reload is judged by what the action buys: alone it is "a reloading tax" and the crossbow is "in a rough place"; bundled with a move, a hide or Take Cover (Running Reload, Crossbow Ace) it becomes the shooter's positional action, and one big shot per turn ("a one-hit-wonder") is the burst players praise, where repeatable extra damage for a penalty (5e's -5/+10) is the burst that was removed.** Evidence: medium (PF2e threads, RPGBot, DM David); Lancer's Loading + Ordnance rifle is the same shape in rules, reception not found.
