# Raw: Crucible's spellcraft (Rune + Gesture + Inflection)

**Date:** 2026-11-19. Crucible's rules and other people's opinions, never 2d12's.

## The question

Lucas, 2026-11-18: riders "aren't ever going to be rated perfectly, which is why spells can follow more open rules. They can have descriptions of their own and rules inside themselves"; and of Crucible, that "adding a word to the spell shapes it" (an arrow + cold makes a ranged cold attack). `magic-design.md` 5i described Crucible from memory only. What does it really do, and what does that say about composing against written cards, about the cut Merge, and about magic outside a fight?

## 1. The parts, as implemented in 0.11.3 [read]

The rulebook's own summary: "If you think of a spell as a sentence, each spell must have a Rune (noun) and a Gesture (verb), and may optionally include an Inflection (adjective/adverb) that modifies either."

**Twelve Runes.** A Rune fixes five things and nothing else: the damage type, the defense attacked, the pool hit (Health or Morale), one of the two abilities the attack scales with, and the proficiency family that gives the skill bonus. It also names its opposed Rune. No Rune carries a cost, a range or a condition.

| Rune | Damage | Defense | Pool | Ability | Family | Opposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flame | Fire | Reflex | Health | Intellect | Elemental | Frost |
| Frost | Cold | Fortitude | Health | Wisdom | Elemental | Flame |
| Storm | Electricity | Reflex | Health | Intellect | Elemental | Earth |
| Earth | Acid | Reflex | Health | Wisdom | Elemental | Storm |
| Kinesis | Bludgeoning, Piercing or Slashing (chosen at the cast) | Physical | Health | Presence | Physical | Control |
| Control | Psychic | Willpower | Morale | Wisdom | Physical | Kinesis |
| Death | Corruption | Fortitude | Health | Presence | Physical | Life |
| Life | restores Health (Poison if negated) | Fortitude | Health | Wisdom | Physical | Death |
| Illumination | Radiant | Reflex | Health | Presence | Spiritual | Illusion |
| Illusion | Psychic | Willpower | Morale | Intellect | Spiritual | Illumination |
| Oblivion | Void | Willpower | Morale | Intellect | Spiritual | Soul |
| Soul | restores Morale (Psychic if negated) | Willpower | Morale | Presence | Spiritual | Oblivion |

**Seventeen Gestures.** A Gesture fixes the cost in Action and Focus, the free hands, the range, the target shape, the Base Damage and the second scaling ability. Distances are feet on a 1-foot grid; a character has 6 Action a round.

| Gesture | Action / Focus | Hands | Range and target | Base | Ability | Tier node |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Touch (free with any Rune) | 2 / 1 | 1 | 1 ft, one creature | 6 | Dexterity | none |
| Arrow | 3 / 1 | 1 | 60 ft, one creature | 8 | Intellect | 1 |
| Fan | 3 / 1 | 1 | 120° arc, 6 ft | 6 | Intellect | 1 |
| Influence | 3 / 1 | 1 | adjacent, one creature | 10 | Presence | 1 |
| Ray | 4 / 1 | 1 | line 1 ft × 30 ft | 6 | Wisdom | 1 |
| Aspect | 2 / 1 | 1 | self, 6 rounds: +2 Resistance to the Rune's type and +2 damage of that type; one at a time | none | Presence | 1 |
| Ward | 2 / 1 | 1 | self, 1 round: +6 Resistance to the Rune's type; one at a time | 6 (the Resistance) | Toughness | 2 |
| Sense | 3 / 1, maintained | 1 | 30 ft around you: detects spellcraft of that Rune and the creature types tied to it | none | Presence | 1 |
| Step | 1 / 1 (code; its card says 3) | 0 | teleport 20 ft in a line, hitting what it crosses | 2 | Dexterity | 2 |
| Strike | weapon's Action / 1 | 0 | a weapon Strike with the Rune's damage type | weapon's | Strength | 2 |
| Create | 4 / 1 | 2 | a Sprite within 10 ft: a Minion of half your level, 6 rounds, one at a time | none | Wisdom | 1 |
| Conjure | 4 / 1, maintained | 2 | a Visitor within 30 ft: a Minion of your level, 12 rounds | none | Wisdom | 4 |
| Pulse | 4 / 1 (code; card says 5 / 2) | 2 | 10 ft around you | 8 | Presence | 4 |
| Blast | 5 / 2 | 2 | 6 ft radius within 60 ft | 8 | Intellect | 4 |
| Cone | 5 / 2 | 2 | 60° cone, 30 ft | 8 | Intellect | 4 |
| Surge | 5 / 2 | 2 | line 10 ft × 15 ft | 8 | Wisdom | 4 |
| Aura | 5 / 1, maintained | 2 | 20 ft around you, moves with you; attacks or heals each creature that enters or starts its turn; combat only | 6 | Presence | 4 |

The Tier 2 area Gestures carry a "Playtest Notes" line: placed at Tier 4 "early" for testing, meant for about level 6.

**Ten Inflections**, each a change to the cost plus one effect:

| Inflection | Cost change | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Compose | +1 Action, −1 Focus | a slower, cheaper cast |
| Quicken | −1 Action, +1 Focus | a faster, dearer cast |
| Extend | +1 Action, +1 Focus | longer duration ("not yet automated") |
| Push / Pull | +1 Focus | moves each creature the spell affected away from or toward you by the spell's Ability Bonus in feet (doubled on a Critical Hit) |
| Negate | +1 Focus | inverts the spell: a heal harms; Aspect becomes a touch curse, −2 Resistance to the Rune's type for 6 rounds |
| Determine | +1 Focus | Counterspell attempts against it take +2 Banes |
| Elude | +1 Focus | the cast gives no reaction Strike |
| React | +2 Focus | the spell is cast as a Reaction |
| Reshape | +2 Focus | an area hits only enemies (or heals only allies) |

One Inflection a spell. Inflections are spoken: a Silenced caster cannot use one.

## 2. How a composed spell's numbers are set [read]

`spell-action.mjs` assembles the spell with no table of combinations:

- **Cost** = the Gesture's cost + the Inflection's. **Range, target and area** = the Gesture's. **Base Damage** = the Gesture's.
- **Damage type, defense and pool** = the Rune's.
- **Attack roll** = 3d8 (Boons, Banes) + Ability Bonus + Skill Bonus. The Ability Bonus averages the Rune's ability and the Gesture's (`this.scaling = [this.rune.scaling, this.gesture.scaling]`), halved. The Skill Bonus comes from the Rune's family (Untrained −4 to Master +3).
- **Damage** = Overflow (roll minus defense) + Base Damage − Resistance. Beating the defense by more than 6 is a Critical Hit, which adds nothing by itself.
- **Name** = generated: "Arrow of Frost", "Quickened Arrow of Flame".

So Lucas's example holds exactly: Arrow + Frost is a 60-foot single-target Cold attack against Fortitude for 3 Action and 1 Focus, and nobody wrote it.

**What limits composition.** Only knowledge: a component is a Talent (the Talent's hint: "grants knowledge of one spellcraft component"). A hero has 3 Talent Points at creation and 3 a level. Rune Talents sit on the tree's innermost nodes (ability 2); Gestures and Inflections on Tier 1 to 4 nodes (level 0 to 4, ability 3 to 5 in that node's ability). Touch is free with the first Rune. There are no spell levels, no minimum in a Rune, no per-combination unlock: every Rune you know works with every Gesture you know. The rulebook's example caster at level 6 knows 3 Runes, 3 Gestures and 1 Inflection: "18 different combinations". The full grid is 12 × 17 × 11 = 2,244 nominal spells [mine]. Some cells are unfinished in code: Aspect and Ward "not configured for healing Runes yet"; summons missing for several Runes fall back to the Flame creature.

## 3. Resources and limits [read; the reception part not found]

- **Action:** 6 a round, refilled each turn. A 5-Action Gesture is most of a turn.
- **Focus:** maximum = half of (Wisdom + Presence + Intellect), rounded up, plus bonuses. "during the midst of combat Focus must be used thoughtfully as it will not naturally replenish." A 10-minute **Recover** refills Health, Morale and Focus in full, so Focus is in practice a per-fight pool [mine].
- **Heroism:** the table fills a shared meter (every Action spent on others counts; a point for everyone each "Number of Heroes × 12" Action). It pays for the biggest Talent actions and may stand in for missing Focus, point for point. Removed on Recover.
- **Maintained** spells (Aura, Conjure, Sense): the Focus cost again at the start of each of your turns; by default one maintained effect at a time. Issue #385 [checked]: the designer chose the caster's own turn and the base cost.
- **Ways back to Focus are Talents:** Refocus (a talisman, a roll), Adrenaline, Wizard (the next spell this turn costs 1 less Focus), Blood Magic (10 Health a Focus), Eldritch Bargain (10 Morale a Focus).
- **Nova or spam complaints: none found.** [mine] Compose on a 1-Focus Gesture gives a 0-Focus spell (Touch at 3 Action, Arrow at 4), so a caster out of Focus still casts every turn; whether tables find that dull I could not learn.

## 4. Iconic Spells [read]

Written spells exist beside the composed ones. The rulebook: Iconic Spells "have specialized effects which are not otherwise possible via the Composed Spell system and cover the breadth of magical capability that a system like Crucible requires." They arrived in 0.6.0 as "an extension of the spellcraft system" [checked, aggregator].

- **Slots** come from Talents: every Inflection Talent gives one; signature Talents one or two (Wizard 2, Conjurer 2). You may swap one known spell when you gain a level.
- **Prerequisite:** you must know the components the spell lists. Several list **two Runes**: Invisibility (Illumination + Illusion, Aspect, Elude), Lifebloom (Life + Soul, Aura, Extend), Revive (Control + Life, Influence, Push), Telecognition (Control + Soul, Sense, Pull), Bind Armament (Control + Kinesis, Strike, Pull).
- **The card writes its own cost and text** (Revive: 6 Action, 4 Focus; Engulfing Darkness: 4 Action, 2 Focus, a 20-foot sphere of darkness for 6 rounds). The components are a key and still give the attack's scaling.
- 14 ship today: six permanent ability curses, Protective Mirage, Dawn Beacon, Engulfing Darkness and the five above.
- The **Sorcerer** signature inverts it: "You cannot cast Iconic Spells, but when you cast a spell using an Inflection it deals an additional +1 Damage per Iconic Spell Slot that you have."

## 5. Conditions and riders [read]

**A Rune has no rider.** A plain composed spell does damage or healing and nothing more. Each damage type has an "associated" condition, "usually as a result of a Critical Hit", and it is a **Talent** that turns it on: twelve Tier 3 Talents of one template, "your Critical Hits which deal damage cause the ... condition".

| Rune | Talent | On a Critical Hit |
|---|---|---|
| Flame | Pyromancer | Burning, 3 rounds (Fire to Health and Morale) |
| Frost | Rimecaller | Freezing, 1 round, and Slowed |
| Storm | Surgeweaver | Shocked, 3 rounds, and Staggered |
| Earth | Dustbinder | Corroding, 3 rounds |
| Death | Necromancer | Decaying, 3 rounds |
| Kinesis | Kineturge | Bleeding, 1 round |
| Illumination | Lightbringer | Irradiated, 1 round (Health and Morale) |
| Illusion | Mesmer | Confused, 1 round, and Disoriented |
| Control | Thoughtbinder | Dominated, 3 rounds |
| Oblivion | Voidcaller | Entropy, 1 round, and Frightened |
| Life / Soul | Mender / Inspirator | Mending / Inspired (extra restoration) |

So the rider is fixed for each Rune, paid for with a Talent, and gated by the margin of the roll, never chosen at the cast. The forced movement is the only rider a caster picks while composing (Push, Pull), and it is an Inflection. Spell effects are "Magical" and carry a Removal DC that only anti-magic beats.

## 6. Talents and dabbling [read]

- **Changing a Rune's reading:** twelve Talents swap a Rune's ability (Acrid Earth: Intellect in place of Wisdom); Blightcaller and Tormentor turn Life and Soul into damage.
- **Signatures:** Spellblade and Arcane Archer (a spell after a Strike costs 1 less Action; Arrow uses the bow), Primalist (elemental stances that carry the Rune's critical Talent onto weapon Strikes), Conjurer (three summons), Telekinetic (Push or Pull free once a turn), Runewarden, Healer, Bard.
- **Hands and voice:** Channeler's Grip, Strong Grip, Deft Grip, Shield Ward lift the free-hand rule; Legerdemain makes low-tier Inflections silent.
- **A non-caster dabbles** with one Talent Point: a Rune gives Touch and the Rune's rote action at once. Strike, Ward and Step sit in the Strength, Toughness and Dexterity parts of the tree. Spell scrolls "temporarily" grant one to three components; item affixes grant Rune, Gesture or Inflection knowledge (issues #870, #871, #1203, titles only [lead]).

## 7. Defense against spells [read]; reception [not found]

- **Counterspell** (a Talent; Reaction, 1 Action + 1 Focus, 30 feet): "Designate a Rune and Gesture which you know"; the opposed Rune gives +2 Boons, "the identical Gesture" +2 Boons; a roll against the caster's Willpower. Recognize Spellcraft names the Rune being cast only "if you also know that Rune". So the grammar is also the defense: knowing the parts is how you read and unpick a spell. Foundry's 2026 showcase names "a richly automated Counterspell workflow" [checked].
- Martial answers: Inquisitor, Interrupting Throw (spells of 3 Action or more), Overwatch: a Critical Hit on the caster loses the spell. Ward and Aspect resist one Rune's type.
- **Reception.** Nothing on spellcraft. EN World, June 2023 [checked, summarizer]: "Its a classless D&D clone optimized for Foundry"; "They do classless by shifting almost everything (including spells) into feats". Foundry's own line: "a fast-paced, strategic, and exciting magic system" (promotion). Usage: 65th among systems, 1.28% of installs (Foundry, May 2026) [checked].
- **[mine], structural, not anyone's report:** two composed spells of one Gesture differ by four labels (type, defense, pool, ability). What makes Flame feel unlike Frost is outside the grammar: the critical Talent, the rote action, the Iconic Spells. Two of the three were added after the grammar (the rotes in 0.8.2, November 2025; Iconic Spells in 0.6.0; when the critical Talents arrived I did not establish), which reads as an answer to "samey", but he does not say so on any page I opened.

## 8. Magic outside a fight [read]

- The rulebook: "Every magic spell has a cost- whether used within a combat situation or for out-of-combat benefits."
- **Each Rune Talent grants one rote action** (3 Action, 1 Focus; Earth's costs none): Enkindle, Condense, Energize, Mould, Propel, Motivate, Ennervate, Bloom, Reveal, Seeming, Erase, Evoke. Each lists three small written effects (Flame: light a candle; a moment's fire immunity; make flames dance) and ends: "You may attempt other uses similar those above, the effectiveness of which is decided by the Gamemaster." Added in 0.8.2 as "cantrip" or "rote" actions "that expand the basic foundation of the magic system" (issue #382's title [checked]).
- The **Sense** Gesture is the grammar's one utility verb. **Aura** refuses outside combat ("an Aura may only be cast during combat").
- Utility with teeth is Iconic: Telecognition (the target's surface thought "in 3 words or less"), Invisibility, Dawn Beacon, Revive.
- Counterspell has a non-combat workflow against a DC (issue #574 [lead]; the code path exists [read]).
- So utility was not left to composition: Create + Earth makes a combat Minion, not a wall. Out-of-combat magic is written text keyed to the Rune, with an explicit GM clause.

## What seems to transfer

| Crucible's part | Nearest 2d12 decision | What differs |
|---|---|---|
| Rune: type, defense, pool, ability | Path (decisions 5, 23; the 5j pool habit) | A Rune is four mechanical labels and no level; a Path has levels and a usual condition that is only a writer's guide (91) |
| Gesture: cost, range, shape, base damage | No equivalent; nearest are Reach and Breadth (26, 43) and "an area is a different spell" (43) | Crucible's jump from one creature to an area is another Gesture, bought once for every Rune; 2d12 writes another card. Schools are verbs; no Gesture is a verb except Sense, Ward, Create |
| Inflection: one cost change + one effect | An Overcast step for 1 Surge (67) | One Inflection a spell, known by Talent, paid from the per-fight pool; no ladder, no spare level, no cap by Escalation |
| Focus (per fight, refilled in 10 minutes), Heroism (built by the table's Actions) | Surge (66 to 70) | Focus starts full and drains; Surge starts low and builds. Heroism is the building one, shared by the table |
| Critical-hit Talent of each Rune | The riders of 5b, now a guide (91) | Fixed, bought, and triggered by the roll's margin; never added by choice |
| Iconic Spell, some with two Runes | A written card; a rare two-Path spell (63) | In Crucible the written spell is the exception that covers what the grammar can't; in 2d12 it is the rule |
| Rote action of each Rune | Out-of-combat magic by Path and School (87, open) | Keyed to the Rune alone; three written uses plus a GM clause |
| Counterspell by opposed Rune or same Gesture | Abjuration ("it answers or stops something") | The defense reads the attacker's parts |

**Lessons (Crucible's evidence, for Lucas to weigh).**

1. **Composing against cards.** Composition gave Crucible breadth at almost no writing cost and, inside its limits, no ambiguity: every composed spell is fully computed. It bought that by making every part a number or a label. The moment an effect needed a sentence (darkness, invisibility, a curse, raising the dead), Crucible wrote a card, and its rulebook says why in so many words. That supports Lucas's own remark that spells with "rules inside themselves" need a description. The cost of composing is not ambiguity but sameness, and it runs on software: no table of combinations exists because the program does the assembling. [mine] What is unambiguous in Crucible is the grammar; what is interesting is the written part.
2. **What stands where Merge stood.** Crucible never lets a caster add a second Rune's effect to a spell. Two substances meet only in written Iconic Spells that require both Runes: the same answer as decision 63. A Rune's condition is not a free add-on either: it costs a Talent and needs a Critical Hit, so it is a reward of the roll, never a button always pressed (Lucas's objection to Merge, decision 91). The chosen add-on Crucible does allow changes position or timing (Push, Pull, React, Reshape), never a condition. And a second substance gets one more job: the opposed Rune is the best counter.
3. **A grammar outside a fight.** Crucible's noun + verb grammar did not produce its utility magic. Utility hangs on the noun alone (one rote for each Rune, written examples plus "decided by the Gamemaster"), on a single utility verb (Sense), and on Iconic Spells; the one area Gesture that would stay up (Aura) is combat only. For decision 87's open piece this is a data point for "written by substance, a few fixed uses each" over "derived from two words", and a warning: Crucible's open clause is exactly the kind of ambiguity 2d12 means to avoid, and its Focus refills in 10 minutes, so its out-of-combat cost limits nothing [mine].
