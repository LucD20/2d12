### 1.2b Level 9 (Relentless Reaction) and level 11 (Exalted Reaction), per cause (`class-features/<cause>.json`)

The levels 9 and 11 are VERIFIED ("Relentless Reaction (9th Level)", "Exalted Reaction (11th Level)" in every cause file). Per cause:

| Cause | Relentless (9) | Exalted (11) | Verdict against the report |
|---|---|---|---|
| Justice | "If your Strike hits, the target takes persistent spirit damage equal to your Charisma modifier." | "Each ally in your champion's aura with the target in their melee reach can spend a reaction to Strike the target with a –5 penalty." | **CORRECTED**: the persistent damage needs the Strike to **hit** (a third gate). The Exalted text the report gave as "legacy, from an older guide" is also the Remaster text, and the penalty is –5, and it costs each ally their reaction. |
| Redemption | "If the enemy refuses, it takes persistent spirit damage equal to your Charisma modifier." | "If the enemy refuses, you can choose to grant the resistance to yourself and all allies in your champion's aura, including the triggering ally. If you do, the resistance is reduced by 2 for all." | CORRECTED: this is the Remaster text too; the reduction is exactly 2. |
| Liberation | "You punish those who ensnare your allies. If the triggering enemy was using any effects to make your ally Grabbed, Restrained, Immobilized, or Paralyzed when you used Liberating Step, that enemy takes persistent spirit damage equal to your Charisma modifier." | "In addition to the ally affected by Liberating Step, you and all other allies in your champion's aura can Step as a free action. You and your allies gain the benefit even if the ally who benefited from Liberating Step can't move." | VERIFIED |
| Grandeur | "The enemy also takes persistent spirit damage equal to your Charisma modifier, and it can't recover from this persistent damage while affected by the revealing light from your Flash of Grandeur." | "In addition to the enemy affected by Flash of Grandeur, each other enemy in your champion's aura is affected by the revealing light spell for 1 round." | VERIFIED; new detail: the persistent damage cannot be ended while the light lasts. |
| Obedience | "If the enemy refuses, it takes persistent spirit damage equal to your Charisma modifier." | "Each enemy in your champion's aura other than the triggering creature must also either drop Prone or take mental damage (each enemy chooses). These creatures take only minimum damage (typically 3 damage at 11th level, 4 at 12th, 5 at 16th, and 6 at 19th) ..." | not in the report |
| Desecration | "If your Charisma modifier is +3 or greater, your resistance against the triggering damage equals your Charisma modifier + half your level." | "Each enemy in your champion's aura takes a –1 status penalty to attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn." | not in the report |
| Iniquity | "An enemy damaged by the initial reaction's damage also takes persistent spirit damage equal to your Charisma modifier." | "Each enemy in your champion's aura other than the triggering creature takes half the damage you deal to the triggering enemy" | not in the report |

So "the enemy also takes persistent spirit damage equal to Charisma" is not one shared rule: Justice needs a hit, Redemption and Obedience need a refusal, Liberation needs the enemy to have been holding the ally, Desecration gets no damage at all.

## 3. NEW FINDINGS (exact quotes the report lacks)

1. **What happens after the one reaction is spent.** Witch of Miracles, Apr 28, 2025, 01:16 am, https://paizo.com/threads/rzs6a2c1 : "people talk like both the AC and reaction are good at the same time, but getting value from them against a given action is mutually exclusive—and in the situation one is useful, the other is useless." and "There are plenty of cases where one enemy can blow your reaction and then another can target a squishy without consequence, and then you're not really doing much tanking at all." and "the arguments about how champion can tank and the supposed catch-22 the champion inflicts just ignore what happens after the champion uses their reaction for the round."
2. **The reaction that never fires.** Ascalaphus, Feb 19, 2021, https://paizo.com/threads/rzs43be1 : "Ironically for champions, their class works the best when they're not the only one in melee, since if their champion reaction never gets triggered, that's a comatose class feature. Fighters have less trouble with that - you could say that their main class feature is having +2 to hit and that's a big deal."
3. **Why the Champion is not felt as "nothing to do on my turn", and the Guardian is.** Lightning Raven, Jun 4, 2024, 06:05 pm, https://paizo.com/threads/rzs4aipp&page=5 : "As long as the Guardian is doing something in their own turns instead of just getting close and waiting to use their reactions and hoping they trigger. The Champion works because it's a dynamic class that happens to have an Amazing reaction, not a purely reactive class that only has their turn to respond to the enemies. Quite the opposite, actually, as a Champion player I actually feel like I don't have enough actions to do what I want, while with the Guardian, my player found the "Routine" quite fast. ... I really don't see this class doing much more than Stride into position, taunt, raise shield, wait to get whacked in the face." Same post: "It needs to have more proactive things to do on its turn. Even if it's just having set up actions that enhance their reactions." (No complaint "nothing to do on my turn" about the Champion was found in any thread read; this is the opposite testimony.)
4. **Three reactions that do not compete.** Ascalaphus, Apr 28, 2025, 06:59 am, https://paizo.com/threads/rzs6a2c1&page=2 : "Reactive Strike is interesting because at first it seems like it competes with shield block or your champion reaction. But actually it doesn't; it fills a gap they didn't cover. - If the enemy attacks you, you shield block - If they attack your front row buddy, you use a champion reaction - If they try to get out of your aura you use reactive strike. Those reactions stack "sideways"; when you're using the one, you probably don't need the other that turn."
5. **The taunt that punishes its user.** Playtest report, Teridax, May 2, 2024, 06:02 pm, https://paizo.com/threads/rzs4aipp&page=3: "AoE was the bane of Gork's existence. I cannot stress how bad it felt to successfully Taunt a dragon out of attacking one of my allies, only for the dragon to use a breath weapon, invalidate my play, and hurt me more than if I'd just done nothing." Another report, Tridus, May 26, 2024, 04:34 pm, rzs4aipp&page=4: "It was nice that the source of the attack didn't matter so it worked against ranged (which something like Retributive Strike doesn't), but otherwise it felt really limiting. Even just having to stay next to people to use it limited what I could do unless I just abandoned that."
6. **The no-unholy campaign: the GM's own instinct was "profane by behaviour".** ComradeCanuck, Feb 15, 2024, 08:16 am, https://paizo.com/threads/rzs43xyj : "My initial thought for a reasonable way to house-rule it ... is to suggest the Champion player take the Vengeful Oath feat, and allow them to treat people who have specifically committed crimes against their kingdom as Unholy. It seems thematically appropriate for the Kingmaker campaign". Finoan's answer: "The changes to Vengeful Oath are probably fine as a houserule." And Finoan, 07:52 am: "The PF2 concept is more that 'no one dies while fighting shoulder to shoulder with a Champion'. ... But 4 points extra damage per hit at level 6 is a pretty small amount of extra damage. I don't think it was ever intended to noticeably change the Champions 'rounds to kill' rating against such enemies. Primarily the purpose was to trigger weaknesses of fiends and undead that have a weakness to good damage."
7. **How rare the profane really is (data, not opinion).** Monster Core in the Foundry data: 33 of 492 creatures (about 7%) have a weakness to holy (3 / 5 / 10 / 15 by level band); all 30 fiends are unholy and 22 are weak to holy; 37 of 38 undead are unholy but only 1 is weak to holy. See R43, R44.

Angwa, same thread: "If the enemy is invisible it is now concealed. ... This is always a net positive." (No poster comment on Flash of Grandeur in play was found in the Paizo threads read.)
