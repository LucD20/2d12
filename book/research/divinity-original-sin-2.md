# Divinity: Original Sin 2: what 2d12 could borrow

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Asked by Lucas,** with his playtesters' ideas for the Paladin and the Archer (`talents-design.md` section 9): "Pillars of Eternity and Divinity Original Sin 2. Probably we could steal stuff from them. Run a research on both of them, compared to our system and things people like about their mechanics (not really the game itself), what people love, what could fit an actual TTRPG, if anything could fit our system." Surfaces (the ambient interaction) are "a whole other topic": only what fits a table is here.

**What was read, and how far to trust it.** One independent researcher; the full report is `raw/2026-11-20-divinity-original-sin-2.md` (a source for each claim, about 20 Steam threads, Larian designer interviews, PC Gamer, RPG Codex, four board game reviews). Rules [read] from the Divinity wiki (2018 Definitive Edition values) and the **official board game rulebook, read in full**. **Reddit could not be reached:** the "many players" evidence is Steam, reviews and the designers. **No fan tabletop conversion with rules was found.** This file is Claude's summary.

**Other games' opinions, never 2d12's rules. Nothing here is decided.**

---

## The most borrowable ideas (ranked by the researcher)

| # | Idea | In DOS2 | Evidence | How it could map onto 2d12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One status track, refreshed never stacked** | The board game: a status sits on a timeline at its duration and slides one slot a turn; reapplying only raises it to the longer; durations capped (5, 3 for minions); bosses list immunities and can't be moved | [read], strong | 2d12 already keeps the longer duration (Chapter 8). A cap on duration is the new part |
| 2 | **A short immunity after hard control** | Larian removed most enemy control because "strip armor, then lock down one character" was "a dominant tactic"; players asked for "a one turn immunity"; Larian added the optional Indomitable talent | [checked], strong | A boss that ends Stunned, Prone, Immobilized and the like is immune to it for 1 round: another shape for the boss rule |
| 3 | **Talents that change the action economy are the loved ones** | The Pawn (1 AP of free movement a turn), Executioner (+2 AP on a kill, once a turn), Glass Cannon (full AP every turn, no protection from statuses), Opportunist (attacks of opportunity, which DOS2 doesn't give), Hothead (a bonus only at full health); flat resistances and +1 points are scorned | [checked], medium-strong | A free square of movement, AP back once a Round, a new Reaction trigger, a window that opens under a condition: horizontal, no number on a roll (T1). Note: AP and Escalation are already priced in the ruler |
| 4 | **Certain control once a visible condition is met** | The armor gate made control predictable (its designer wanted that after OS1's saving throws "prevented long-term planning"); players hated the physical/magic split (one damage type per party; "save the fun abilities"); Larian dropped it for its next game | [checked], medium | A control rider that lands only on a target already Pressed or Staggered, never a second health bar |
| 5 | **Elemental Arrowheads** | 1 AP: take an element from a surface nearby; your shots add it for one turn | [read]; players mixed | Elemental ammo with nothing to count: one Action takes a damage subtype from a hazard, Zone or light source in reach, for the rest of the turn |
| 6 | **Special arrows as options, not stock** | 15 crafted arrow types (fire, poison, explosive, slowdown, charm, knockdown...); players either hoarded them or had "a ridiculous amount"; arrow recovery called bad | [checked], medium | Supports Trick Shots (Pin, Startle, Knock Back) staying options with no ammo count; the "Green Arrow" ammo the players want fits as Modes |
| 7 | **Height as one bonus a card can give or take** | High ground adds damage and range; Sky Shot gets it anywhere; Glitter Dust strips it; Ballistic Shot adds damage with distance; "height + a strong basic shot + AP" is the archer's real engine | [read]; [checked] | High ground as a Field Boon a card can grant or deny |
| 8 | **A second matching hit makes a condition worse** | Wet then Shocked = Stunned; Chilled twice = Frozen; hard controls give immunity to each other | [read] | For example, Hindered then hit again becomes Immobilized (priced: Mild to Moderate) |
| 9 | **One element tag per space** (the board game) | Wet, Warm, Charged, Oily each add +2 damage of one type in the space; a new tag replaces the old | [read] | A Zone makes everyone in it Weak to one subtype; a Talent can read it |
| 10 | **Borrow AP from next turn** | Adrenaline: 2 AP now, 2 fewer next turn | [read] | A Talent or Feature shape (it tracks itself) |
| 11 | **One small pick lends one tool from another kit; two traits open a combined option** | One point into a school to borrow a skill; two skillbooks make a third skill | [read]; [checked] | Paths and Schools combinations for magic; **avoid the board game's "training talent" gate** (the permission trap) |
| 12 | **Pet Pal shows the third trap** | Loved, yet taken by one character, or respecced in for the animal quests and out again | [checked], weak to medium | More support for non-combat Talents on their own track (T2, T6) |

## The archer's skills players asked about (Lucas's players: Pin, Ricochet, the rain of arrows, ammo)

| Skill | DOS2 (AP, cooldown) | What players said |
|---|---|---|
| **Ricochet** | 2 AP, cd 4: a normal arrow forks to another target within 5 m, up to 2 times (4 in OS1) | Kept: "3 targets from far away with 2 AP is a sweet deal" |
| **Pin Down** | 3 AP, cd 3: damage and Crippled 1 turn (can't move) | "the 3 AP cost often felt like an awkward price" |
| **Arrow Storm** (the rain of arrows) | 3 AP + 3 Source, cd 4: 15 or 16 arrows fall in an area | An ultimate; OS1's Rain of Arrows was 300% weapon damage to each enemy |
| **Barrage** | 3 AP, cd 3: three arrows at three targets you choose | Kept |
| **Ballistic Shot** | 2 AP, cd 4: more damage the farther the target | Kept ("up to 100% damage for 20 m") |
| **Sky Shot** | 2 AP, cd 3: leap and shoot with the high-ground bonus anywhere | Kept |
| **Marksman's Fang** | 2 AP, cd 2: an arrow through every enemy in a line, ignores armor | "seems nice, but mostly wasn't" |
| **Reactive Shot** | 2 AP, cd 2: shoot the first three enemies that move in a marked circle until your next turn | "too finicky and unreliable" |
| **Elemental Arrowheads** | 1 AP, cd 4 | "situationally useful, but I rarely used it" / "sweet extra damage" |

## How it applies to 2d12

- **The Archer's player ideas already have DOS2's answers:** Ricochet is one line of rules (a fork to a second target), and 2d12's magic already has a Fork stretch; the rain of arrows is an Encounter or Apex area (Covering Fire and Volley are near it today); Pin is Trick Shots' Pin.
- **Ammo as Modes, never as stock** is what the evidence supports.
- **The boss rule** gets a second candidate shape: control, then a round of immunity to it.
- **What makes a DOS2 talent loved** fits T1: it changes how a turn is spent, not a number.
