# DC20 against 2d12: how close it is, and what to take from it

**The question (Lucas, 2026-11-27):** "you mention DC20 is closest, is it really that close to my system? what other thing can we learn from it? Or even get from it?"

**Source:** DC20 RPG 0.10.5 Beta, as Markdown in Lucas's private repository (`LucD20/lucd20-personal`), read 2026-11-27 by section: Checks (Advantage & Disadvantage, Multiple Check Penalty, Check & Save Outcomes), Combat Resources (Action Points, Health Thresholds, Stamina Points, Mana Points), Actions (Dodge, Help, Move), Damage (Heavy Hits, Critical Hits, Damage Reduction). Its Reactions are in `reactions.md`. [read] = Claude read the rules text; [agent] = a reader's notes from the same file.

## How close it is

**Close in the action economy, nearly the same rules. Not close in the rest.**

| | DC20 | 2d12 |
|---|---|---|
| AP | 4 a turn; "Any spent Action Points are not regained until the end of a creature's turn" [read] | 4 a turn; regained at the end of the turn (decided 2026-11-27, not built) |
| Reactions | paid with AP; no cap; "only 1 Reaction per Trigger"; all 4 spent: "your turn would immediately end and you'd get your 4 AP back" [agent] | the same, decided; the 0-AP case not written yet |
| Repeating an attack | the Multiple Check Penalty: the same Check again in your turn takes DisADV 1, then 2, then 3; it applies only during your own turn, not to Reactions [read] | Banes on a repeated At-will; a repeated At-will [Strike] Doomed (decided, not built) |
| Stacking | ADV X / DisADV X: one more d20 per instance, keep the best (worst); they cancel one for one [read] | Boons and Banes (+1 / −1, named limits of 2); 5 left become Favored or Doomed (3d12 keep 2) |
| Hit quality | Heavy Hit 5 over the Defense (+1 damage), Brutal 10 over (+2), a natural 20 a Critical [read] | Tiers 1, 2, 3 and Doubles / Critical |
| Health states | Bloodied at half, Well-Bloodied at a quarter, Death's Door at 0 down to a Death Threshold [read] | Bloodied and Strained (two pools), Desperate, Dying, Shattered |
| Healing | Rest Points spent to heal, through actions (Medicine, spells) [read] | Rally Points (Steel Yourself, Tend, Lay On Hands) |
| A second currency | Stamina (martial) and Mana (casters), with a class "Stamina Regen" in combat [read] | none (decided: "nothing new to track", class charter question 6) |
| Growth | Combat Mastery, half your level, added to attacks, saves and defenses [read] | horizontal: a level gives options, never a number on a roll or a DC |
| Defenses | PD (8 + CM + Agility + Intelligence) and AD (8 + CM + Might + Charisma) [read] | Armor Defense and Ward Defense, Attribute DCs |
| Movement | Move: 1 AP for your Speed, and "can break up your movement" around other Actions [read] | Advance in one piece |

So the two systems agree on how a turn is paid and how Reactions are paid, and on the shape of a repeat penalty. They part on the die (one d20 against 2d12 with Tiers), on growth (vertical against horizontal) and on resources (three pools against one).

## What to learn, or take

1. **The 0-AP rule** [agent]: a creature that spent all 4 AP reacting before its turn has its turn end at once and regains 4. 2d12's end-of-turn wording leaves that case open. **Take it** (at the build).
2. **AP buys Advantage** [read]: "When a creature makes a Check or Save in Combat, it can spend 1 or more AP to gain ADV on its Check or Save per AP spent." It is DC20's answer to "what is the third or fourth AP for?", the gap `action-economy.md` found (nothing but a repeated Strike is worth an AP). In 2d12 a Boon is +1, about 8% of a Strike, so "1 AP: 1 Boon" would be too small; "1 AP: 2 Boons" (about 16%) or a Feint already sits there. **Worth a look with the Actions review**, not a take as is.
3. **Dodge and Full Dodge** [read]: 1 AP for DisADV on the next attack against you before your next turn; 1 AP more for every attack. **The shape of Lucas's future Full Defense** (2 AP, some Guard): a 1 AP and a 2 AP version, chosen on your turn, which is DC20's way of making defense a decision on your own turn rather than a Reaction.
4. **The Help Action's decay** [read]: 1 AP gives a d8 Help Die; each more Help in the same turn gives a smaller die (d8 to d6 to d4). A repeat penalty that shrinks the effect instead of adding Banes: a candidate for Aid if it is ever repeated.
5. **The Multiple Check Penalty doesn't apply to Reactions** [read]. 2d12's repeat rule counts "in a turn" too, so a Reaction Strike on another creature's turn isn't a repeat. **Write it plainly at the build** (the least ambiguity).
6. **Small defenses for AP, big ones for a slow pool** [agent]: +5 to a Defense or DisADV for 1 AP; real damage reduction for Stamina or Mana. 2d12 has no second pool (and decided not to have one); its stand-in is a card's Frequency and trigger (Shield of Faith on the Judged creature).
7. **Stamina Regen keyed to how the class plays** [read]: the Champion regains on a Maneuver, the Barbarian on a Heavy Hit, the Rogue on hitting a creature with a Condition. 2d12's Escalation hooks (the Paladin's level 2 hook: an ally Bloodied or Down) are the same idea on a ladder that is read, not spent.
8. **Damage Reduction halves, but not on a Heavy Hit or a Critical** [read]. A halving that a great hit gets through: a rider shape if Shield of Faith's half ever proves too strong (not proposed).

**Not to take:** Combat Mastery (a number that grows on every roll, against the horizontal rule); Stamina and Mana as pools for martials (against "nothing new to track"); the d20's single die.
