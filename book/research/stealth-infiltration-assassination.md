## 1. The Six Failure Modes of Camp Infiltration & Assassination

Every forum thread and design post-mortem on stealth raids circles the same six traps:

1. **Binary Stealth ("one bad roll = fight the entire camp").** If a single failed Stealth check triggers the whole camp's alarm, players learn stealth is a suicide gamble and default to kicking in the front door (`[checked]` Sly Flourish, Alexandrian).
2. **The Solo Scout ("the Paladin scrolls their phone for 45 minutes").** If sneaking only uses one skill, sending the sneaky character alone is mathematically optimal, sidelining everyone else (`[checked]` RPG.SE, Alexandrian).
3. **The HP Wall ("I slit the sleeping warlord's throat for 12 damage; roll Initiative").** Running active-combat HP attrition on a helpless sleeping target breaks immersion and punishes clever play (`[checked]` RPG.SE; `[lead]` r/dndnext).
4. **The Cheap Boss Delete (and the PC Symmetry Trap).** Conversely, letting one Stealth roll auto-kill a major villain feels anticlimactic, invalidates tactical builds, and breaks the moment an enemy assassin tries the exact same rule on a sleeping PC (`[lead]` r/DnD, r/DMAcademy).
5. **The Hollow Minigame ("click skills on a menu").** Subsystems that ignore class features, spells, gear, map space, and combat resources feel "dissociated" and boring to tactical players (`[checked]` Alexandrian; `[lead]` r/Pathfinder2e critique of PF2e subsystems).
6. **Planning Paralysis.** Players spend two hours debating contingencies before touching a die because they fear one surprise gotcha will blow the mission (`[read]` Blades; `[checked]` Sly Flourish, Alexandrian).

## 2. Six Ways Games Run a Camp Infiltration

| Approach | Core mechanic | Strengths | Weaknesses / Traps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SWADE Dramatic Tasks** `[checked]`/`[lead]` | Fixed rounds (3–5); collect **Task Tokens** (success = 1, raises = +1 each); **Clubs = Complication** (roll at -2 or suffer setback); supports **Mixed Encounters** (running alongside combat/support) | High tension, fast pacing, everyone acts or Supports, exploding dice create dramatic clutches, **layers seamlessly onto a tactical fight** | Feels abstract if tokens aren't tied to real camp stages; requires prepped complications; single-person math is tight without meta-currency |
| **D&D 4e Skill Challenges** `[read]`/`[checked]` | X successes before 3 failures; Complexity 1–5 | Gave tactical RPGs a template for non-combat encounters with real rewards | Famous traps: closed "skill menus," broken early DC math, **probability artifacts** (optional checks warping odds `[checked]` Alexandrian), awkward with spells, "fail = filler combat" (`[checked]` Sly Flourish) |
| **Draw Steel Montage Tests** `[read]` | Hard **2-round cap**; party-scaled Success/Failure limits (4 PCs: 4/4, 5/3, 6/2); **no repeating a skill**; spells/items = auto-successes; **Interludes** pause for quick fights; 3 outcomes (Total/Partial/Fail); feeds **Malice/Victories** | Fixes almost every 4e flaw; never drags; everyone participates; bridges directly into combat resources | Too brief if the players want a full tactical stealth caper with maps and guard patrols |
| **PF2e Infiltration** `[read]` | Sequential **Objectives** made of **Individual & Group Obstacles** (multiple paths); **Infiltration Points** vs. shared **Awareness Points** (+1 on fail, +2 crit fail, **+1/round**, **+1/round of loud combat**); Escalating Thresholds + Complications (can zoom into brief fights); **Opportunities** (*Smooth the Path*); pre-heist **Edge Points** | Purpose-built for camp/fortress infiltration; models noise from brief skirmishes; lets fast PCs help stuck allies; rewards scouting/prep without infinite planning | High GM prep; criticized on `r/Pathfinder2e` `[lead]` as feeling disconnected ("hollow") from combat abilities and character builds |
| **Blades in the Dark** `[read]` | **Engagement Roll** skips tedious approach -> Controlled/Risky/Desperate start; **Progress Clocks** named after *obstacles* (not methods); **Danger / Racing / Linked Clocks**; **Flashbacks** (spend Stress to retroactively prep) | Gold standard for heist pacing; kills planning paralysis; failure escalates Clocks instead of instant-ending stealth; any creative method works | High narrative abstraction; doesn't use tactical grid, cover, senses, or combat cards |
| **Adversary Roster + Tactical Grid** `[checked]` (Alexandrian, Angry GM) | Give players a camp **map + guard roster/schedule**; track **Awareness states** (Inattentive -> Alert -> Aware/Searching -> Alarm); resolve cat-and-mouse action by action | Maximum tactical depth; uses real positioning, light, cover, senses, and class tools; removing a patrol actually clears it from the roster | Slows to a crawl if run turn-by-turn across a huge camp; best paired with an abstract approach and zoomed in for the climax |

## 3. Coup de Grace & Stealth Assassination: Should It Work?

### Printed rules side by side

| System | Target required | Action & Roll | Effect | Design lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D&D 3.5e / PF1e** `[read]` | Helpless (asleep, bound, paralyzed, unconscious) | Full-round action (provokes AoO); auto-hit, auto-crit (+ sneak attack) | Damage + **Fortitude Save (DC 10 + damage) or die** | **Warning:** breaks combat via *Hold Person* / *Sleep*; feels awful when used on downed PCs |
| **D&D 4e** `[read]` | Adjacent Helpless | Standard action, any attack power; auto-crit on hit | **Dies instantly ONLY if damage >= Bloodied value (50% HP)** | **Smart filter:** auto-kills minions & weak guards, finishes wounded foes, almost never 1-shots a full-HP boss |
| **13th Age** `[read]` | Engaged Helpless | Give up Move + Quick; Standard attack (1 target) | **Auto-crit** (no save-or-die) | Heavy opening blow, zero instant-kill cheese |
| **D&D 5e / PF2e** `[read]`/`[lead]` | Unconscious | No Coup de Grace (5e: Adv + auto-crit in 5 ft; PF2e: -4 AC/Ref/Per, off-guard, wakes on damage) | Normal combat damage | Clean in combat, but causes the "12 damage to a sleeping throat" immersion break out of combat |
| **SWADE** `[lead]` | Unaware (*The Drop*) vs. Completely Helpless (*Finishing Move*) | *The Drop:* +4 hit/+4 dmg (+ Vigor or KO if Shaken). *Finishing Move:* 1 action, lethal weapon | *Finishing Move:* **automatic kill**, unless GM rules tough/armored/dramatic exception (Extras vs. Wild Cards) | Separating mooks (Extras) from bosses (Wild Cards) lets stealth kills work cleanly on guards while protecting climaxes |
| **Worlds Without Number** `[lead]` | Completely unsuspecting (*Execution Attack*) or helpless | **1 full minute uninterrupted prep** (any suspicion ruins it); ignores AC | **Max weapon damage + Physical Save (at -combat skill) or Mortally Wounded (0 HP)**; helpless adjacent = no roll | **Widely praised middle ground:** impossible mid-fight; the 1-minute setup *is* the stealth challenge; tough bosses get a save and still take max damage; PCs go to 0 HP (stabilizable), not instant-delete |

### The Community & Design Consensus: Five Rules That Make It Work

1. **Rank-gate silent takedowns (mooks vs. bosses).** Killing an isolated sentry silently should work cleanly (like SWADE Extras / 4e Minions / WWN). Bosses shouldn't die to a single Stealth check — they need either a **full encounter's worth of infiltration risk** or a **decisively tilted climax**.
2. **Never trigger Coup de Grace from combat conditions.** Keep it off `Prone`, `Immobilized`, `Dazed`, etc. Restrict it to genuinely helpless/sleeping targets *out of combat* (or an uninterrupted Execution setup like WWN's 1 minute).
3. **Make the whole party indispensable.** Structure the camp infiltration around **complementary roles** (distraction, disabling wards/alarms, handling beasts/locks, holding the escape route, covering sightlines) rather than forcing everyone to roll Stealth (`[checked]` RPG.SE, Sly Flourish; `[read]` PF2e obstacles, Draw Steel Montage).
4. **Make the Escape half the mission.** *"Killing someone is easy; the hard part is getting away with it"* (`[checked]` RPG.SE). Even if the leader dies silently in their tent, getting out past the waking camp (or collapsing the camp as panic spreads) gives the tank and frontliners their spotlight!
5. **Protect PCs asymmetrically (or structurally).** If an assassin sneaks into a PC camp, watches block Surprise, alarm clocks warn before execution, and PCs enter Dying/Wounds rather than instant deletion.

## 4. How This Applies to 2d12 (Observations)

2d12 **already has almost every mechanical hook** that other games have to bolt on awkwardly:

| Existing 2d12 piece | Where it lives | What it already solves for camp infiltration |
|---|---|---|
| **Alert (0–6)** | `book/ideas/expedition-loop.md` §2 | Already IS PF2e's Awareness Points / Blades' Danger Clock! Thresholds (`Calm 0–1 / Wary 2–3 / Alert 4–5 / Lockdown 6`) already escalate defenses, block Surprise at Alert 4+, and bring reinforcements on **Round 3** of noisy fights at Wary+. |
| **Momentum (+1)** | `02-turns.md`, `book/ideas/expedition-loop.md` §1 | Already awards +1 Momentum for overcoming an encounter via sneak or Challenge — so infiltrating isn't punished compared to fighting. |
| **70% Non-Enemy Loot** | `items-economy.md` Part 3 | Only 30% of treasure is monster loot (70% is found caches + quest rewards), so bypassing camp guards doesn't starve the party. |
| **Decided Fight (Leader Down)** | `book/ideas/expedition-loop.md` §3 | **Huge synergy:** *"The fight is decided when the enemies' side has lost half its CV, or its leader is Down."* Killing the camp leader stealthily naturally breaks/routs the camp instead of forcing a mop-up grind! |
| **NPC Ranks (Minor vs. Standard vs. Elite)** | `14-bestiary.md` | Already cleanly splits **Minor enemies** (Minions & Grunts) from **Standard** and **Elite** — a natural rank gate for silent takedowns vs. boss durability! |
| **2d12 Tiers + Exploding + Doubles** | `01-roll.md` | Directly powers Dramatic Tasks / Infiltration Points without inventing new dice: **Tier 1** = fail (+1 Alert / Complication); **Tier 2** = +1 Progress (with a wrinkle); **Tier 3 / Critical** = +2 Progress (or +1 and drop Alert / help an ally). |
| **Unconscious & Surprise** | `04-health.md`, `items-economy.md` Part 2 | Unconscious already makes everyone **Unsensed** to the sleeper (so attacker is **Favored** + **Prone** +1 Field Boon in melee + **Surprised**), and PCs roll on the Dying/Shattered Table while NPCs die at Down (natural PC protection!). *Note:* `decided.md` #13 leaves open whether sleeping = Unconscious — directly relevant here! |
| **Out-of-Combat Magic (Surge) & Retry Rule** | `decided.md` §2–3 | Out-of-combat spells spend **Surge** (refilled by Momentum) and *"open a way, never solve it"* (preventing spells from auto-skipping infiltrations); the **Retry Rule** + *"repeat an approach: 1 Bane"* (`roleplay-skills.md` #9) already stops skill spamming! |

### Three Concrete Ways 2d12 Could Handle "Infiltrate Camp & Kill Leader" (For Lucas to Weigh)

These are **design patterns from the research**, not rules proposals:

- **Pattern 1 — "The Infiltration *IS* the Encounter" (Draw Steel Montage / SWADE Dramatic Task / PF2e Infiltration):**
  Run a multi-stage Challenge (e.g., *Perimeter -> Inner Camp -> The Leader -> Escape*) racing **Progress Tokens** against the site's **Alert (0–6)** track. Every PC acts each round using different approaches, spells (spending Surge), consumables, or combat abilities (no repeating approaches; Tier 2 = 1 token, Tier 3 = 2 tokens, Tier 1 = +1 Alert + Complication). If they finish the Leader stage before Alert hits Lockdown (6), **the assassination succeeds outright** (earning Momentum +1 and throwing the camp into rout/escape) — they earned the kill by beating a full, risky encounter. If Alert triggers a Complication or Lockdown, it transitions seamlessly into a tactical fight (with Alert's current state + Escalation).
- **Pattern 2 — "Abstract Approach + Zoom-In Climax" (Hybrid — strongest fit for a tactical combat game):**
  1. **Approach (Infiltration Challenge / Dramatic Task):** Abstract the 30-guard camp into 2–3 rounds of obstacles (scouting, distracting patrols, silencing a sentry, bypassing wards) tracking **Alert (0–6)**. Successes strip away guards/CV, earn **Preparation (+1 Escalation)**, and let the party reach the leader's tent cleanly.
  2. **The Strike (Zoom to the Tactical Grid — 1–2 rounds):** Put the leader (and maybe 1–2 bodyguards, or 0 if lured away) on the map! The party starts **Hidden/Unsensed (Favored)**, **Surprised** target (unarmored / Unconscious if asleep), plus an **Execution / Coup de Grace** payoff (below). Because of 2d12's **Decided Fight** rule (Leader Down = camp breaks) and **Wary+ reinforcements only arriving on Round 3**, the players have a thrilling, high-stakes **2-round tactical puzzle**: *drop the leader fast and quiet before Round 3 brings the camp!* Uses every combat card, Action, AP, and Talent on their sheet!
  3. **Exfiltration (Parting Beat / Escape):** If Alert stayed low, slip away; if loud, a 1-round escape or Chase Challenge where the tank/frontliner holds the exit.
- **Pattern 3 — Coup de Grace / Execution Attack Rule (if added to Core Rules):**
  If Lucas wants a codified rule when a PC reaches a sleeping/helpless/unaware foe, three research-backed shapes fit 2d12:
  - **Shape A (Rank-gated + 4e Bloodied threshold):** Against a **helpless** (Unconscious/bound) creature with no active enemies in the fight, a **Minor enemy** (Minion/Grunt) dies automatically; a **Standard or Elite** takes a **Tier 3 / Critical** blow (or ignores Armor Defense), and dies outright *only* if the blow deals at least its **Vigor (Bloodied)** value (otherwise it wakes up Bloodied/Strained and Surprised).
  - **Shape B (WWN Execution Attack):** Requires uninterrupted setup (e.g., 1 minute undetected, or completely helpless) out of combat -> deals max damage + target rolls a **Body Save** (vs. Action DC) or goes **Down** immediately (killing an NPC; putting a PC on the Dying Table, not instant-deleting them!).
  - **Shape C (No Coup de Grace; Isolation + Surprise + Unarmored does the work):** A sleeping leader isn't wearing Heavy Armor (base AD 11!), is Prone (+1 Field Boon) and Unconscious (attacker Favored), and Surprised (loses surprise-round AP). A 4-PC ambush with Favored Strikes + Preparation (+1 Escalation) can realistically burst down a Standard or even an unarmored Elite in 1–2 rounds before reinforcements arrive on Round 3 — using 100% pure combat rules with zero special-case kill switches!
