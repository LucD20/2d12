# Talents: the design record

**Opened 2026-11-19.** Lucas: "Talents are the meat and likely the most important part. Research has to be thorough [...] Let's prepare as well as we can. Start." **Status: preparation. Nothing here is decided and nothing is in the book.** This file is the record of the Talents' design, the way `book/magic-design.md` is magic's: his words, what is already fixed, everything that waits on Talents, the research, the problems, the steps, then each decision as it is taken.

---

## 1. Lucas's words

- **2026-11-19, opening this piece:** "The talents is going to be the actual meat of the game, so far we have been doing the skeleton. Talents is going to be the main thing, how things work, how all of it comes together. How a diplomatic paladin differs from a quiet, ranged paladin, with similar strengths and capabilities. I don't want to fall into the trap of skill feats from 2e, or skill tax from 1e and 3.5, or competing talents like edges from Swade (why nobody really gets social edges instead of combat edges)."
- **2026-11-19:** "Talents are the meat and likely the most important part. Research has to be thorough, as many systems that uses this type of mechanic as possible: savage worlds, Star Wars (fantasy grounds) etc."
- **2026-11-15, the class redesign** (`class-charter.md`, rule 11): "the build is more Talents than class".
- **2026-11-18, magic** (magic record, decision 44): the base rule carries the limit, and a Talent is where a character grows past it. "Remember, progression is horizontal."
- **2026-11-18, magic** (decision 49): the classes got hard limits because they are beginner classes, "to start out"; "talents and such, will likely be 'rules altering' like spells as well in the long run."
- **2026-11-09, the structure question:** categories for each level (today's rule) or Talent points, "with the fear of powergamers hoarding".
- **2026-11-07, roleplay** (`roleplay-skills.md`): "it is a Roleplaying game, so this is a big part of the system." His dislikes there: Draw Steel's over-the-top out-of-combat features, Pathfinder 2e's skill feats.
- **2026-11-19, skills:** the system has no "trained" tick, "it was just modifiers"; the Attribute a skill uses can't be locked ("You want to intimidate with strength sometimes").

### His three traps, as tests for every Talent and for the structure

| The trap | From | The test |
|---|---|---|
| **Permission** | Pathfinder 2e's skill feats | Does this Talent grant leave to try something anyone should be able to try? |
| **Tax** | D&D 3.5 and Pathfinder 1e | Is this Talent paid only to keep a number usable, or to reach the one that was wanted? |
| **Competition** | Savage Worlds' Edges | Does a pick ever set a combat option against a non-combat one? |

### The system's standing tests (CLAUDE.md)

Fun, engaging, tactical, **the least ambiguity possible**; growth is **horizontal** (a level gives options, never a number on a roll or a DC); complex is fine, intuitive is required.

## 2. What is already fixed

### In the rulebook (`rules-src/05-characters.md`)

- **The level list's slots.** Level 1: a Skill Talent. Level 2: a Talent. Level 3: a Skill Talent. Level 4: a Stage Talent (and at the first level of Stages 2 and 3). **Every slot is empty:** "The Talents are set aside for now."
- "A Talent is a feature any character can take, apart from their class."
- "Levels give options; Stages change the numbers." Attributes don't grow with level or Stage. A Modifier is never above +4.
- **Swapping on a Rest:** every choice of the level list, Talents included, can be changed on a Rest for one that could have been made at that level.
- Effects of the same kind don't stack.

### In the set-aside chapter (`rules-src/set-aside/16-talents.md`; out of the book since the class redesign)

- A Talent has **a category** (Combat, Mobility, Magic, Skill), **a level** and sometimes **Prerequisites** (a Talent, a level, "an Action with a trait you have", an Attribute). Chains existed (Brawler, then Kickboxer).
- **"Talents That Modify Actions":** a Talent names the Actions it changes by their traits (Heavy Blows: "[Melee, Strike, Weapon] Actions with a Two-handed weapon").
- Twenty cards: twelve Combat, two Mobility, four Magic (all Divine), Class Expertise. Deprecated with the old classes; their prices and reasons are in `options-talents.md`.

### In the class charter (`class-charter.md`)

- A class is a fixed shape (about 18 cards at Stage 0): 2 Features, Hallmark cards, Arts, Escalation abilities, Picks, the Apex.
- **Rule 11:** a card that counts "your Actions" names a trait, never all of them, because the build is more Talents than class. The Paladin's glue is [Divine]; "a later Talent can carry the trait to work with the class's cards".

### The earlier proposal (rules-backlog, "TALENT STRUCTURE: PROPOSAL", 2026-11-09; never answered)

Two tracks that never compete (a Talent and a Skill pick each level; Skill Talents never give combat numbers); Minor and Major Talents (a level's Talent is one Major or two Minor); ranks I to III; Stage Talents as milestones; retraining one pick at each level-up; to avoid: one currency for skill and combat, trees with filler. A short research pass stood behind it.

### The frame of play outside a fight (magic record, decisions 113 to 118; 2026-11-19)

- Anyone can attempt anything plausible; a skill makes you better and never grants permission (`roleplay-skills.md`, proposals 1 and 2).
- **Routine:** if 11 + your total skill modifier is at least the DC, with no threat and no time pressure, you do it without rolling. No "trained" tick.
- One guide for a failed Check. No help Action outside a fight; in a fight, help gives 1 Support Boon.
- Five kinds of outcome belong to a Check: getting past, swaying people, finding out, enduring and travelling, making and mending.
- **For the Skills pass:** how a skill's number is made (his five directions) and that its Attribute can't be locked.

### Magic's side (magic record)

- A Magic Talent or a class **opens** a Path and a School (27). Talents grant spells and change spells by trait; **they never give ranks** (17).
- Paths and Schools are traits a Talent can key on ("your [Fire] spells...", "your [Evocation] spells...").
- Every PC has Surge; only spells spend it for now; the classes get their uses "at their next pass" (68).

## 5. The problems to design against

### Lucas's three traps, with their causes on record

| Trap | Its causes, by the research | What 2d12 already has against it |
|---|---|---|
| **Permission** | The sentence "anyone can try; this makes you better" not printed on the pick; **a list so long each pick needs ground, taken from the base rule**; a GM made to remember a penalty for everyone without the pick | `roleplay-skills.md` proposal 1 (the sentence Pathfinder didn't print); Routine is a base rule, not a Talent |
| **Tax** | A pick that restores a number the math assumes; a rung used only to reach the next; a pick everyone of a kind takes first; buying back a penalty; a forced order of upgrades | Flat numbers, the +4 cap, no "trained" tick, no rising DCs |
| **Competition** | One budget, and the fight is where you die; **the non-combat pick is a number while the combat pick is an action**; the skill alone already does the job; the circumstance never comes up; the leak: actions usable in a fight in the non-combat slot | Typed slots in the level list; separate spell picks (magic, 120) |

### Four more, from the research

- **Memory.** Conditional and off-turn triggers are what players forget; a wider sheet nobody can remember is the risk of horizontal growth. The class charter already says "no silent bonuses, no tracking across turns".
- **Filler.** More picks than good ideas, a fixed number of boxes, a single node everybody races to. "We don't think it's humanly possible to have 40-50 fun, interesting and balanced talents in a tree" (Street).
- **Dominated options.** Two picks that can be put on one scale; one of them wins.
- **Sameness.** Two characters of a class end up alike (Genesys's recorded worry). Lucas's own test: a diplomatic Paladin against a quiet, ranged one.

### Conflicts already on file

- **The set-aside chapter is Savage Worlds' Edge structure almost item for item** (categories, a level, Prerequisites, chains); its example Heavy Blows is a number.
- **Retraining:** any Rest (Chapter 5) against one swap at each level-up (the 2026-11-09 proposal).
- **"Skill Talents never give combat numbers"** (the proposal) is narrower than the leak the research found (actions used in a fight).
- **"No certainty"** (`roleplay-skills.md`) against the research's finding that loved non-combat options are reliable (Routine is one answer already taken).

## 6. The steps (one decision at a time; the order is Claude's proposal)

| # | The step | What is decided |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **What a Talent is for** | What a Talent may contain: a new verb, a rule exception, a lifted limit, a number? The rule every Talent obeys |
| **2** | **The budgets** | The tracks and what may sit in each: the wall on content (what a non-combat Talent may never do in a fight, and the reverse) |
| **3** | **What the class fixes and what Talents decide** | The line between the class's cards and the Talents, so a diplomatic and a ranged Paladin differ by Talents and share the class |
| **4** | **The size of a pick** | One size, or Minor and Major; ranks inside a Talent (three written steps) or not |
| **5** | **How picks are gated and found** | A list with prerequisites, trees, a pyramid, paths, a small hand drawn by trait; prerequisites by trait, level, Stage |
| **6** | **The schedule** | How many picks, at which levels, of which kind (the level list's slots are in the book and empty) |
| **7** | **Retraining** | Any Rest, one at a level-up, or another rule |
| **8** | **The memory budget** | How many live Talents and triggers a character carries at each Stage |
| **9** | **Skill Talents** | What they do, by the frame of play outside a fight (Routine, the five kinds of outcome, no help action); with the Skills pass |
| **10** | **Magic Talents and the seeds** | How the ~30 seeds already assigned become Talents (opening a Path, the counterspell tree, the summoner's chain, the classes' Surge uses) |
| **11** | **Writing and pricing** | A template for a Talent card, a price method, how many at launch, the filler test |
| **12** | **The test** | Build the two Paladins and a few others on paper; an independent audit against the tests in `research/talents.md` sections 6 and 7 |

Steps 1 to 3 are the skeleton of Talents; steps 4 to 8 its structure; 9 to 12 its content.

## 7. Decisions

| # | Decided | Lucas's words |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | **What a Talent may contain: never a bare number.** A Talent adds a verb (a new Action, or a new way to use one), changes a rule, or lifts a limit of the base rules. A number may appear only as part of a verb (a new Action's damage), never as "+1 to X". **A fallback, not the default:** a Boon in a very circumstantial situation ("a boon when haggling") may be allowed from time to time, only if Talents without it don't hold up in play; he would rather not. | "A, but I think it can grant boons to very circumstantial situations from time to time, though I'd like it not to. This is just an idea in case Talents without it doesn't really hold up, like a boon when haggling." |
