# What is decided: magic and Talents (2026-11-18 and 19)

**The one page to read.** Everything Lucas decided in the magic brainstorm and the Talents design, in short, with nothing that is only an assumption or still open; those are in section 5. **Nothing here is in the rulebook,** except one sentence of Chapter 7. The full reasons and his words: `book/magic-design.md` (decisions 1 to 128), `book/magic-out-of-combat.md`, `book/talents-design.md` (T1 to T10). The rules of magic in reading order: `book/magic-rules-draft.md`. A number in brackets is the record's decision.

---

## 1. Magic in a fight

**What a spell is.** A Power Action with [Spell], one Path and one School, and a minimum rank in each ("Fire 2 • Evocation 1"); rarely two Paths, never three [5, 63, 100]. Eight Paths (Fire, Air, Water, Earth, Astral, Death, Nature, Glamour), each with a usual damage subtype and a usual condition that is only a writer's guide [23, 64, 91]. Astral's subtypes are Force and Psychic; Glamour deals **false damage** (Nonlethal Resolve damage, removed when its caster goes Down, the rest at the encounter's end; no subtype; **never made Lethal**) [95, 105; Lucas, 2026-11-19]. Six Schools, sorted in a fixed order: Conjuration, Abjuration, Enchantment, Evocation, then Alteration or Thaumaturgy [65]. The Paladin's Divine is not magic; magic and the Divine meet like any two effects [12, 94]. The spell cards written so far are placeholders [84].

**Getting magic.** A Magic Talent or a class opens a Path and a School together, rank 1 each, with two spells [27]. Mastery: one point an encounter (won, normal or harder) into a single thing you used, a weapon, a Path or a School [75]. Ranks at 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 Mastery; **rank 6 is the cap** [27, 105]. A new spell at each new rank; **each spell pick is paired: one for fights, one for outside a fight** [120]. A rank is never a number on a roll or a DC; Talents never give ranks [17, 18].

**Casting.** A card prints [Handle] (a free hand), [Incanted] (you must be able to speak) or [Focus] (concentration) when it needs one [77, 78, 85, 105]. A casting is always noticed, who and on whom, unless the spell is [Subtle] [97, 100]. You know a spell's Path, its School, or the spell itself if you meet its ranks in them [97, 98]. Nothing counters a casting: a counterspell is a Talent [99]. **A spell takes the ranged rules only if it prints [Ranged]** [108]. A Save spell: the caster's Boons and Banes change its **Save DC** (a set number in place of 2d12), including what applies to one creature; **a target hidden from the caster lowers it for that target, unless the spell is an [Area]** (Chapter 1's "your Senses don't change a Save Action" goes); where the roll would be Doomed, the Saves are Favored [88, 100, 109; Lucas, 2026-11-19]. A [Handle] Reaction spell can't be cast with full hands [103].

**Armor.** Medium: start an encounter with 1 less Surge. Heavy: the same, and your first spell each turn costs 1 more Surge. Only in fights; never below 0 at the start [80, 89, 100].

**Surge** (unplayed). You start an encounter with Surge equal to your Escalation; +1 at the start of each round after the first, +1 whenever anything else raises your Escalation; none while Unconscious; gone at the end. An ordinary spell costs none; a stronger spell prints 1 to 3 beside its AP; each stretch of Overcast costs 1. Only spells spend it for now; the classes get uses later. An NPC caster gains 1 a round [66 to 73, 90, 100]. The word Momentum stays [66].

**Overcast.** A spell's Overcast line; each thing bought on it is a **stretch**. A stretch needs one spare rank. Three shared ladders at 1 Surge, paid by spare School rank: **Carry** (+2 squares), **Fork** (+1 creature), **Echo** (+1 round). A spell's own stretches, paid by spare Path rank, print their price. The Surge spent on Overcast in one casting is at most Escalation minus 1. An [Area] has no Fork. **Merge is cut** [26, 43, 61, 67, 91, 104, 110, 111].

**Overreach.** Only a spell that prints the line; once a casting, chosen before the roll, paid even on a miss, **paid after the casting and its Reactions resolve**; the card prints the whole price, usually Nonlethal Resolve damage nothing reduces; a line may pass one limit once, and says so [57 to 59, 105].

**Held magic.** [Woven]: one at a time. [Sustained]: Sustain 1 AP at the start of your turn. Both end on Down, by choice, at the encounter's end, and when you become Bloodied or Strained [28, 29].

**Summons.** One Summon spell for each Path; one summon at a time; a Minion of your Stage, 3 AP, no Reactions, Sustain 1 AP, its own turn after yours; built on a Rest; leaves on Down, an unpaid Sustain or the spell's end. It fights beside you like any ally and never counts for Escalation, Mastery, Momentum, or any rule that counts creatures going Down or becoming Bloodied or Strained [41, 44, 62, 92, 105].

**No limit across a fight** beyond AP, Frequency and Surge, on watch [60]. The high-Momentum opening is accepted, on watch [74].

## 2. Magic outside a fight

- **The stance:** magic changes what is possible and never does what a skill does; **it opens a way, it never solves it**; the Check that follows belongs to whoever is best suited. No bonus to a Check from magic [114, 118].
- **A spell with a duration is combat only.** Outside, an instant battle spell can be cast for its Surge [87].
- **Surge outside a fight** is a pool equal to the party's Momentum; it refills whenever the party gains Momentum; it is cut down when Momentum drops [71, 86, 105].
- **A spell outside a fight is a written card** with a Path or a School (or both) and a minimum rank; **10 minutes to cast** unless it says otherwise; a Surge cost of 0 to 3; always noticed unless [Subtle]; it works with a printed limit that leaves the obstacle standing, and may name a skill's Check to do better; it may print its own stretch with a price, and a line a higher rank unlocks. No Overcast ladders there. Written in three scales: how far (touch, 10 squares, within sight), how many, how long (10 minutes, an hour, until your next Sleep); the special steps (a place you have been; a room; it stays) only where a card says [119, 123 to 127].
- **Each Path gives free small magic** that never changes a Check and never matters in a fight [119].
- **The two words outside:** a Path works only on its own stuff; each School has an edge (Evocation: nothing lasts, loud; Alteration: what can be attempted, never how well; Enchantment: never a number in a fight; Conjuration: what it brings can't fight; Thaumaturgy: never decides for a mind; Abjuration: warns or bars, never harms) [123].
- **The scene-breaking families** each exist in a form that opens a way (speaking with the dead, truth, finding, charm, domination, travel, shelter, food and light, hiding, opening); a [Subtle] form of truth and thoughts can exist at a higher rank; once and partial gives a lead, repeatable and certain ends a scene; the few that could skip a whole scene are **rare**, handed out by the GM [128].
- **The Limits of Magic:** magic doesn't restore Health (Guard only; spells that remove Wounds come with the Wounds tables); it doesn't take a whole turn from a PC or a major enemy; magic that lasts needs a fight (battle spells). **Bringing back the dead is to exist,** heavily limited, likely once [36, 39, 87, 93, 101, 105].

## 3. Play outside a fight (fixed with magic; the Skills pass inherits it)

- **Routine:** if 11 + your total skill modifier **meets** the task's DC, with no threat and no time pressure, you do it without rolling; **a Tier 2 at most**. The system has no "trained" tick [115; Lucas, 2026-11-19].
- **A failed Check:** Tier 1 no, or yes at a real cost; Tier 2 yes, but; Tier 3 yes, and more. A casting always spends its Surge and is always noticed [116].
- **No help Action outside a fight:** the one best suited makes the Check; what the others do changes the task or is its own Check. **In a fight, a help Basic Action gives 1 Support Boon, no roll** [117].
- **Five kinds of outcome belong to a Check,** as a guide: getting past, swaying people, finding out, enduring and travelling, making and mending [118].
- **A retry rule:** a failed attempt at a task can't be repeated until the situation changes or you try something different; a different way of reaching the goal is always open (wording to come).
- **The attempt belongs to the party (Lucas, 2026-11-19: "A is good"):** when a Check fails, nobody may make the same attempt until the situation changes; a different way is always open. **The party's Check is rolled by the PC with the highest modifier for it.** An adventure writes it as "While in this room, PCs can make a DC 13 Athletics **Party Check**" (**named the Lead Check**, Lucas, 2026-11-19; a group check that everyone rolls may become another mechanic). **When two PCs have the same highest modifier, the players choose who rolls**. **Low stakes:** a task with nothing at stake needs no roll, but **a GM may still ask for one** (his: "Sometimes, we want locked chests to exist to hold some extra loot, just you know, so people have that dungeoneer feeling"), usually with time as its price, or with the loot as an extra on a success. Research: `research/party-retries.md`.
- **Skill numbers grow on their own track, never from the Skill Talent slot** [T7].

## 4. Talents (`book/talents-design.md`)

- **T1** A Talent never gives a bare number: it adds a verb, changes a rule or lifts a limit. (A circumstantial Boon is a fallback only.)
- **T2** A Skill Talent gives nothing usable during an encounter; it may shape what happens before one through the preparation rules. A combat Talent may have uses outside a fight. **Nothing simply lets you bypass:** no use outside a fight repeatable without limit; a lasting cost never cleared by quick, free attempts.
- **T3** The class fixes the role; Talents decide the style; any character can take any Talent; no Talent is written for one class. **Classes grow by their own upgrades at each Stage** ("Select X upgrades": skeleton examples Smite's dice, Smite with an area, a new 2 AP Smite, Judge on two targets).
- **T4** One size of pick, with up to three written ranks; each rank worth a pick by itself; never a bigger number.
- **T5** Talents sit in named groups (Archery, Diplomacy...); a Talent requires only a minimum Stage and the trait it works on; no Talent requires another except its own ranks.
- **T6** One pick a level: a Skill Talent on odd levels, a Talent on even ones; seven Talents and eight Skill Talents by level 15, plus the class's upgrades (fallback: both every level). **No Talent is to be the obvious pick of its Stage.**
- **T7** Skill numbers never come from the Skill Talent slot.
- **T8** Free retraining in Stage 0 (the tutorial) for every choice; after Stage 0, its own mechanic, for Talents, Skill Talents and the class's choices alike.
- **T9** No memory rule: Talents as wide as can be (new Reactions, triggers, specialties: an Opportunity Attack that triggers on [Spell] Actions, that disrupts one). **A Talent never changes a class's own Actions or Reactions:** that is the class upgrades' job.
- **T10** A class card keyed on a trait reaches every Action with that trait, Talents included. A trait Talents key on must be carried by more than one source. **A class's own cards carry the class's name as a trait** ([Paladin, Divine]); Hallowed Stance is to key on [Paladin, Divine].
- **T11 (to confirm)** Ranks may branch: at a rank a card may offer up to three branches, each a new rule, drawn as a small tree on the card.
- **T12** A rank is a pick, never a gift of the Stage; a rank may write a minimum Stage as a gate. Deepen or widen at each Talent level.
- **T13** Every rank is worth a whole pick (checked against an average Rank I); a Talent has as many ranks as it needs, none to two.

## 5. Questions still to ask

**Answered 2026-11-19:** Routine meets the DC, a Tier 2 at most; false damage is never Lethal; a hidden target lowers a Save DC unless the spell is an [Area]; **a lower cost in a Talent is not allowed by T1** (Lucas: "Not really, no"): the seeds that only lower a cost (a cheaper Sustain, a smaller Overreach die, a lighter armor cost) are out as written.

**Small, still open:**
4. The retry rule: can another character make the same attempt? Lucas, 2026-11-19: "This requires extensive search". His points: a failed attempt must mean something and never stop the story; it needs a drawback where the task matters (the villain prepares, the noise alerts others, time is lost); a low-stakes task (a locked chest) has none, so anyone can just try again; group rolls don't fit a chest; "everyone rolls" is easy but dull; "Tier 1 means failure and nobody can try again" is decent; in a social scene a retry needs good faith and a changed situation. Research: `research/party-retries.md`. And: is "the GM decides" a separate exit, or the judge of whether the situation changed?
5. **Answered (Lucas, 2026-11-19): a Lingering may have no subtype after all** (it replaces decision 105's "every Lingering writes its subtype"). An untyped Lingering is the default "bleed", and the rule says what it is for any body: "this can mean bleeding for a living creature with blood, the sap of a plant, the parts of an elemental creature... What matters is that it affects any creature that doesn't have Immune Lingering" (his words; the cure for Pathfinder 2e's "how does an ooze bleed?"). It counts as its own kind for one-Lingering-a-subtype (82) and works like any damage. **Resist, Weak and Immune can name Lingering** ("Resist Lingering 5") and then cover every Lingering; **when two Resists cover the same Lingering, the highest applies, one only** (his example: Resist Lingering 5 and Resist Fire 10 against an 8 Fire Lingering: Fire 10 applies). Claude's reading, to confirm: the same for two Weaks; a Resist and a Weak that both cover it each apply once. Wounding Shot (the Archer's) keeps its untyped Lingering. **How Resist, Weak and Immune are written (Lucas, 2026-11-19): the word first, then each subtype with its number,** the same for one or several: **Weakness Fire 2**; **Weakness Fire 2, Holy 3**; on a stat block one line each (**Resistance** Fire 5, Cold 2 · **Weakness** Holy 5 · **Immunity** Poison, Lingering); in a rule, "You gain Resistance Fire 2, Holy 5"; a rule that names the kind with no number reads "a creature with Fire Weakness". Replaces Chapter 1's "Resist 2 Fire, Weak 5 Holy, Immune Fire".
