## 4. Rules questions and Claude's answers

**R1. The cover picture (Lucas: "The line is passing through an ally, shouldn't it be Low cover? ... No wall, it's more about Kaela. Does Sylwen
ignore Low cover from allies?")**
- **What the book says** (Chapter 3, Cover): a creature gives Low cover only when it is on **the line from the center of your square to the
  center of the target's space**. The four corner lines only count walls and objects: "Creatures never count as blocked corners."
- **In the picture:** the line through Kaela is a **corner line** (Sylwen to the Ogre's top-left corner). The center line (the dashed white one)
  passes just below Kaela's square. So by the book: no cover, and Foundry's "No cover: 0 blocked" is RAW. Foundry checks it that way
  (`module/cover.mjs`: only the center line looks for creatures).
- **Sylwen doesn't ignore it:** "Your allies give your targets no Cover" is **Clear Lines**, an Archer Escalation Passive of **level 2**. At
  level 1 she has no such rule; here no rule was needed.
- **The question for Lucas:** is that the rule you want? It is unambiguous, but an ally standing in front of one corner gives nothing. The
  alternative ("a creature on any corner line gives Low cover") gives Low cover much more often (and makes Clear Lines worth more).

**R2. Minion squads (checklist minion-squad, FAIL: "I made 3 goblins, it put the three in one single group ... with 3 goblins, it should create
1 with 2 goblins, 1 with 1").** **A conflict with the book:** Chapter 14 says "the fewest squads that hold them, of sizes as close as possible"
with **up to 3** a squad, so three Minions are one squad of three, and Foundry did that. Lucas wants 2 + 1. Lucas: no rule, a recommendation (section 3, item 5). Also the Initiative window can only move a Minion between the squads that exist: with one squad there is nowhere to move
it. It needs a way to start a new squad.

**R3. Warn, don't block (Lucas, twice: the token HUD on a used Action; checklist g2-darting: "dont prevent it. Shows a warning on the Roll
dialog. In fact, we should deprecate most of those yellow warnings and add them to where they actually matter: Roll dialog").** A principle for
the whole system: **when an Action breaks a rule, the system warns where you decide (the roll dialog's notices) and lets you go on; it doesn't
stop you with a yellow notification.** The system has **174** yellow warnings (`ui.notifications.warn`). They need an audit, sorted in three
kinds:
1. **Blocks to turn into dialog warnings:** a used Action, a weapon that doesn't fit (Darting Blade), out of range, no AP...
2. **True impossibilities that stay:** no token selected, no target at all, a missing item.
3. **Information to drop or move into the chat.**

Lucas: nothing else stays blocked (section 3, item 6).

**R4. Charge ending on an ally's space:** against the rules (no creature ends a move in an occupied square). A bug: section 5.

**R5. Something found in the log:** both Paladins forgot to Judge when initiative was rolled. The Situational tab could offer Judge (0A) at the
start of the encounter.

## 5. Foundry: bugs (playtest and checklist), grouped

**F1. Effects still wait for Tier 2 (cause found; wider than the report).** Darting Blade's Tier 1 showed neither its Evade nor its Effect line.
The book removed "Effects only on Tier 2+" (rules-backlog "EFFECTS HAPPEN ON TIER 2+ IS REMOVED"), but Foundry's roll code still applies an
Action's Effects (`fx.all`) and its after-chain only on Tier 2 or 3 (`module/actions.mjs` lines 1531, 1600, 1606). **Every card with an Effect
line is affected.**

**F2. Charge**
- a. Let you change the target while the "Charge X squares" path is shown.
- b. Show it in the HUD's Situational tab.
- c. Clicking Weapon Attack from it opens the item sheet instead of attacking.
- d. It lets you end on an ally's space (R4).

**F3. Movement**
- a. Rewinding a move in the Reactions chat card doesn't refund the movement.
- b. A Large token pays for difficult terrain only when its center crosses a creature, not for every square of its space that passes one.
- c. The chat card's "Pushed 2" button did nothing.

**F4. The HUD and the combat strip**
- a. The Reaction pip has the old AP fill: hard to see.
- b. Remove "Bloodied" and "Dying" from the strip.
- c. The token HUD blocks a used Action: warn instead (R3).
- d. The Minions' "which one goes now" in the Situational tab shows the token pictures, the name on hover. This is a new hover text: Lucas asked, so
  t264's list gets it.

**F5. Rolls and the chat**
- a. Darting Blade without a Light weapon: allowed, with a warning in the roll dialog (g2-darting; R3).
- b. **chat-follow** (FAIL): after Damage, then Apply, on a plain Weapon Attack the card grew and the chat didn't scroll with it.
- c. Shield of Faith: the Passive's line names its source (section 3, item 3).

**F6. Minion squads:** the recommended split by default (one squad for every 2 Minions, rounded up) and a "new squad" choice in the
Initiative window (section 3, item 5).

**F7. The cover lines on the map** (R1): draw the center line strong and mark a creature it crosses (that is the one that gives Low cover);
the corner lines fainter, there only for walls and objects.
