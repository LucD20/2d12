# Chapter 6: Basic Actions

Every creature can use these Actions.

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Advance</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You press forward, one deliberate step at a time.

*Move • Self*

**Frequency:** At-will

| 1 AP |
|---|
| When you use this Action, you move up to your Speed in squares. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Charge</span><span>2 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You close the distance in one straight, reckless line.

*Move • Self*

**Frequency:** Round

**Requirements:** You have at least Speed 3.

| 2 AP |
|---|
| Advance at least 3 squares and up to your Speed in a straight line. You can't Charge through Difficult Terrain (an unwilling creature's space is Difficult Terrain), even if you have movement left: the Charge ends in the last **empty** square where the line is legal. At the end of your movement, make a Weapon Attack (1A) with 1 Boon against a creature of your choice. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Crawl</span><span>1 / 2 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You drag yourself along the ground.

*Move • Self*

**Frequency:** At-will

| 1 AP | 2 AP |
|---|---|
| You move 1 square. | **Requirements:** Your Speed is 5 or higher. You move 3 squares. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Evade</span><span>1 / 2 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You slip through the fray without giving anyone an opening.

*Self*

**Frequency:** At-will

| 1 AP | 2 AP |
|---|---|
| Evade 1. | Evade half your Speed (rounded down). |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Fists</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** When there is nothing else in your hands, there are always your hands.

*Strike • Weapon*

**Frequency:** At-will

**Target:** One creature

**Distance:** Weapon

**Vs:** AD

**Requirements:** You have a free hand.

| | 1 AP |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | 0 |
| **Tier 2** | [B] 1[W] + [Mod] |
| **Tier 3** | [B] 1[W] + [Mod] |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Haul</span><span>1 / 2 / 3 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You snag them and drag them in.

*Appeal • Body • Handle • Strike*

**Frequency:** Round

**Target:** One creature

**Distance:** Reach

**Vs:** Appeal or Body (the one used to roll)

**Requirements:** Your Reach is 2 or more. The target's SR is at most 1 higher than yours and is at least 2 squares away. The target must remain within your Reach throughout the whole movement.

| | 1 AP | 2 AP | 3 AP |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | No effect. | No effect. | Pulled 1. |
| **Tier 2** | Pulled 1. | Pulled 2. | Pulled 3. |
| **Tier 3** | Pulled 2. | Pulled 4. | Pulled 5. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Interact</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You pull the lever, open the door, grab what you need.

*Handle*

**Frequency:** At-will

**Distance:** Reach

| 1 AP |
|---|
| You do one of the following: draw an object you carry into a free hand; stow an object you hold (put it away on your person, such as sheathing a sword); swap two objects (stow the one you hold and draw another, in the same Action); grab objects within Reach, as many as you have free hands; help a Prone creature within Reach stand up; help an Imperiled creature within Reach get to safety; wake an Unconscious creature within Reach; open a door, chest or something similar. This Action is broader than most: it can be used for similar things in the world. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Leaning</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You lean out from cover, just enough to see.

*Self*

**Frequency:** At-will

| 1 AP |
|---|
| Until the end of your turn, you calculate cover from one of your corners instead of the center of your square, and your own cover against other creatures' Actions is reduced by 1 level. You can stop Leaning at any time on your turn, at no cost. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Regrip</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You shift your hands on the grip, and the weapon sits where you want it.

*Self*

**Frequency:** At-will

| 1 AP |
|---|
| You do one of the following: hold a weapon you hold in one hand with both hands (your other hand must be free); move a weapon from one hand to the other; or swap the weapons in your two hands. Regrip doesn't have the Handle trait: it doesn't need a free hand. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Release</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You let it go.

*Self*

**Frequency:** At-will

| 0 AP |
|---|
| You drop an object in your square, or let go of an object you hold with one hand (a two-handed weapon is then held with one hand). |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Reload</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You work the mechanism and seat a fresh bolt.

*Weapon • Handle*

**Frequency:** At-will

**Target:** One target

**Distance:** Reach

**Vs:** DC 13

**Requirements:** You or an ally within Reach is wielding an unloaded weapon.

| | 1 AP |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Your next Reload attempt gains 2 Boons. |
| **Tier 2** | You Reload your weapon. Then you can Regrip (1A) to hold the weapon with two hands, or the ally can. |
| **Tier 3** | You Reload your weapon and the weapon's wielder can Evade 1. Then you can Regrip (1A) to hold the weapon with two hands, or the ally can. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Relocate</span><span>1 / 2 / 3 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You take them by the arm and put them where you want them.

*Body • Handle • Instinct • Strike*

**Frequency:** Round

**Target:** One creature

**Distance:** Reach

**Vs:** Body or Instinct (the one used to roll)

**Requirements:** The target's SR is at most 1 higher than yours. The target must remain within your Reach throughout the whole movement.

| | 1 AP | 2 AP | 3 AP |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | No effect. | No effect. | Reposition 1. |
| **Tier 2** | Reposition 1. | Reposition 2. | Reposition 3. |
| **Tier 3** | Reposition 2. | Reposition 4. | Reposition 5. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Shove</span><span>1 / 2 / 3 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You put your weight behind it and drive them back.

*Body • Handle • Strike*

**Frequency:** Round

**Target:** One creature

**Distance:** Reach

**Vs:** Body

**Requirements:** The target's SR is at most 1 higher than yours.

| | 1 AP | 2 AP | 3 AP |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Tier 2** | Pushed 1. | Pushed 2. | Pushed 3. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Stand</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You get your feet back under you.

*Move • Self*

**Frequency:** At-will

| 1 AP |
|---|
| You stand up: you are no longer Prone. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Steel Yourself</span><span>2 / 3 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You grit your teeth and find a little more to give.

*Self*

**Frequency:** Round

| 2 AP | 3 AP |
|---|---|
| You use 1 Rally Point. | You use 2 Rally Points. |

You can't use this Action while Down (Desperate, Dying or Shattered) or Unconscious.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Trip</span><span>1 / 2 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** A hooked foot, a twist, and they hit the ground.

*Body • Handle • Instinct • Strike*

**Frequency:** Round

**Target:** One creature

**Distance:** Reach

**Vs:** Body or Instinct (the one used to roll)

**Requirements:** The target's SR is at most 1 higher than yours.

| | 1 AP | 2 AP |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | No effect. | No effect. |
| **Tier 2** | No effect. | Prone. |
| **Tier 3** | Prone. | [B] 1d8 Bl; Prone. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Weapon Attack</span><span>1 AP</span><span class="cat">Basic Action</span></div>

**Flavor:** You strike with the weapon in your hands.

*Strike • Weapon*

**Frequency:** At-will

**Target:** One creature

**Distance:** Weapon

**Vs:** AD

You attack with the weapon you are wielding. Weapon Attack takes the Melee or Ranged trait of that weapon, and its Range.

| | 1 AP |
|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | 0 |
| **Tier 2** | [B] 1[W] + [Mod] |
| **Tier 3** | [B] 1[W] + [Mod] |

</div>
