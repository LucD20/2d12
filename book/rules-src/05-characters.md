# Chapter 5: Characters

## Building a Character

1. **Attributes.** All four Attributes (Body, Mind, Appeal, Instinct) start at **0**.
2. **Species.** Apply your species' Attribute changes (the species are in the [[Species]] document). It also gives you a **Vigor** and a **Resolve** bonus, a **Speed**, your **Reach** (1), your **Steadiness** and your **Size** (all current species are Medium (3)).
3. **Class.** Apply your class' Attribute increases. It also gives you a **high** and a **low Health value** (and the steps they grow by at each Stage), a **Rally** value, a **Speed Modifier**, a **Weapon Modifier** and a **Power Modifier**. Your Speed is your species' Speed plus your class' Speed Modifier.
4. **Choices.** When a species or class says "or", pick one. A class's choices between two Attributes must be different from each other (see [[Distinct Choices]]). If your class lets you, choose which of its two Modifier values is your Weapon Modifier and which your Power Modifier (see [[Weapon, Power and Signature Modifiers]]).
5. **[[Health Pool]].** Assign your class's high Health value to Vigor or to Resolve and the low one to the other, then add your species' bonuses (neither can be under a third of the total). Your Health Pool is the two added together.
6. **[[Rally Points]]** = your class' Rally value + 1 (it grows with your [[Stages|Stage]]).
7. **Level 1.** Take what your class's level list gives at level 1: its Features, its **Hallmark Action** and **Hallmark Reaction** (a choice of two each), an **Art**, and your **Escalation 2** ability (see [[Leveling Up]]). Weapons and armor are in the [[Gear]] document.

## Classes

Your class gives you Attribute increases, a **high** and a **low Health value** with their Stage steps (used for your Health Pool), a **Rally** value (used for Rally Points), a **Speed Modifier**, a **Weapon Modifier** and a **Power Modifier**, an **[[Armor Training]]**, and a list of **Escalation** abilities.

When you pick your class, you choose its Attribute increases when they say "or", and the order of its Modifiers if it lets you. The Attribute choices can't be changed later; the rest can, for a while (see [[Swapping on a Rest]]).

Each class is written in its own document: [[Paladin]] and [[Archer]].

### Class Actions, Picks and the Repertoire

Over its first levels, every class gives you the same kinds of things:

- A **Hallmark Action**: a choice of two Actions that are the class's fantasy; you take one, and the other is not yours. A Hallmark Action doesn't need the [[Signature]] trait: that trait only sets the Modifier an Action rolls.
- A **Hallmark Reaction**: a choice of two Reactions, the same way.
- An **Art**: a small Action with three **Modes** (see [[Reading an Action]]), for the things that are neither damage nor healing (at most a Modifier's damage, as Trick Shots). A class offers three; you take one at level 1 and a second at level 3.
- An **Encounter Pick**.
- The **Escalation** abilities: one for each of Escalation 2, 4 and 6, then the Apex (see [[Escalation Abilities]]).
- Its **Features**.

A class's **Class Actions** are the Actions it gives you at level 1, the Hallmark Action and the Hallmark Reaction among them. Its **Repertoire** is everything else it offers, listed by [[Frequency]]: **Passive** (it works on its own), **Turn**, **Round**, **Encounter** and **Daily**. A **Pick** is one choice from the Repertoire: the level list says how many Picks you take at each level and from which list (an Encounter Pick comes from the Encounter list; a Pick "from any list" can come from any of them). Two classes' lists don't need to match: each offers what fits it.

### Leveling Up

Each class has a **level list**: what you gain at each level (Features, Class Actions, Picks and Escalation abilities). When you reach a new level, you take what its entry gives you. **Levels give options; Stages change the numbers** (below).

The first three levels have the same shape for every class:

| Level | You gain |
|---|---|
| **1** | Your class's Features, a Hallmark Action, a Hallmark Reaction, an Art, your **Escalation 2** ability, a Skill Talent |
| **2** | Your class's Escalation hook, an Encounter Pick, your **Escalation 4** ability, a Talent |
| **3** | A second Art, your **Escalation 6** ability, a Skill Talent |
| **4** | The Apex, a Stage Talent and the numbers of Stage 1 |

So a level 1 character's Escalation ability starts working at Escalation 2: round 2 of a fight, or from the start with [[Momentum]].

### Talents

A **Talent** is a feature any character can take, apart from their class. The level list has two kinds of slots: **Talents** and **Skill Talents** (for skills, out of combat), and a **Stage Talent** at the first level of Stages 1, 2 and 3.

> The Talents are set aside for now: none is in the book while the first three levels of the classes are finished. **Every Talent slot stays empty** until they are written again (the Skill Talents come with the Skills rules).

### Swapping on a Rest

When you complete a [[Rest]], you can change any of the choices your class's level list gave you: your Hallmark Action, your Hallmark Reaction, your Arts, your Picks, your Escalation abilities and your Talents. Each new choice must be one you could have made at that level.

During Stage 0 you can also swap the order of your Modifiers on a Rest, if your class lets you choose it. From Stage 1 on, that order and the Modifier you raise at Stage 1 are permanent.

**Optional rule.** If the GM allows it, on a Rest you can also change your class's Attribute choices and which of its two Health values is your Vigor.

### Stages

The levels are grouped in **Stages**: **Stage 0** is levels 1 to 3, **Stage 1** levels 4 to 7, **Stage 2** levels 8 to 11 and **Stage 3** levels 12 to 15. Your numbers change only when you reach a new Stage, and the NPCs of the [[Bestiary]] follow them (their tables are written by Stage).

| | Stage 0 (levels 1-3) | Stage 1 (levels 4-7) | Stage 2 (levels 8-11) | Stage 3 (levels 12-15) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Modifiers** | your class's values (+2 and +1) | +1 to your Weapon or Power Modifier | | +1 to your Weapon and Power Modifiers |
| **Health** | your class's values | + your class's Stage 1 steps | + your class's Stage 2 steps | + your class's Stage 3 steps |
| **Guard per unspent AP** | 2 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| **Rally Points** | Rally + 1 | Rally + 2 | Rally + 3 | Rally + 4 |
| **Stage Talent** | | at level 4 | at level 8 | at level 12 |

**Your Attributes don't grow with your level or your Stage.** A Modifier is never above +4.

> [!tbd]
> Only Stage 0 is written so far: the classes' levels 4 and above, their Health steps for Stages 1, 2 and 3, and the Bestiary's later tables come after it.

### Distinct Choices

When your class gives you a choice between two Attributes, you can't pick an Attribute you already picked from another of its choices: each Attribute increase goes to a different Attribute. If a class offers "Body or Appeal +1" and "Appeal or Instinct +1" and you pick Appeal for the first, you must pick Instinct for the second.

### Weapon, Power and Signature Modifiers

Your class gives you two **Modifier values**: **+2 and +1** at level 1. One is your **Weapon Modifier**, which [[Weapon]] Actions roll; the other is your **Power Modifier**, which [[Power]] Actions roll and which sets your [[Defenses|Ward Defense]]. Your class says which is which, or lets you choose. They don't come from your Attributes, and your Attributes don't change them.

They grow with your [[Stages|Stage]]: at **Stage 1** (level 4) add +1 to the one you choose, at **Stage 3** (level 12) +1 to both. A Modifier is never above +4.

Your **Signature Modifier** is the higher of your Weapon Modifier and your Power Modifier (if they are equal, choose one). Actions with the [[Signature]] trait roll it.

Your **Attributes** set your Checks (Initiative is one), your Saves, your Defenses against an Attribute, your rolls on the Dying and Shattered Tables and the Attribute Actions, such as Shove and Trip (see [[Rolls]]).

> [!example]
> Lonny's Paladin has Weapon Modifier +1 and Power Modifier +2 (Ward Defense 13; his Signature Modifier is +2, from his Power Modifier). At level 4 he adds +1 to his Weapon Modifier: +2 / +2. At level 12 both rise: +3 / +3.

### Escalation Abilities

Each class has a list of **Escalation** abilities, two for each of Escalation 2, 4 and 6, and **Apex** Actions, the class's big Encounter Action that needs Escalation 6. An Escalation ability is a **Passive**, which works while your Escalation is at least the number on its card, or an Action that you can use only while your Escalation is at least that number. You choose one for each of Escalation 2, 4 and 6: the Escalation 2 one at level 1, the Escalation 4 one at level 2 and the Escalation 6 one at level 3. You choose your Apex at level 4. Nothing spends Escalation. See [[Escalation]] for how it rises.
