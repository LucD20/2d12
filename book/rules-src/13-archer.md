## Level 1

### Features

As a marksman, you gain two Features at level 1.

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Sharpshooter</span><span class="cat">Archer Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** You were made for the shot no one else would take.

Choose your Hallmark Action: **Double Shot** or **Rolling Shot**. Choose one [[Projectile]] weapon, such as a bow or a crossbow: you start with **3 Mastery** with it. Your [Shot] Actions don't take the Bane for targeting a creature Engaged with a creature other than you.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Marked Quarry</span><span class="cat">Archer Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** You pick the one, and the world narrows.

At the start of each encounter, choose one enemy as your **Marked Quarry**. You gain 1 Boon on [Shot, Strike] Actions against them, and those Actions deal [B] [Mod] on a Tier 1. When they die or go Down, you can choose a new Marked Quarry (0R [Focus]). Once per turn, you can change your Marked Quarry during an encounter by spending 1 AP [Focus].

</div>

### Hallmark Action Selection

Choose one of the following Hallmark Actions:

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Double Shot</span><span>2 / 3 AP</span><span class="cat">Sharpshooter 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** Two shots, so close together they sound like one.

*Shot • Strike*

**Frequency:** Round

**Target:** Two creatures

| 2 AP | 3 AP |
|---|---|
| You make two [Ranged, Shot] Weapon Attacks (1A), each against a different creature. | Evade 1, then as 2 AP. |

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Rolling Shot</span><span>1 / 2 / 3 AP</span><span class="cat">Sharpshooter 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** You keep moving, and the shot rides along with the motion.

*Ranged • Shot • Strike • Weapon*

**Frequency:** Round

**Target:** One creature

**Distance:** Weapon

| 1 AP | 2 AP | 3 AP |
|---|---|---|
| Evade 1, then make a [Ranged, Shot] Weapon Attack (1A). | Evade 2, then make a [Ranged, Shot] Weapon Attack (1A) that deals an extra [B] 1d6 Pi. | Evade 3, then make a [Ranged, Shot] Weapon Attack (1A) that deals an extra [B] 2d6 Pi. |

</div>

### Picks

- You pick one Talent (the [[Talents]] document).
- You pick one Passive Escalation ability from the [[Archer Escalation]].
- You pick one Active Escalation ability from the [[Archer Escalation]].
