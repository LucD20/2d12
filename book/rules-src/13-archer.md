## Level 1

At level 1 you gain the Features Sharpshooter, Marked Quarry and Bow Mastery, and the Class Actions Double Shot and Precise Aim.

As a marksman, you gain three Features at level 1.

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Sharpshooter</span><span class="cat">Archer Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** You were made for the shot no one else would take.

You gain **Double Shot** and **Precise Aim**.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Marked Quarry</span><span class="cat">Archer Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** You pick the one, and the world narrows.

At the start of each encounter, choose one enemy as your **Marked Quarry**. You gain 1 Boon on [Shot, Strike] Actions against them. When they die or go Down, you can choose a new Marked Quarry (0R [Focus]). Once per turn, you can change your Marked Quarry during an encounter by spending 1 AP [Focus].

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Bow Mastery</span><span class="cat">Archer Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** One weapon has been in your hands longer than any other.

Choose one [[Projectile]] weapon, such as a bow or a crossbow. You start with **3 Mastery** with it.

</div>

## Archer Escalation

### Passives

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Patient Hunter</span><span class="cat">Archer Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** The shot is worth waiting for.

While your Escalation is 2 or more, if you have not left your square since the start of your last turn, gain 1 Boon on [Shot, Strike] Actions until you leave your square.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Steady Nerves</span><span class="cat">Archer Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** They are in your face. You are still calm.

While your Escalation is 2 or more, you don't take the normal Banes for using [Ranged] Actions while Engaged.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Killer's Focus</span><span class="cat">Archer Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** First blood is the only blood that matters.
