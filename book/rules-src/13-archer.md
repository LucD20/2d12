# Archer

<p class="intro">You win by never being where the enemy looks. The Archer is a fast, fragile marksman who brings a foe down with many small hits and not one great one: you shoot, you move, you slip a knife in when something gets close, and you are gone again.</p>

## Level 1

### Level 1: Features

Every Archer has these four. **Shifting Aim** pays you for never shooting twice from the same square. **Darting Blade** is the knife for when a foe is next to you, where a bow is [[Ranged Combat While Engaged|Doomed]]: a quick stab, and you are out of reach again.

Many of your Strikes deal more hits the better you roll: "2[W]" is your weapon's damage twice, and "3[W]" three times (see [[Weapon Damage]]). Even on a Tier 1 they deal your Arms Modifier: an arrow of yours always finds something.

### Level 1: Hallmark Action

**Choose one.** *Quick build: Quick Shots.*

- **Quick Shots** (Round, 2 AP): arrow after arrow into one foe, then you step away.
- **Strafe** (Round, 2 AP): you run across the fight and put an arrow into up to three foes.

@@CARD:Quick Shots@@

@@CARD:Strafe@@

### Level 1: Hallmark Reaction

**Choose one.** *Quick build: Snap Shot.* Both answer a foe that comes to you.

- **Slip Away** (Turn, 1R): when a foe ends its turn next to you, you step away and change weapons.
- **Snap Shot** (Round, 1R): as a foe comes to you, you step back and shoot it.

## Level 2

### Level 2: Escalation Hook

You gain Thrill of the Kill: it raises your Escalation when an enemy you have damaged goes Down.

@@CARD:Thrill of the Kill@@

### Level 2: Encounter Pick

**Choose one.** *Quick build: Volley.*

- **Volley** (2 AP): arrows down on every creature in a patch of ground.
- **Covering Fire** (2 AP): a stretch of ground no enemy crosses without paying, for 2 rounds.
