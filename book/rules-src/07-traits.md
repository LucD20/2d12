# Chapter 7: Traits

All Actions have traits. Traits have their own rule interactions. Traits with no rule of their own (such as Divine) are used by other rules and features.

### Area

These Actions have an **Area** (see [[Areas]]), and can have a Distance that says how far from you the area can be placed. They target every creature inside their area that they affect, and they ignore Senses: they target creatures you perceive as Obscured, Hidden or Unsensed, with no penalty for it, and light doesn't change their roll. You need Line of Effect to the Point of Origin to place the area, and Line of Effect and Cover are checked from the Point of Origin to each creature.

### Body, Mind, Appeal and Instinct

An Action with one of these traits rolls that Attribute Modifier. A Body Action rolls Body, and so on. Some effects care about them, for example "your Body Actions gain 1 Boon".

When an Action has **more than one** of these traits, you roll the one with the **highest** value. The Action still has all of them: Trip is a Body Action and an Instinct Action, whichever it rolls. If the Action has a Vs. entry that says "the one used to roll," that Attribute is also the Defense: if you roll Body, you roll against Body.

### Fear

The Fear trait has no rules of its own. Other rules, Talents and Tags refer to it.

### Focus

These Actions require concentration. This is not a mechanic by itself, but some features affect your ability to concentrate.

### Handle

To use an Action with the Handle trait, you must be able to use your hands (or similar appendages). You also need a **hand free** to make any Action with this trait. Releasing an object costs no AP (Release).

### Melee

These Actions need a melee weapon used in hand, and use its Range as their Distance. A Weapon Action without this trait gets it when you use a melee weapon in hand (see [[Weapon Actions and Your Weapon]]).

### Move

A **Move** is an Action with the Move trait.

To use an Action with the Move trait, you must have a Speed of at least 1.

You can't end your movement in a square with another creature. A creature can allow you to pass through their squares. Otherwise, passing through the squares of an unwilling creature is Difficult Terrain.

### Nonlethal

These Actions deal Nonlethal damage by default. A Nonlethal Action can deal [[Lethal and Nonlethal|Lethal damage]] by taking a Bane.

### Power

These Actions roll your **Power Modifier**.

### Ranged

These Actions target creatures from a distance. The Action's Distance can be written like a ranged weapon's Range: normal / far / maximum, with 1 Bane at far range and 2 at maximum. A single number is a normal range only. A [Ranged, Weapon] Action needs a ranged weapon, or a weapon with the Thrown property used thrown, and uses that weapon's Range as its Distance. A Weapon Action without this trait gets it when you use a ranged weapon or throw a Thrown weapon.

### Save

A Save Action: its targets roll a Save instead of you rolling against their Defense. See [[Save Actions]].

### Self

An Action with the Self trait always targets you, the origin of the Action. An [Area] Action with Self is centered on you: you are the target of its Emanation.

### Shot

Shot Actions need a weapon with the [[Projectile]] property, such as a bow or a crossbow, and use its Range as their Distance. A Weapon Action without this trait gets it when you use such a weapon (so a Weapon Attack with a crossbow is a Shot).

### Signature

These Actions roll your **Signature Modifier**: the higher of your Weapon Modifier and your Power Modifier. A Signature Action is a Signature roll: effects on Weapon rolls or Power rolls don't apply to it (see [[Rolls]]).

### Split

A Split Action has two modes, **Weapon** and **Power**, and you choose one each time you use it. In that use, the Action takes the chosen mode's trait and is an Action of that mode for every rule: a Weapon Action in Weapon mode (it needs a weapon, takes Melee or Ranged from it, and uses its Range), and a Power Action in Power mode (no weapon). The card shows the two modes side by side; everything else (Frequency, target, trigger, Critical) is shared.

### Strike

Strike is any Action that tries to hit a creature, such as an attack with a weapon or a spell. You can make at most 3 Strikes per turn.

### Weapon

This uses a weapon you are wielding. Actions with this trait require a weapon, and their Distance is the weapon's Range unless they say otherwise.

These Actions roll your **Weapon Modifier**. A Weapon roll normally targets Armor. When a rule calls for a Weapon roll against a DC, use the DC instead.

A Weapon Action with the Melee or Ranged trait needs a weapon with that property. A Weapon Action without them gets the one that matches the weapon you use.

### Tags

Creatures have **Tags** instead of traits: words that other rules refer to, such as **Profane** (Profane Judgment). A creature's Tags are listed in its stat block. Like traits with no rule of their own, a Tag does nothing by itself.
