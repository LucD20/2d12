# Chapter 7: Traits

An Action's traits are the words under its Flavor. Each trait below has a rule of its own, or is a word other rules and features refer to.

### Area

These Actions have an Area: they affect every creature in it and ignore Senses (see [[Areas]] and [[Senses]]).

### Body, Mind, Appeal and Instinct

An Action with one of these traits rolls that Attribute Modifier. A Body Action rolls Body, and so on. Some effects care about them, for example "your Body Actions gain 1 Boon".

When an Action has more than one of these traits, you choose which of them to roll. The Action still has all of them: Trip is a Body Action and an Instinct Action, whichever it rolls.

### Divine, Fear, Focus and Magical

These traits have no rules of their own. Other rules and features refer to them.

### Firearm

Firearm Actions need a weapon with the Firearm property (see [[Properties]]) and use its Range as their Distance. A Firearm is not a Projectile weapon: Projectile Actions can't use it.

### Handle

To use an Action with the Handle trait, you must be able to use your hands (or similar appendages). You also need a **hand free** to make any Action with this trait. Releasing an object costs no AP (Release). **One exception:** stowing an object you hold, or swapping it for another you carry (Interact), needs no free hand: the hand that holds the object does it. With both hands full you can stow one weapon, or swap it for another.

### Melee

These Actions target creatures in close combat.

### Move

A **Move** is an Action with the Move trait.

To use an Action with the Move trait, you must have a Speed of at least 1.

You can't end your movement in another creature's space, and you can't enter an unwilling creature's space at all; a willing creature's space is Difficult Terrain (see [[Movement]]).

### Nonlethal

These Actions deal Nonlethal damage by default. A Nonlethal Action can deal [[Lethal and Nonlethal|Lethal damage]] by taking a Bane.

### Power

These Actions roll your **Power Modifier**.

### Projectile

Projectile Actions need a weapon with the Projectile property (see [[Properties]]), such as a bow, and use its Range as their Distance.

### Ranged

These Actions target creatures from a distance (how a range is written: [[Weapon Range]]; shooting next to an enemy: [[Ranged Combat While Engaged]]). A [Ranged, Weapon] Action needs a ranged weapon, or a weapon with the Thrown property used thrown.

### Save

A Save Action: its targets roll a Save instead of you rolling against their Defense. See [[Save Actions]].

### Self

An Action with the Self trait always targets you, the origin of the Action. An [Area] Action with Self is centered on you: you are the target of its Emanation.

### Signature

These Actions roll your **Signature Modifier**: the higher of your Weapon Modifier and your Power Modifier, even when they also have the Weapon or Power trait. They keep their other traits: a [Signature, Weapon] Action still needs a weapon and gets every effect on [Weapon] Actions (see [[Rolls]]). Signature is used for a class's own Actions, such as the Paladin's Smite.

### Strike

A Strike is an Action with the Strike trait: an attack aimed at a creature. You can make at most 3 Strikes per turn. All At-will [Strike] Actions share one At-will count: your second At-will [Strike] in a turn takes 1 Bane, and your third takes 2 Banes, even if they are different Actions. A Strike made as a Linked Action counts toward neither, unless its Action says otherwise (see [[Linked Actions]]).

### Subtle

A Subtle Action doesn't draw attention: using it doesn't end [[Hiding|Hide]], while any Action without this trait ends Hide against every creature once it is resolved. [[Hide]], [[Sneak]], [[Seek]] and [[Lean]] are Subtle. [[Interact]] can be: see its card.

### Unfailing

When the roll of an Unfailing Action gets a Tier 1, that use doesn't count toward the Action's Frequency: you can use it again. You still spend its AP, and its Tier 1 still happens. An Encounter Action with Unfailing that gets a Tier 1 is still unspent this encounter.

### Weapon

This uses a weapon you are wielding. Actions with this trait require a weapon, and their Distance is the weapon's Range unless they say otherwise.

These Actions roll your **Weapon Modifier**.

A Weapon Action with the Melee or Ranged trait needs a weapon with that property. A Weapon Action without them gets the one that matches the weapon you use.

### Tags

Creatures have **Tags** instead of traits: words that other rules refer to, such as **Profane** (the Paladin's Smite). A creature's Tags are listed in its stat block. Like traits with no rule of their own, a Tag does nothing by itself.
