## Level 1

### Features

As a holy warrior, you gain two Features at level 1. Font of Mercy is an Escalation Passive that every Paladin has: it doesn't use your Passive Escalation Pick.

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Divine Power</span><span class="cat">Paladin Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** Your god's strength flows through your weapon and your hands.

When you deal Holy damage to a creature with the Profane [[Tags|Tag]], that creature is Enfeebled 1 (EoT).

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Font of Mercy</span><span class="cat">Paladin Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** Where you kneel, wounds remember how to close.

While your Escalation is 1 or more, when you or an ally within 5 squares of you uses a Rally Point, they heal your Power Modifier + 2 more.

</div>

### Picks

- You pick one Talent (the [[Talents]] document).
- You pick one Encounter Action from the [[Paladin Repertoire]].
- You pick one Passive Escalation ability from the [[Paladin Escalation]].
- You pick one Active Escalation ability from the [[Paladin Escalation]].

## Paladin Escalation

### Passives

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Hallowed Stance</span><span class="cat">Paladin Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** You do not move. Neither do those beside you.

While your Escalation is 2 or more, if you haven't left your square since your last turn, you and your adjacent allies take less Body damage, reduced by your Power Modifier (minimum 0). This reduction applies to one instance of damage per Action; if the Action has several, the player chooses which one is reduced.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Radiant Zeal</span><span class="cat">Paladin Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** The longer the fight, the brighter you burn.

While your Escalation is 1 or more, the first [Strike] Action you make each round that deals damage deals an extra [M] 1d6 Ho.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Mending Light</span><span class="cat">Paladin Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** Light gathers where you stand, and the hurt are drawn to it.

While your Escalation is 1 or more, at the start of each of your turns, you or one ally within 5 squares of you heals your Power Modifier + 1.

</div>
