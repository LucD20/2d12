## Level 1

At level 1 you gain the Features Divine Power and Profane Judgment, and the Class Actions Smite and Lay On Hands.

As a holy warrior, you gain two Features at level 1.

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Divine Power</span><span class="cat">Paladin Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** Your god's strength flows through your weapon and your hands.

You gain **Smite** and **Lay On Hands**.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Profane Judgment</span><span class="cat">Paladin Feature 1</span></div>

**Flavor:** The profane flinch at your god's light.

When you deal Holy damage to a creature with the Profane [[Tags|Tag]], that creature is Enfeebled 1 (EoT).

</div>

## Paladin Escalation

### Passives

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Divine Vigil</span><span class="cat">Paladin Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** Your watch does not end when the fighting starts.

While your Escalation is 2 or more, you and your allies in an Aura 3 around you gain 1 Boon on Saves against [Fear] Actions.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Avenger's Oath</span><span class="cat">Paladin Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** Every wound your friends take is written in the ledger.

While your Escalation is 2 or more, when an ally within 5 squares becomes Bloodied, you gain 1 Boon on your next [Strike] Action.

</div>

<div class="action" markdown="1">
<div class="action-head"><span>Hallowed Stance</span><span class="cat">Paladin Escalation (Passive)</span></div>

**Flavor:** You do not move. Neither do those beside you.

While your Escalation is 2 or more, if you haven't left your square since your last turn, you and your adjacent allies take less Body damage, reduced by your Power Modifier (minimum 0). This reduction applies to one instance of damage per Action; if the Action has several, the player chooses which one is reduced.

</div>
