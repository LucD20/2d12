# Paladin

<p class="intro">You stand in front so the others can stand back. The Paladin is a holy warrior who judges a foe before their god, shields the allies near them, and, when the fight has built long enough, brings all of it down in one blow.</p>

## Level 1

### Level 1: Features

Every Paladin has these four. **Judge** names the foe your god's attention is on: the creature you Judged counts as **Profane** for you, which your other cards read. **Smite** is your one great blow of the fight; it grows with your [[Escalation]].

@@CARD:Judge@@

@@CARD:Smite@@

**Your Basic attacks.** Every character has the [[Basic Attack]]; your **Sworn Blade** adds a line to it, and **Drawing Light** is your own Power Basic attack: a cord of light that hauls a foe to you. A Weapon Basic attack (Vigor damage) pays off against a Strained foe, a Power one (Resolve damage) against a Bloodied foe, so the two feed each other.

@@CARD:Sworn Blade@@

@@CARD:Drawing Light@@

### Level 1: Hallmark Action

**Choose one.** *Quick build: Binding Light.*

- **Binding Light** (Round, 2 AP): a weapon blow with holy light that roots the foe where it stands.
- **Lay On Hands** (Round, 1 or 2 AP): you step to an ally and give them Guard; at 2 AP they also use a Rally Point.

@@CARD:Binding Light@@

@@CARD:Lay On Hands@@

### Level 1: Hallmark Reaction

**Choose one.** *Quick build: Shield of Faith.* Both are paid with your Reaction Point or with AP you kept from your turn (see [[Reactions]]).

- **Shield of Faith** (Turn, 1R): when a foe would damage an ally, you take some of it off and draw the foe to you.
- **Rebuke** (Turn, 1R): when a foe moves away from you, it slows and may stop.

@@CARD:Shield of Faith@@

@@CARD:Rebuke@@

## Level 2

### Level 2: Escalation Hook

You gain Kindled Wrath: it raises your Escalation when an ally becomes Bloodied or goes Down.

@@CARD:Kindled Wrath@@

### Level 2: Encounter Pick

**Choose one.** *Quick build: Bulwark of Faith.*

- **Bulwark of Faith** (1R): you step into a blow meant for an ally.
- **Consecrated Ground** (2 AP): holy ground that slows foes and steadies allies, for 3 rounds.
- **Word of Radiance** (2 AP): a burst of light that burns and throws back the foes around you.

@@CARD:Bulwark of Faith@@

@@CARD:Consecrated Ground@@

@@CARD:Word of Radiance@@

## Level 3

Level 3 gives a Skill Talent (see [[Talents]]); your class gives no card at this level.
