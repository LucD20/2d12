# Magic, as decided: a reading copy

**2026-11-19.** The rules of magic as they stand after decision 99 of `book/magic-design.md`, put in reading order. **This is not rulebook text and nothing here is in the book.** It is Claude's wording of Lucas's decisions, written so that the whole can be read in one sitting. **The record is the authority:** where this page and the record differ, the record wins. A number in brackets is the decision it comes from.

**Words (decision 107).** A Path or a School has a **rank**; "level" is the character's. A spell is the card; a casting is one use. This page still says "level" in places.

**Marks.** **[A]** an assumption of Claude's that Lucas has not confirmed. **[open]** not decided. **[unplayed]** decided, never tried at a table. Nothing is priced: the SIM has no caster.

---

## 1. What magic is

- A **spell** is a Power Action with the trait **[Spell]**, one **Path** and one **School**. It writes a minimum level in each: "Fire 2 • Evocation 1". You can cast it if you have chosen it and meet every minimum. [5, 16]
- A rare spell is written with **two Paths** ("Fire 2 • Earth 1 • Evocation 1"). You need all three minimums. It counts as a spell of both Paths. Never three. [63; "never three" A]
- A Power Action adds no Modifier to its damage (Chapter 1).
- **The Paladin's Divine cards are not magic.** Nothing keyed on [Spell], a Path or a School reads them. Magic and the Divine still meet like any two effects: neither is immune to the other. [12, 94]
- **Blood** is a class apart, fuelled by the living. [1, 11; not designed]

### The eight Paths (what a spell is made of)

| Path | It is | Its usual damage | Its usual condition (a writer's guide, not a rule) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | Flame, heat, rage | Fire | Lingering |
| Air | Wind, storm, lightning | Electricity, Thunder | Pushed |
| Water | Water, ice, cold | Cold | Hindered |
| Earth | Stone, metal, weight | Blunt, Slash, Pierce | Prone |
| Astral | Magic itself, the stars, the will | Force, Psychic | Dazed |
| Death | Death, decay, darkness, the dead | Unholy | Frightened |
| Nature | Life, growth, beasts, poison | Poison (a new subtype), Pierce | Immobilized |
| Glamour | Illusion, dreams, emotion, luck | **False damage** | Pressed |

- A Path's damage and condition are its usual ones, not its only ones: any spell may use another when its fiction calls for it. [23, 64, 91, 95]
- **False damage** is Nonlethal Resolve damage. All the false damage a caster has dealt is removed when that caster goes Down; what is left is removed when the encounter ends. A creature brought Down by it is Unconscious. It has no subtype: nothing Resists it. [95, 105; no Lethal switch: A]

### The six Schools (what a spell does)

A spell's School is found by asking in this order; the first yes decides. This is the writer's rule: a player reads the School on the card. [65]

1. It brings a creature or a thing that wasn't there: **Conjuration**.
2. It answers or stops something: **Abjuration**.
3. It is held: **Enchantment**.
4. Its main effect is damage: **Evocation**.
5. Otherwise, it changes the body or the ground: **Alteration**; the mind or the senses: **Thaumaturgy**.

A School doesn't say whether a spell is a Strike or a Save. [8]

## 2. Getting magic

- A Magic Talent or a class **opens** one Path and one School together, each at level 1, with two spells of your choice. A later Talent opens one more Path or School, with one spell. [27]
- **Mastery.** When your side wins an encounter that was normal or harder, you gain **1 Mastery in a single thing you used in it: a weapon, a Path or a School**, chosen at its end. [75]
- Rank 2 at 10 Mastery, 3 at 20, 4 at 30, 5 at 45, 6 at 60. **6 is the cap.** [27, 105]
- At each new level you choose one spell you meet the minimums for. Spells can be swapped on a Rest. [19, 27]
- **A level is never a number on a roll or a DC.** It unlocks spells and Overcast steps. Dice grow with the Stage, as everything does. [18]
- Talents grant more spells and change spells by their traits. They never give levels. [17, 19]

## 3. Casting a spell

### What the card asks of you

A card prints one of these only when the spell needs it. [77, 78, 85]

- **[Handle]:** you must be able to use your hands and have one free (Chapter 7).
- **[Incanted]:** you must be able to speak. [78, 105]
- **[Focus]:** it needs concentration. Most spells have it. It has no rule of its own; other rules can key on it.

### Who notices

- **A casting is always noticed.** Everyone who can perceive you knows who is casting and who is being cast on. [97]
- A spell with **[Subtle]** isn't noticed as it is cast; what it does is still seen. [97; that reading: A]
- **What is being cast** is known only to those who could cast it. With a spell's Path at its level or higher you know its Path; with its School, its School; with both, the spell itself. No roll, no Action. [97, 98; no roll: A]
- **Nothing counters a spell as it is cast.** A counterspell is a Talent tree, to be made with Talents. [99]

### While Engaged

A spell takes the ranged rules only if its card prints **[Ranged]**: Doomed while Engaged, range bands, a Prone target's Bane. A spell with neither [Ranged] nor [Melee] is cast freely while Engaged. A [Melee, Spell] needs no weapon. [108, 77]

### A Save spell

- The targets roll the Save; Tier 1 is the worst for them (Chapter 1).
- **Your Boons and Banes change the DC they save against:** each Boon you would have on the roll adds 1, each Bane subtracts 1. Working name for that number: the **Save DC**. [88; the name: A]
- Where your roll would be Doomed, their Saves are Favored; where it would be Favored, theirs are Doomed. So a [Ranged] Save spell cast while Engaged gives the targets Favored Saves. What applies against one creature only changes the Save DC for that creature. **An [Area] ignores Senses;** a Save spell that picks a creature takes Sense penalties through its Save DC. [76, 88, 100, 109]

### Armor

- **Unarmored or Light:** nothing.
- **Medium:** you start an encounter with 1 less Surge.
- **Heavy:** you start with 1 less Surge, and your first spell each turn costs 1 more Surge (not a Reaction spell, a Sustain or an Overcast step).
- Your Surge at the start can't go below 0. Armor you aren't trained in keeps Chapter 15's penalty. [79, 80, 89; the floor: A]

## 4. Surge [unplayed]

- **Three words.** **Momentum** is your party's, between fights. **Escalation** is your level in the fight. **Surge** is what you spend. [66]
- You start an encounter with Surge equal to your Escalation. You gain 1 at the start of every round after the first, and 1 whenever anything else raises your Escalation. An Unconscious character gains none. [66, 73, 90; "anything else": A]
- Spending Surge doesn't lower your Escalation. It is gone when the encounter ends. It does nothing unless one of your Actions spends it. [66, 68]
- Every PC has Surge; **only spells spend it for now.** The classes get their uses at their next pass. [68]
- **An NPC caster** gains 1 Surge a round; its entry can say otherwise ("starts with 3 Surge"). [72; it starts with 1: A]

### What costs Surge

| What | Surge |
|---|---|
| An ordinary spell | 0 |
| A stronger spell | The cost printed beside its AP ("2 AP · 1 Surge"), 1 to 3 |
| Each Overcast step | 1 |

[67, 69]

## 5. Overcast: stretching a spell

- Overcast is its own line on a card. Each thing you buy on it is a **stretch**. [26, 110]
- A stretch needs **one spare rank** in its axis, and Surge. [26, 67]
- **Spare School rank** buys stretches on the three shared ladders, 1 Surge each; the card lists which it may use: **Carry** (+2 squares to its Distance), **Fork** (+1 creature), **Echo** (+1 round). [43, 111]
- **Spare Path rank** buys the spell's own stretches, each written on the card with its price ("+2 Surge: the flames stay on the ground"). [26, 104]
- The Surge you spend on Overcast in one casting is at most **Escalation minus 1**; outside an encounter, the Surge you hold is the only limit. A spell's printed Surge cost doesn't count against that. [70, 71, 104]
- **An [Area] spell has no Fork.** A bigger area is the next spell of the line. A Talent may open area growth later. [43, 61]
- **Merge is cut.** Two Paths meet only in spells written with two Paths. [91]

## 6. Overreach: only where a card says so

- Only a spell that prints an **Overreach** line can be Overreached. The line says what it buys and its whole price. [57, 58]
- Once a casting; you decide before any roll; the price is paid even if the spell fails. **It is paid after the casting and any Reaction to it have resolved.** [105]
- The usual price is Nonlethal Resolve damage that Guard doesn't take and nothing reduces, with exploding dice. It doesn't grow with Stage.
- A line may get past one of magic's limits, **once**, and says so ("a second time in this encounter"). [59]
- **Nonlethal damage now stays until a Sleep,** unless a Rally Point or a heal removes it. The Breather is deleted. [55, 56]

## 7. Magic that is held

- **[Woven]:** you hold one Woven spell; casting another ends it. [28, 29]
- **[Sustained]:** "Sustain: 1 AP" at the start of your turn, or the spell ends; as many as you can pay for. [29]
- Both end when you go Down, when you choose, at the end of the encounter, and **when you become Bloodied or Strained.** [28]
- Buffs mostly last a count of rounds; one [Woven] spell can cover several allies. [33]
- A spell that takes more than one turn to cast, if such spells are written, is lost when its caster becomes Bloodied or Strained. [85]

## 8. Summons

- **One Summon spell for each Path,** each "Path 1 • Conjuration 1". [62]
- One summon at a time. [44]
- It is a **Minion of your Stage**: 3 AP, no Reactions. It grows when you reach a Stage. [37, 41, 44]
- It costs **Sustain: 1 AP**, and takes its own turn after yours. [41]
- **Built on a Rest:** its Path gives its theme and its attack's subtype; then picks from a short menu. [44]
- It leaves when it goes Down, when you don't pay, and when the spell ends (so also when you become Bloodied or Strained; a Talent tree will lift that). [44]
- It can't Flank, summon, use Rally Points or be healed. It can be given Guard. [44]
- **It fights beside you like any ally, and never triggers or earns anything:** no class hook, no Mastery, no Momentum. It never counts for Escalation, Mastery, Momentum, or any rule that counts creatures going Down or becoming Bloodied or Strained. [92, 105]

## 9. Outside a fight

- **A spell with a duration is combat only.** Held magic, buffs and summons exist only in an encounter. [87]
- **An instant spell can be cast outside,** paying its printed Surge cost and its Overcast steps; there the Surge you have is the only limit on steps. [71, 87]
- **Surge outside a fight is a pool:** it refills to your party's Momentum whenever the party gains Momentum (any encounter overcome), and is cut down to Momentum when Momentum drops below what you hold. [71, 86]
- **Lasting magic outside a fight is its own piece, [open]:** read from the same two words (the Path is what it is made of, the School what it does), wider with level, paid with that Surge. Its name and rules are not designed. Research: `research/out-of-combat-magic.md`. [87]

## 10. The Limits of Magic

1. Magic doesn't restore Health. It can give Guard. [36]
2. Magic doesn't take a whole turn from a PC or a major enemy. Charm is "you can't target that creature with a harmful Action"; control is one Action of the caster's choice; confusion is "its next Strike targets the nearest creature". Some spells are stronger against Minor enemies. [39]
3. Magic that lasts needs a fight to feed it. [87]

**Bringing back the dead is to exist,** with extreme limits and likely just once. Not designed. Research: `research/revival.md`. [93]

Each limit is something a Talent, a class or a rare spell may later break on purpose.

---

## 11. What a caster is held by

- **In a turn:** AP (4 and a Reaction AP), each spell's Frequency, three Strikes.
- **In a fight:** Surge. **No other limit across the fight, for now, on a close watch.** [60]
- **Across a day:** Overreach's Nonlethal damage, the Surge spent outside fights, a Daily Frequency.
- **In the build:** the minimums, few spells known, one Mastery point an encounter.
