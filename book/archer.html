Archer

You were made for the shot no one else would take. The Archer is a marksman who singles out one target, narrows the world to it and lets the arrow fly: you choose how, a little more at every level.

Level 1

Features

As a marksman, you gain two Features and the Mark Quarry Action at level 1. Thrill of the Kill is your class's Escalation hook.

SharpshooterArcher Feature 1
You were made for the shot no one else would take.

Choose your Hallmark Action: Double Shot or Rolling Shot. Choose one Projectile weapon, such as a bow or a crossbow: you start with 3 Mastery with it. Your [Shot] Actions don't take the Bane for targeting a creature Engaged with a creature other than you.

Thrill of the KillArcher Feature 1
The first one falls, and the rest of the world sharpens.

Once an encounter, when your Marked Quarry goes Down, your Escalation increases by 1.

Marked Quarry

Your Marked Quarry is the enemy you chose with the Mark Quarry Action. You have one at a time.

Mark QuarryArcher Feature 1Turn0/1AP
One name, one face, and the rest of the field goes quiet.
KeywordsFocus
Targets: One creature

At the start of each encounter, choose one enemy as your Marked Quarry with this Action. You gain 1 Boon on [Shot, Strike] Actions against them, and those Actions deal [V] [Mod] extra damage (the same subtype as your weapon) on a Tier 1. You can only have one Marked Quarry at a time.

Effects
0APThe targeted creature becomes your Marked Quarry: at the start of an encounter, or as a 0R [Focus] when yours died or went Down.
1APThe targeted creature becomes your Marked Quarry instead of the one you had.

Archer Escalation

Your Escalation is a level from 1 to 3 that rises as the fight goes on and is never spent (see Escalation). You choose one Passive for each level: it works while your Escalation is at least that number. The Apex is your big Encounter Action: you can use it only while your Escalation is 3.

Escalation 1 Passives

Killer's FocusArcher Escalation (Passive)
First blood is the only blood that matters.
Escalation 1

While your Escalation is 1 or more, your first [Shot, Strike, Weapon] Action each round that deals damage deals an extra [V] 1d6 Pi.

VanishArcher Escalation (Passive)
They reach for you, and you are already somewhere else.
Escalation 1

While your Escalation is 1 or more, you have the Vanish Reaction (0R, Round). Interrupt: An enemy would enter a square adjacent to you during a Move Action. You can Evade 1.

Harrying ShotsArcher Escalation (Passive)
Every arrow says the same thing: not yet.
Escalation 1

While your Escalation is 1 or more, the first time each round you deal damage to your Marked Quarry with a [Shot] Action, they take 1 Bane on [Strike] Actions (EoT).

Escalation 2 Passives

Hunter's EdgeArcher Escalation (Passive)
You know where it will be before it does.
Escalation 2

While your Escalation is 2 or more, your [Shot] Actions against your Marked Quarry ignore Cover, and the first one each round that deals damage deals an extra [V] 1d4 Pi.

Sure StepArcher Escalation (Passive)
Loose, step, and let them wonder where the next one comes from.
Escalation 2

While your Escalation is 2 or more, once a round, when you deal damage to your Marked Quarry with a [Shot] Action, you can Evade 1.

Escalation 3 Passives

FinisherArcher Escalation (Passive)
A wounded thing runs in a straight line.
Escalation 3

While your Escalation is 3 or more, the first [Shot] Action you make each round that deals damage to a Bloodied or Strained creature deals an extra [V] 1d8 Pi.

Staggering ShotArcher Escalation (Passive)
The bolt knocks the wind out of them.
Escalation 3

While your Escalation is 3 or more, the first time each round you deal damage to your Marked Quarry with a [Shot] Action, they are Dazed 1 and Hindered (EoT).
