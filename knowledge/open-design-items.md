- [ ] **NAMED DCs: DECIDED (Lucas, 2026-11-15: "Yes"; research `book/research/save-dcs.md`; not applied).** **Power DC** = 11 + the Power Modifier; **Weapon DC** = 11 + the Weapon Modifier; **Signature DC** = the higher; **an Attribute's DC** (Body DC...) = 11 + ⌈Attribute ÷ 2⌉ + 1. Later Talents can raise a DC (rare: +1 is a full step of difficulty). **The guardrail (Pathfinder 2e's class DC against spell DC): never a second DC that overlaps these.** **Places when applied:** Chapter 1 (Saves; Save Actions), Chapter 5 (Weapon, Power and Signature Modifiers), Chapter 8 (the Immobilized DC = the DC of the Action that caused it), the Glossary, the Bestiary's NPC DCs, Foundry's sheet and roll window.
