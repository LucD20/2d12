- [ ] **RANGED AGAINST MELEE: MEASURED, NOTHING DECIDED (Lucas's question, 2026-11-12, before the Stage 0 run).** Lucas: in most games ranged deals less because it is safe; here the Weapon Modifier serves both, so anyone can carry both weapons. **What the rules have today:** ranged weapons deal as much as melee ones (Crossbow 0 / 1d4+2 / 1d10+2 against the Greatsword's 0 / 1d8 / 2d6; Shortbow = Longsword, 0 / 1d6 / 1d10); a Reload is optional (a bonus shot, not a cost); what ranged pays is Banes (2 while Engaged, 1 Field Bane into a melee, Cover from creatures in the line: about 0.9 Banes on an average shot, near 9% of its damage); nothing on the Paladin's cards asks for a melee weapon (Smite is [Weapon, Strike]). **What SIM measures (4 Standards, open field; the same builds, only the weapon changed):** the Smite Paladin with a Crossbow deals the same (24.9 damage a turn that counted against 25.4) and takes less than half (4.2 a turn against 9.0); with both Paladins on Crossbows the normal fight costs 8% of the party's Health instead of 14% and the deadly one is won 83% instead of 73% (among the pillars 87% against 61%); the Double Shot Archer with a Greatsword deals 17 a turn instead of 27 and takes three times as much. The ranged side also shoots in round 1 while the enemies walk; a melee PC spends that AP walking. **So ranged is the better choice for everyone as written, and nothing pays a PC for standing in front.** Limits of the measure: the enemies are all melee, the plan policy (no one holds a line), no ranged enemies, level 1. **Candidates to measure in the Stage 0 run (Lucas decides):** (a) ranged weapons one die smaller; (b) something for melee (a damage or Guard edge while Engaged, or Flanking worth more); (c) a real cost for shooting while Engaged or for being reached (today the Archer shoots on with 2 Banes); (d) class Actions that name [Melee] where they should (Smite with a Crossbow?); (e) a cost to switch weapons, so "carry both" isn't free.
- [ ] **[IDEA] LOW COVER OBJECTS, SEEN FROM THE SIDE (Lucas, 2026-11-12, during SIM-2; nothing applied).** Lucas: "if the view becomes side to side instead of top to bottom, low cover objects get in the way of the lines so it is already there, just not explicit, might be worth adding to the rules." Today Chapter 3's Cover counts the corners of the target's space that are blocked from the center of your square, drawn from above: a crate or a low wall either fills its square (it blocks every line through it, like a pillar) or doesn't exist. Seen from the side, the same rule gives the answer for a low object: lines from your height to the target's corners (its feet and its head) pass over it or not. **What a rule could say (to write with Lucas):** an object has a height (low: about half a creature; a creature is as tall as it is wide); draw the lines from the side as well, and an object lower than both ends blocks only the lines that pass under its top: a crate between you and a standing target blocks its two lower corners (Medium cover), none against a target standing on it, all four against a Prone one behind it. **Already in Foundry:** the Cover height window (`module/heightcover.mjs`, v0.0.166) computes exactly this side view from the tokens' elevations; the book doesn't say it. **For SIM:** maps have only full-height walls (`#`); a low object would be a new map character with a height, and `gridrules.mjs coverLevel` a second pass from the side. Not built: it waits for the rule.

(2) **The paper test:** every rule runnable with a pencil in under a minute; watch the Load grid, Flanking's cancellation, Alert's d12; a GM screen page. (3)* **The class-charter check** (book/class-charter.md) against sameness: each class owns a mechanic nobody else uses, a rhythm (nova, steady, builder) and an out-of-combat identity; the ledger shows the classes winning fights differently; not every class the same Frequency mix. (4) **Dissociated martial Dailies:** one sentence of fiction per Frequency in Chapter 2; a candidate to make martial Dailies cost something visible (a Rally Point, or Exhausted 1 until a Sleep). (5)* **Traps as terrain objects** with a CV cost, written as a situation (a trigger; an effect: Hazardous Terrain, a Deadly Hazard, Alert +1; a use: a found trap is a weapon to Push enemies into); spot and disarm as tiered Checks, three rolls only for a major trap, a failed disarm costs time or Alert, never the trigger. (6)* **Flight and falling by reuse:** falling = Collision (2 Blunt Vigor a square, Prone on landing); a flyer is at a Height, a Prone or Immobilized flyer falls (Trip, a downward Shove and the Archer's control ground it); Reach hits Height 1; a Bestiary rule: every flyer has a reason to come down or a way to be brought down; climbing and swimming as Difficult Terrain with a Body Check under pressure (Load matters: heavy armor sinks). (7) **Journeys:** a short daily procedure on the Challenge rule: each PC takes a role (Guide, Scout, Hunter, Lookout), one Check each, results in the systems (a Supply, a shortcut, Alert, Surprise, a Rough Day). (8)* **The companion rule, written now:** a companion or summon never has its own turn; it acts with your AP, its Actions are cards on your sheet, an area hits it once with you (the Steed rule, applied generally). (9) **Elite Resolve** (for the Bestiary review): once a round an Elite may raise a Save one tier, at a cost (its Escalation, or its Reaction); never a blanket immunity. (10) **Party size:** Challenge progress 1.5 × the PCs; exits never fewer than the PCs minus 2; the Circumstance cap limits flanking with 6 PCs; the ruler runs 3 and 6 PC parties. (11) **Retraining:** at each level-up swap one Talent or Pick; Hallmarks, Attributes and the Health side stay, except in a tutorial window (levels 1 and 2; with the STAGE 0 candidate, all of Stage 0). (12) **The Opportunity Attack** trigger "leaves a square you threaten" (consistent with Flanking); the positions model measures how often movement is punished. (13) **Support:** every support class keeps an offensive rhythm (the Paladin's Smite; a future pure support keeps a Strike line).
- [ ] **ARMOR: CANDIDATE (Lucas, 2026-11-09; nothing applied; fills Chapter 15's "not written yet" armor box).** +1 AD is about -14% Health taken a fight (the ruler), so **AD stays 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 (Unarmored to Heavy) and nothing raises it** (the economy's narrow +1 Warded exception aside, capped by Stacking); armor gets interesting on axes the rules already have. (1) **Weight:** Unarmored 11, Evade +1 square, swims and climbs freely; Light 12, Evade +1 square; Medium 13, Hearing reaches you from 15 squares instead of 10; Heavy 14, **Steadiness +1**, Evade at most 1 square, Hide takes 1 Bane, sinks in water. No Speed penalty (Speed is worth 0 DE on the ruler's field). (2) **Make:** Resist 1 against one subtype (2 for Heavy): padded Blunt (Light), leather or brigandine Pierce (Light or Medium), mail Slash (Medium), scale or lamellar Pierce (Medium), plate Slash 2 or Pierce 2 (Heavy); once per Action, so it is real against the subtype you prepared for (rough estimate: -15 to -20% of matching attacks' damage, a third of the time); enchantments add to it (Resist Fire on mail, Warded +1 WD). (3) **Donning and sleeping:** Light an Interact (1 AP); Medium a Breather, or all your AP for two rounds in a fight; Heavy a Breather, never in a fight; **sleeping in Medium or Heavy: Exhausted 1** (gone at a Sleep out of armor); with Alert's interrupted Sleep, you fight in what you slept in. (4) **Shields:** **Block** (a Reaction: reduce a Strike's damage; the Bulwark Shield of the economy design); **Splinter** (once an encounter, no AP: a Strike that would hit you hits the shield instead, no damage, the shield is Dulled until Repaired; Riders still happen; spare shields cost Load and +1 AP to draw from the pack). (5) **Optional notch** (test last): once an encounter, reduce a hit by 3 × the Armor value (Heavy 9, Medium 6, Light 3), the armor loses 1 Armor value until Repaired. Covers the box's Resist by subtype, drawbacks (Noisy, Bulky reworked), Warded, shields, and starts the unarmored Talent tree. **The ruler checks:** Resist 1-2 by Make against the Bestiary's subtype mix; Heavy's Steadiness against how often NPCs push PCs; Light's Evade for the Archer; Splinter's worth against Repair. - [ ] **TALENT STRUCTURE: PROPOSAL (Lucas, 2026-11-09; nothing applied; goes with roleplay-skills.md proposal 3).** Lucas's question: categories per level (today) or Talent points, with the fear of powergamers hoarding. **Research:** PF2e's separate skill-feat pool works for parity, but its best skill feats are combat buffs in disguise and many read as permission; one currency for everything gets min-maxed (point-buy, GURPS, Fuzion's second pool); FFG Star Wars' trees have filler and steep XP; WFRP 4e's XP is "a shopping list"; Savage Worlds' advance (a Major Edge, or two Minor, or a Minor and a skill); Lancer's ranks I-III with redistribution. **Proposal:** (1) **Two tracks, never competing:** each level one Talent (Combat, Mobility, Magic or Class) **and** one Skill pick (a skill rank or a Skill Talent); **Skill Talents never give combat numbers** (no Boons on Strikes, damage or Defense); the permission rule (roleplay-skills.md item 1). (2) **Minor and Major:** a level's Talent is one Major or two Minor; the ruler sizes them (Minor about 0.2-0.3 AP a turn, Major 0.4-0.6, a rung 1 Passive); 2 points a level, spent at the level-up, at most 1 carried over (no hoarding, no shopping list). (3) **Ranks I-III:** a pick can raise a Talent you have instead (rank II from level 4, rank III from Stage 3: renumber with the STAGE 0 candidate); chains (Brawler, then Kickboxer) become ranks or stay Prerequisites. (4) **Stage Talents stay** as milestones (Major or larger). (5) **Retraining:** swap one Talent or one Skill pick at each level-up. (6) **Avoid:** one currency for skill and combat, trees with filler, XP per session as the engine, Skill Talents with combat numbers. **Minimal fallback:** today's rule plus only the separate Skill pick and retraining one at level-up.
