# Archer

You were made for the shot no one else would take. The Archer is a marksman who singles out one target, narrows the world to it and lets the arrow fly: you choose how, a little more at every level.

**The Archer at a glance**

Attributes: Body or Instinct +1, Mind or Appeal +1

Health: high 16, low 8 (Stage 2: +9 / +5; Stage 3: +11 / +6)

Rally: 5

Speed Modifier: +1

Modifiers: Weapon +2, Power +1

Armor Training: Medium

Escalation hook: Your Marked Quarry goes Down (Thrill of the Kill)

## The Archer by Level

Level 1:

Features: Sharpshooter, Thrill of the Kill; the Mark Quarry Action

Hallmark Action: Double Shot or Rolling Shot

Hallmark Reaction: Overwatch or Covering Shot

Picks: 1 Encounter Pick

Escalation: 1 Passive for each Escalation level (1, 2 and 3), 1 Apex

Talents: 1 Talent

Level 2:

Talents: 1 Talent

Level 3:

Picks: 1 Daily Pick

Talents: 1 Talent

Level 4:

Talents: 1 Talent

Level 5 begins Stage 2 (a Stage Talent and the Stage numbers: see [[Stages]]). Further levels will be added here.

## Archer Escalation

Your Escalation is a level from 1 to 3 that rises as the fight goes on and is never spent (see [[Escalation]]). You choose one Passive for each level: it works while your Escalation is at least that number. The Apex is your big Encounter Action: you can use it only while your Escalation is 3.
