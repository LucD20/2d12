# Archer

You win by never being where the enemy looks. The Archer is a fast, fragile marksman who brings a foe down with many small hits and not one great one: you shoot, you move, you slip a knife in when something gets close, and you are gone again.

**The Archer at a glance**

Attributes: Body or Instinct +1, Mind or Appeal +1

Health: high 16, low 8 (Stage 2: +9 / +5; Stage 3: +11 / +6)

Rally: 5

Speed Modifier: +1

Modifiers: Weapon Modifier +2, Power Modifier +1

Armor Training: Light

Starting Mastery: 3 with one Projectile weapon of your choice (see [[Mastery]])

Escalation hook: An enemy you have damaged goes Down (Thrill of the Kill, level 2)

> [!tbd]
> **Levels 1 to 3 are written.** Level 4 (the Apex and the numbers of Stage 1) and the Talents are not written yet: every Talent slot is empty for now. The Archer's cards need a bow or a crossbow and, for some, a [[Light]] weapon such as a Dagger: the weapons themselves are rebuilt in the weapons pass.

## The Archer by Level

Level 1:

Features: Shifting Aim, Darting Blade

Hallmark Action: Quick Shots or Strafe

Hallmark Reaction: Slip Away or Snap Shot

Art: 1 of Footwork, Trick Shots, Spotter

Escalation: 1 Escalation 2 ability: Lean and Loose or Blade and Bow

Talents: 1 Skill Talent

Level 2:

Features: Thrill of the Kill (your Escalation hook)

Picks: 1 Encounter Pick

Escalation: 1 Escalation 4 ability: Clear Lines or Opening Shot

Talents: 1 Talent

Level 3:

Art: a second Art

Escalation: 1 Escalation 6 ability: One More Arrow or Fleet

Talents: 1 Skill Talent

Level 4 brings the Apex and Stage 1 (see [[Stages]]). Further levels will be added here.

## Level 1

### Features

Every Archer has these two. **Shifting Aim** pays you for never shooting twice from the same square. **Darting Blade** is the knife for when a foe is next to you, where a bow is [[Ranged Combat While Engaged|Doomed]]: a quick stab, and you are out of reach again.

Many of your Strikes deal more hits the better you roll: "2[W]" is your weapon's damage twice, and "3[W]" three times (see [[Weapon Damage]]). Even on a Tier 1 they deal your Weapon Modifier: an arrow of yours always finds something.

## Archer Escalation

Your Escalation is a level from 1 to 6 that rises as the fight goes on and is never spent (see [[Escalation]]). You choose one ability for each of Escalation 2, 4 and 6: it works while your Escalation is at least the number on its card.
