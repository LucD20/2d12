# Named Save DCs (a "Power DC" on the sheet)

## 2026-11-15: should the DC of a Save have a name?

**The question (Lucas):** name the DCs (Power DC, Weapon DC, Body DC...), so a card says "Appeal Save vs your Power DC", and later Talents can raise a DC and not a Modifier. Bad for the system, or disruptive?

**How far to trust this file:** our rules were read; the forum lines are search summaries of pages not opened ([lead]).

**Our rules today.** Chapter 1: a Save's DC "is usually 11 + the Weapon, Power or Signature Modifier that sets it (or 11 + ⌈Attribute ÷ 2⌉ + 1 for an Attribute-based Save); if no DC is listed, it is usually 13". Which Modifier sets it is read from the Action's traits ([Power, Save] = 11 + the Power Modifier). The same "11 + Modifier" is computed again for the Immobilized DC (Chapter 8) and inside Ward Defense (11 + the Power Modifier + the ward). Only four cards are Save Actions today.

1. **A named save DC on the sheet is the common practice.** 5e's "spell save DC" and Pathfinder 2e's "class DC" and "spell DC" are written on the character sheet and cards point to them.
2. **Pathfinder 2e's trouble is having two DCs that overlap.** [lead, Paizo threads "What use is the Class DC now?", "Am I missing a reason not to merge spell proficiency into class DC?"] Players ask which one an ability uses; some classes have both; feats "use your class DC unless they explicitly state you use your spell DC"; the name is called awkward. The confusion is about **which DC applies**, not about DCs having names.
   - *For 2d12:* name a DC only where it maps one to one onto a Modifier the character already has: Power DC, Weapon DC (and Signature DC = the higher), and one per Attribute. Never a "class DC" beside them.
3. **Things that raise a DC are few and treated as strong.** [lead, EN World] In 5e there are "very few" items that raise a spell save DC against "a plethora of ways to increase attack bonus"; posters ask for more and designers hand them out sparingly.
   - *For 2d12:* +1 on a Modifier is already "a full step of difficulty" (Lucas, 2026-11-12). A Talent that gives +1 to a DC is that strong, on every Save the character forces: such Talents are possible once the DC has a name, and should be rare.

### How it applies
- **Yes to named DCs.** It removes a formula from play (and the ⌈Attribute ÷ 2⌉ + 1 one from everybody's head), makes a card say what it means instead of leaving it to its traits, and gives Talents something to point at. It is cheap now (four Save cards).
- The traits can stay the default ("a [Power, Save] Action is against your Power DC"); a card or a Passive with no such trait writes "vs your Power DC".
- The Immobilized DC becomes "the DC of the Action that caused it".
