- [ ] **[Q] WOULD 2d12 BENEFIT FROM A SMALLER DIE? (Lucas, 2026-11-18, after `book/research/dominions-core.md` showed one point is worth about 8 points of chance on Dominions' 2d6 against about 6 on 2d12; discussion, nothing applied).** Claude's opinion: no. Computed with the same exploding rule and a DC at the same place: a Modifier of +1 to +4 gives a Tier 2 or better 62 / 69 / 75 / 81% on 2d12, 64 / 72 / 79 / 85% on 2d10, 67 / 77 / 84 / 91% on 2d8, 72 / 83 / 92 / 97% on 2d6. A smaller die makes each point bigger, which turns the +2 a character gains over fifteen levels and every Boon into more vertical growth, the opposite of the system's direction; it also raises Doubles and explosions (1 in 12 a die now, 1 in 8 on a d8) and would need every DC, the Tier 3 line (DC + 7), the Boon caps, the Bestiary, the ruler and Foundry's tests re-fitted. The fine grain of the d12 is what lets Boons and Banes be handed out one at a time.
