The Weapon and Power Modifiers use the higher of the listed Attributes, and cannot share the same Attribute. See Classes.

New rule — Save Actions

An Action with the Save trait doesn't roll: each target rolls the Save the card names, such as Instinct Save. The DC comes from the Action's other traits: 11 + your Weapon Modifier for a [Save, Weapon] Action, 11 + your Power Modifier for a [Power, Save] Action, 11 + your Signature Modifier for a [Save, Signature] Action, and for a Save Action that rolls an Attribute (such as [Body, Save]), 11 + that Attribute ÷ 2 (rounded up) + 1.

The tiers are the target's result: Tier 1 is the worst for them. So everything that follows your result on an attack follows theirs here, reversed: a [Save, Weapon] Action adds your Weapon Modifier to its damage ([Mod]) on Tier 1 and 2, and its Effects row happens on Tier 1 and 2.

A Save Action has no Critical, and it is not a Strike. Boons and banes on your own rolls don't apply to it; effects on the target's Saves do. Vulnerable gives the target that many banes on the Save, and Cover gives them boons, counted from the center of the area's Point of Origin to the target.

New trait — Shot

Shot Actions need a ranged weapon that you don't throw, such as a bow or a crossbow, and use its Range as their Distance. A thrown weapon can't be used for them. A Weapon Action without this trait gets it when you use such a weapon (so a Weapon Attack with a crossbow is a Shot).
