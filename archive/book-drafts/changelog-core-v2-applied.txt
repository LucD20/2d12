#region 1. Decisions I recorded

 1. Collision counts the UNUSED squares of the destination the origin chose. The origin can always move less than the value:
    no damage, or aim into an obstacle to collide on purpose.
 2. Pulled: the origin can end the movement next to itself (no damage) or name its own square as the destination (Collision, both take damage).
 3. Slid and Pulled get diagrams.
 4. Slid and Reposition: the origin needs Line of Effect to the creature.
 5. Reposition: no Reach limit, no Size limit, needs Line of Effect. The Reposition Action is deprecated and will be rewritten.
 6. "Moving and Leaving Your Square": "Forced Movement (Pushed, Pulled and Slid)" and a new row "Reposition": No, No, Yes.
 7. Flanking: a weapon Property for Fists, "not allowed to flank".
 8. Minor enemies = Minions and Grunts, defined in the Bestiary.
 9. Collision, Deadly Hazards, Leaning, Corners and Squeezing stay as subsections inside their sections.
10. Merge the Size "cube" paragraph and the Elevation bullet.
11. Solid corner: the corner POINT where four squares meet is "occupied" by a wall or a solid object (your definition; my first wording was wrong).
12. Fists have the Unarmed property and NEVER flank, Brawler included. "Unarmed" is used nowhere else in the book, Foundry or the compendium: kept.
13. The Reposition Basic Action: use my draft for now (= the old card, unchanged; see the entry), marked "to be rewritten".
14. Losing Line of Effect during a Slid or Reposition ends the movement with no damage.
15. Forced Movement and Reposition never squeeze a creature.
16. The "Forced Movement at a glance" table goes in the book.
17. Areas moves: it goes after Cover (order below).

#region 4. GLOSSARY AND RULES LANGUAGE (ready to apply with your OK)

[ADD] Rules Language, a new item after "Allies":
   "**Origin.** The origin of an effect is the creature that uses the Action, or the Point of Origin of an Area. When a rule says "the
   origin chooses", the origin decides, not the creature affected."
[ADD] Rules Language, moved from Movement:
   "**Since your last turn, this turn.** A feature that says "since your last turn" counts from the end of your last turn. One that says
   "this turn" counts from the start of your turn."
[CHG] Rules Language, the movement notation row: it lists "Evade 2, Pushed 2, Advance 2" (movement of up to that many squares).
   -> "Evade 2, Advance 2, Pushed 2, Pulled 2, Slid 2, Reposition 2"; the links "Advance and Evade" -> "Movement" (Advance, Evade) and to
   each new heading.

Glossary rows (new or changed):
   Forced Movement   | Movement you impose on another creature (Pushed, Pulled or Slid): no reactions, ignores Difficult Terrain, each square counts 1.
   Pushed            | Forced Movement away from the origin, up to its value, inside a 45 degree cone.
   Pulled            | Forced Movement toward the origin, up to its value, inside a 45 degree cone.
   Slid              | Forced Movement along a path the origin chooses (it can curve); the origin needs Line of Effect.
   Reposition        | Moving a creature along a path you choose, up to its value. Not Forced Movement: it passes through creatures, never enters hazards, needs Line of Effect.
   Origin            | The creature that uses an Action, or the Point of Origin of an Area.
   Collision         | Where a Forced Movement stops when something blocks the destination the origin chose; the unused squares hurt.
   Corners           | You can't cut a solid corner: a wall or solid object touching the corner point where four squares meet.
   Squeezing         | Moving through a gap one square narrower than you: Difficult Terrain, and Doomed and Vulnerable if you stop there.
   Ranged Combat While Engaged | Using a [Ranged] Action while Engaged: 2 banes. Against a creature Engaged with someone else: 1 bane.   (renames "Ranged Combat")
   Minor enemies     | Minions and Grunts (CV Multiplier below 1).
   Unarmed           | A weapon property: the weapon doesn't threaten, so you can't flank with it. Fists have it.
   Moved / Left your square | add "Reposition" to the existing texts.
   Movement          | replaces the links to "Advance and Evade".
