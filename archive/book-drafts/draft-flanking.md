# Draft: Flanking (NOT in the book or in Foundry)

Proposed place: Chapter 3, after Engaged and Ranged Combat. The diagrams are drafted in `diagrams/diagrams-draft.html` (Flanking has five panels).

## Your answers
1. **Boons:** the number of OTHER allies flanking: 2 flankers = 1 boon each, 3 = 2, 4 or more = 3.
2. **Which Actions:** only [Melee] Actions (not only Weapon ones). Wielding a Melee Weapon is only the requirement to flank.
3. **Who threatens:** Unconscious creatures don't; other conditions work as normal. Open idea of yours: instead of "wielding a Melee Weapon", use "as long as the creature can make a [Melee] Action that deals damage" (Fists are not weapons and need a free hand).
4. **The loop:** if everyone is flanking, no one is flanking. Count every threatening enemy to decide who is Flanked, even enemies that are Flanked themselves.
5. **Ranged Combat:** unchanged. Threatening uses Reach, Engaged uses adjacency.

## Wording, draft 2 (uses the "can make a [Melee] Action" idea)

### Threatening
As long as a creature can make a [Melee] Action that deals damage, it **threatens** every enemy within the Reach of that Action. An Unconscious creature threatens no one.

### Flanking
Creatures that threaten the same enemy **flank** it. Each flanking creature gains **1 boon** on its [Melee] Actions against that enemy for **each other flanking ally**, up to **3 boons**.

### Flanked
A creature is **Flanked** while two or more enemies threaten it. A Flanked creature does not flank: it isn't counted as a flanker, and it gains no boons from Flanking.

To decide who is Flanked, count every enemy that threatens the creature, even if that enemy is Flanked itself. So if everyone is flanking, no one is flanking.

### Examples (the panels of the diagram)
1. **Two flankers.** Nari and Kai both threaten a goblin: each has 1 boon on [Melee] Actions against it.
2. **More flankers.** A third ally also threatens it: 2 boons each. A fourth: 3 boons. A fifth adds nothing.
3. **Reach.** Nari holds a spear (Reach 2) and is 2 squares from the goblin. She threatens it and flanks with Kai, who is next to it: 1 boon each.
4. **A Flanked flanker.** Kai, Nari and Mara threaten goblin A; goblin B also threatens Kai. Kai is Flanked (by both goblins), so he doesn't flank: Nari and Mara have 1 boon each and Kai none. Goblin A is Flanked too (three creatures threaten it), so it doesn't flank Kai either: goblin B alone doesn't flank him.

## Small points left
- **Which Reach:** a creature may have several [Melee] Actions that deal damage with different Reach (a spear at 2, Fists at 1). Proposal: the longest.
- **What "can make" means:** having the means now (a weapon in hand, or a free hand for Fists), not having AP left and not being in range.
- **A naming note:** in the basic panels the enemy being flanked also has the "Flanked" ring, because the rule says anyone threatened by two or more enemies is Flanked.
