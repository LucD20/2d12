#region 1. DECISIONS RECORDED

 1. SR = Size Rank. Used in Requirements lines; "Size" stays as the concept.
 2. The Handle Actions share one ladder, three versions each (1A, 2A, 3A):
        1A: No effect / 1 / 2     2A: No effect / 2 / 4     3A: 1 / 3 / 5     (Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3)
 3. Shove: Pushed. 3A Tier 1 is Pushed 1 (your typo fixed). 2A Tier 1 becomes "No effect" (a nerf, intended).
 4. The Reposition Action is renamed Relocate (the movement keeps the name Reposition). Value stronger than before (intended).
 5. The new Action is Haul (Pulled). It needs Reach 2+. A weapon Property for Haul comes later (nothing written now).
 6. Strike on all three. Relocate and Haul: the target must remain within your Reach throughout the whole movement.
 7. Steadiness on every species: 0 by default, never negative. Foundry: buttons 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. A creature can choose not to apply it.
 8. Immobilized: an Action or effect that attempts to Force Move or Reposition you and doesn't roll is automatically disrupted.
    An Action that rolls (and a Save Action, whose origin rolls with the Action's Modifier) must beat the Immobilized DC =
    11 + the Modifier the origin of the condition used. The Immobilized DC replaces the "Opposed Check" (the book has none).
 9. Grab comes later: nothing about Grab is written here.
10. Fists become an ordinary weapon (properties Barehanded, Melee, Unarmed; NAME OF THE LAST ONE PENDING, see section 5). While you have a free
    hand, your Fists count as a weapon you are wielding (also next to a sword): the classes' Weapon Actions work with them. Brawler's "Your Fists count as a Melee Weapon" and the rules that say "Fists aren't a Weapon" go away.
11. Flanking: Fists never flank, EXCEPT with Brawler (Pummel allows flanking). This replaces my earlier line "Brawler included" in the
    Flanking region of the Core changelog: the Unarmed property says "unless a feature says otherwise", and Brawler says it.
12. Steadiness: a stat on each species (Dwarf 1, the others 0); Features, Talents and armor can add to it.

#endregion

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Ch.8 - Conditions - Immobilized

[REV] Immobilized (the first paragraph is today's, unchanged; the rest is new). Changed from your text: "you" and "the Action you use" mixed two
      people, so it says the target and the origin; the Opposed Check is replaced by the Immobilized DC; Save Actions are covered:
   "### Immobilized
   You can't use Move Actions or Evade. Unless otherwise specified, you can remove Immobilized by using an Interact (1 AP) Action.

   An Immobilized creature is also hard to move. An Action or effect that attempts to Force Move or Reposition you and doesn't roll is
   automatically disrupted: you aren't moved.

   > Example: Jared is Immobilized and his ally, John, has a feature that says "At the start of your turn, you can Reposition 3 an ally
   > within Reach." If John attempts to use this on Jared, nothing happens and the effect is wasted.

   If the Action rolls, the movement also needs the roll to beat the **Immobilized DC**: 11 + the Modifier the origin of the condition used
   (the Power Modifier for a Power Action, the Weapon Modifier for a Weapon Action, and so on; 13 if the condition has no origin; the highest
   if there are several). A Save Action doesn't roll, but it always has a Modifier: the origin rolls 2d12 + that Modifier against the Immobilized
   DC. If the result is below the Immobilized DC, you aren't moved. The rest of the Action works as usual."
   [DECIDED] your point: a Save Action always has a Modifier (its Weapon, Power or Attribute trait), and the one causing the Save rolls with it.
   [Q] A Save Action that moves several Immobilized creatures: ONE roll, compared with each creature's Immobilized DC. [default: one roll]

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- What changes in play

 - Fists roll the Weapon Modifier and add it to damage: about +2 damage per hit at level 1 (before: the highest of Body, Instinct and Mind, and no
   damage bonus). Fists T2 is 3 and T3 4.5 on average, like a weak weapon.

 - Brawler is thinner: its gain is Pummel (1d6 at Tier 3 instead of 1d4, [Mod] at Tier 1 instead of 0) and flanking. [Q] Rebalance Pummel or give Brawler
   something else later? (Not now: nothing changes in this pass.)
 - Opportunity Attack and Charge (a Weapon Attack at the end) now work unarmed.


 [NOTE on removing the Weapon trait from Pummel and the Fists card] If the trait went, Fists and Pummel would roll an Attribute again, use
 flat damage instead of [W], lose "Distance: Weapon", and stop working with Leg Sweep (a Weapon Technique needs a Weapon Action). It would also bring
 back "Fists roll against AD although they are not a Weapon Action" and the other rules this region deprecates. The reason I think you may want it
 gone: with "if you wield no weapon" a swordsman with a free hand couldn't punch any more. That is fixed by the wording above ("while you have a
 free hand"), so the trait can stay.

#region 3. SECOND PASS: WHAT ELSE CHANGES

 A. The word "Size" in comparisons becomes SR in three Basic Actions lines (Shove, Relocate, Trip) and in the Size section. Elsewhere "Size"
    stays (the Size table, Squeezing, Elevation, Species). No rule changes.
 B. Brawler's Pummel Critical uses the Reposition Action by name ("Reposition (1A)"): it must say Relocate. Found by searching the book.
 C. Nobody has Reach 2+ today (species have Reach 1, a Large creature still has Reach 1), so Haul can't be used until a feature or the
    Hooked property exists. That is expected; it is written down so it doesn't look like a bug.
 D. Relocate at Reach 1 can only move the target around the 8 squares next to you (it must stay within Reach), passing through creatures;
    Reposition 5 at 3 AP is a long walk around you. Fine, but it is not "5 squares anywhere".
 E. Balance: a Shove into an adjacent wall (Pushed 5, 3 AP, Tier 3) is 10 damage, against 19 to 26 for a 3 AP Special at Tier 3: weaker, as it should be.
 F. Shove 2 AP Tier 1 (was Pushed 1) is now "No effect". It matches Relocate and Haul (Tier 1: no effect at 1 and 2 AP).
 G. Immobilized now interacts with Steadiness and Forced Movement: Steadiness applies after the Immobilized check.
 H. The Reposition section already says "Your Reach and the creature's Size don't limit it"; the limits are now in the Action. Consistent.
 I. The glossary gets SR and Steadiness (the Actions have no glossary rows today, like Shove and Trip).
