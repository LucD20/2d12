   You can't cut solid corners when you move. Moving diagonally takes you through the corner point where four squares meet. If a wall or anything solid occupies that point,

#region 6. PULLED

[CHG] The two Pulled figures were wrong, as you said: C5 and E5 are next to you from C4 and E4 already, and the squares were not a cone. The cause: the cone counted its 45 degree edge,
   and "closer" is measured in a straight line, so a square next to you looked closer than another square next to you. The fix is three things: a cone under 45 degrees with its apex on the
   creature; each square closer than the last; and a creature next to the origin stops.
[REV] Pulled (the paragraph "That cone is every step going in a direction within 45 degrees..." is replaced; "narrowing as it gets closer" was my mistake: a cone from the creature widens toward the origin):
   "### Pulled
   Being **Pulled** is a Forced Movement, the opposite of Pushed. Pulled has a value: up to that many squares you are moved. The origin chooses the destination. Each square you are
   pulled into must be closer to the origin than the previous one (measured in a straight line between the centers of the squares), and the path must stay inside a cone that has its apex
   on you and points toward the origin.

   That cone is every direction less than 45 degrees from the straight line from you to the origin: the 45 degree edge is not part of it. Each square counts as 1 toward the value, even
   when it is diagonal. A creature that is next to the origin can't be pulled any closer, so it stops there.

   You can't be pulled into the origin's square. The origin can end the movement next to it, or choose its own square as the destination: then Collision applies, with the origin as the
   creature in the way, and both take damage for the unused squares.

   Example: A goblin stands directly above you, 3 squares away, and you use Pulled 3 on them. They can end in any square in the cone shown below: straight toward you after 1 step, or
   any of the 3 squares next to you after 2 steps. Then they stop. You can leave them there, with no damage, or choose your own square as the destination: the 3rd square is then unused,
   Collision applies and you and the goblin take 1 damage each."
[CHG] The figures (see the image; "O" is "Y"):
   Straight: the goblin on D2, you on D5: D3 (1 step), C4, D4, E4 (2 steps). That is your list.
   Diagonal: the goblin on D2, you on A5: C3 (1), B3, B4, C4 (2), A3, A4, B5, C5 (3): a cone.
