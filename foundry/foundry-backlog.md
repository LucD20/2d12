## The basic mechanics (code, no settings)
The core rules every creature has (decided in the editability sweep, 2026-10-31). Anything not on this list that the system automates must be a setting or a field on an item (test t164 fails on a card name in module/ code).
- **Turns and AP:** 4 AP and the Reaction AP each turn (apPerTurn, getRAP, combat.mjs startTurn; v0.0.243), a Reaction paid with the Reaction AP first (payReaction), the kept AP as Guard at the end of the turn (keepApGuard, guard.mjs), Reset Turn, End turn, the AP ticker / Turn Log (ticker.mjs), the 3 Strikes and Frequencies (usage.mjs), out-of-turn checks.
- **Initiative:** the roll, the dialog and the Initiative card (initcard.mjs), the turn order and its zipper, a creature added mid-combat.
- **Swap Initiative and the thaw** (combat.mjs: 1 AP, frozen until its slot, no second swap; sysmsg.mjs cards).
- **Escalation (a level since v0.0.243):** 1 at the start (2 with Momentum, a world setting), the gain each round, +1 on a Critical once an encounter, the reset to 1 after combat (escalation.mjs); an Action's Escalation is a requirement; the class hook is a setting ("Escalation hook", states.mjs and mark.mjs); an item's Escalation minimum is its own setting.
- **Health:** the Health line (data.mjs healthLine: the class's values, the species' bonuses, the Stage steps, the floor), Bloodied / Strained as hooks announced in the chat (twod12.mjs, states.mjs), Guard and the Guard of kept AP (damage.mjs, guard.mjs), Down, Desperate, Dying, Shattered, Unconscious and waking (down.mjs, twod12.mjs registerDeadHooks), an NPC's Dead overlay, Rally Points and Breathers (rally.mjs, rest.mjs), Exhaustion and sleep (sleep.mjs, clock.mjs).
- **Movement:** Speed, Advance / Evade / Crawl / Stand / Charge, Engaged, Opportunity Attack prompts, Corners, Squeezing, Forced Movement (Pushed, Pulled, Slid, Reposition; a straight path takes one tie side, linearea.mjs), Steadiness, Collision, Immobilized's DC (move.mjs), Hazardous Terrain regions (hazard.mjs), Flanking (flanking.mjs), Leaning (lean.mjs), Cover and Line of Effect (cover.mjs, loe.mjs, heightcover.mjs), light and senses (light*.mjs, senses.mjs, sightgate.mjs).
- **Conditions from the core rules:** their effects are settings on the Conditions compendium; the code only gives durations (EoT, rounds), linked conditions, the Imperiled check (combat.mjs), Prone / Hindered / Immobilized handling in movement.
- **Weapons and Mastery:** Fists as a weapon on every creature, hands and grips (hands.mjs; main and off hand, loadout.mjs, v0.0.208), Reload, Mastery gain on a Tier 3 and the Weapon Techniques at 3 (mastery.mjs).
- **The cards come from `book/cards/*.json`** (pack CS, v0.0.227 to v0.0.232): `tools/content.mjs` builds every Action, Feature, Talent, Technique and NPC Action of the compendium from them (`tools/cardgen.mjs`); a card's automation is its `foundry` block (settings, Effects applied, appended buttons), never code. **The Basic Actions** (basic.mjs; their data is `module/basic-data.mjs`, **generated** from `book/cards/basic-actions.json` by `node tools/gen-basic.mjs`: edit the card, never the generated file; test t177) and their names in the Actions tab groups (config.mjs basicGroups).
- **The mark's life** (mark.mjs): an Action with "Marks the target" marks; the mark ends when an encounter starts and when the marked creature goes Down; the crosshair. What the marker is told: Chat message settings.
- **Class levels and grants** (classlevels.mjs, classwindow.mjs, grants.mjs: the items a class or a Feature grants; removing a Feature removes what it granted).
- **Display only (no rule):** the "Class picks" card groups Features whose name says Pick or Hallmark Action (actor-sheets.mjs isPick), a layout choice, not a card rule.

## Known limits (by design for now)
- **Effect Details tab** (v0.0.112; replaced by v0.0.323, PACK EFFECT-SHEET: its limits are in the v0.0.323 item above): not tried in Foundry yet. Which settings are open is kept only while the sheet stays open (they start collapsed again). A "+ Any of" row that was opened but left empty goes away when the sheet is reopened. The JSON tab of the effect window is unchanged. Editing an applied effect (v0.0.113): a light setting changed there is not re-applied to the token, editing the value of a condition in its list doesn't change the copy it already applied, and the editor's title keeps the old value until it is reopened.
- **Clock and Exhausted:** only characters are tracked by the clock; rolls that don't open the roll dialog skip the Exhausted penalty (initiative applies it since v0.0.310); going back in time doesn't undo Exhaustion already counted; the Mind Save at threshold 0 is a Mind Check (Save-only modifiers don't apply) and the GM applies its result.
- **Several targets:** a creature with no Line of Effect, Unsensed (for non-Area Actions) or out of range is left out with a warning; the dialog's one DC field overrides every target; Saves and Cleave on the card use the card's target list; the merged damage card can only be changed by its author or the GM; tile pictures use the token's texture.
- **Darkness, enemies' squares and the ruler:** the ruler counts 2 for such a square only if Foundry's own movement cost includes it. Your Foundry has `CONFIG.Token.movement.actions.walk.getCostFunction`, but the setting is read-only (v0.0.96 was refused). v0.0.97 works around a locked setting: it assigns, then defines the property, then swaps in a copy of the action, of the actions, or of the movement settings. It could not be tried in Foundry itself. Run `game.twod12.rulerInfo()`: `installed` and `how` say where and how it attached; if it failed, `failed` says why and `hints` says what is frozen. Until it attaches the ruler shows the plain distance and the chat message shows the right cost.
- **Conditions** (v0.0.106): a Conditions compendium; valued conditions (Empowered, Enfeebled) with a badge and click +1 / right-click -1; an effect applies conditions (Details tab, drag and drop, with values); two chips in the roll dialog; Tags as a selection (world setting `Tags`). Known limits: Empowered, Enfeebled and Vulnerable are valued (Exhausted keeps its own way); an old world copy of "Vulnerable 1/2/3" stays as it is (it still counts) until you replace it with the new Vulnerable; `shadowedEffects()` only looks at the Effects compendium now; the effect editor window (Shift+click) has no field for the value yet; a tag is not used by any rule yet (`hasTag` is ready). Existing worlds: effects that were conditions stay where they are in the world; only the compendium moved.
- **Round 9 rules** (v0.0.105): Empowered N and Enfeebled N (+N or -N per die, min 1), the Speed cap (`|uncapped:yes` for abilities that go beyond it), the dark-fight rule, the Interrupt/Trigger kind on Actions, Fear, Tags, and the same effect keeping the longer duration. **Not built:** tracking "moved" and "left your square" (store the square at the start and end of each turn and a flag set only by your own movement, when the first feature needs it), "increase your value by 1" stacking, and any Interrupt automation (by hand, as decided). Existing characters keep their old copies: Cut Through's Setup says "Evade up to 2" until edited, and an old Bloodlust effect that stored a tick counts as Empowered 1.
- **Vision** (v0.0.100, v0.0.101): the character tokens' vision follows their Senses (none, or darkvision for Dark Sight), and since v0.0.101 Foundry's basic sight also asks the rules of Light, so **Global Illumination can stay on**: a creature is shown only if the light on it (the scene's Darkness, adjusted by Adjust Darkness Level regions, Darkness sources and light sources) is not Darkness for its Sight. Turning Global Illumination off (`game.twod12.globalLight(false)`) is optional now, and only changes how the map looks. Existing characters keep their vision until you run `game.twod12.syncVision()` or save their Senses. Dim-light Sight has no Foundry equivalent. NPC tokens are not touched.
- **Darkness regions** (v0.0.101, v0.0.102): the Darkness at a point is Foundry's own answer when it has one (it accounts for Adjust Darkness Level regions), and the gate that hides creatures sits on Basic Sight, Light Perception and See Invisibility (Global Illumination shows tokens through Light Perception). If a creature inside a Darkness region is still seen, run `game.twod12.lightInfo()` and copy the console line "twod12 | light": it lists the detection modes Foundry has and the ones each token uses.
- **Hearing:** a creature that fights without being in the combat tracker is not heard (the GM can mark it Hidden); the Hide rules are still to be written; the range is the same for everyone unless a character's Senses say otherwise.
- **Cut Through** (v0.0.99): a character that already has it needs `|terrain:ignore` added to the Move tag of its Setup (the compendium's has it).
- **Flanking is SWADE-style** (v0.0.104, decided): Field Boons = other creatures that threaten the target minus the target's allies that threaten you, never below 0, then at most 2 (v0.0.301: the limit of Field Boons, after the cancels; was up to 3). There is no Flanked status.
- **Flanking** (v0.0.103): walls are not considered (the book doesn't say a Line of Effect is needed); allies and enemies are by token disposition; a [Melee] Action of an NPC counts only if it has the Melee trait, no Weapon trait, and damage on some tier; a weapon's `Reach` field is read as extra squares. The chip is on the roll only (the card lists it with the other modifiers).
- **Setup** (v0.0.98): only a Setup's Advance and Evade are made before the roll (other tags stay on the card); the movement is made before the AP is spent, so cancelling at the roll dialog after the Setup keeps the movement; closing the Setup window cancels the Action; it needs combat (or the setting).
- **Kept areas** are made of squares: the native rounded Emanation shape of V14 is not used.
- **Movement:** outside combat the buttons only show the area (nothing tracked; Charge's line and Evade's terrain are not enforced); Darkness as Difficult Terrain is counted only for tracked (combat) movement and only from light sources, magical darkness and the scene's light, not from other terrain; a Prone creature can still be moved by Forced Movement or Reposition.
- **Engaged:** adjacency and token disposition only (neutral creatures are nobody's enemy, hidden tokens are ignored); a creature that is Down but awake can be Engaged; only Unconscious and dead creatures can't. The roll dialog's "You are Engaged" chip reveals that something is next to you, not what.
- **Distances** (fixed in v0.0.98): `tokenDistance` now counts adjacent as 1 and every second diagonal as 2, as the book says. It feeds the Charge follow-up and the range of senses.
- **Unconscious:** every creature is Unsensed to an Unconscious one, so every Action against it is Favored by the Senses rule.
- **Conditions from the HUD** apply the compendium Effect with that name; a world Effect with the same name wins over the compendium (see `game.twod12.shadowedEffects()`). The "Also applies" field of Effects exists but no Effect uses it now.
- **Not automated:** Ricochet's Line of Effect and Cover from the surface; Cover for area Saves; targeting walls and objects; hiding and sneaking; Disrupting Actions; which damage the Attribute joins for Magic Actions; where an Imperiled creature stands (the GM picks); Deadly Hazard damage.

## Tools (console or macros)
- `game.twod12.time(seconds)`: move the world clock (negative goes back).
- `game.twod12.exhausted(delta, actor)`: change Exhausted (default: the selected token).
- `game.twod12.refreshBasics()`: refresh the Basic Actions of every character and NPC you own (new texts, Crawl).
- `game.twod12.globalLight(false)` (or `(false, { all: true })`): turn Global Illumination off in this scene (or every scene), so Darkness hides tokens. `game.twod12.syncVision()`: every character's token vision follows its Senses. `game.twod12.visionInfo()`: why a token sees what it sees (a line of text to copy).
- `game.twod12.lightInfo()`: the light by the rules on the controlled tokens (or the characters), the Darkness regions with their raw settings, and whether Foundry's sight follows the rules (a line of text to copy).
- `game.twod12.rulerInfo()`: whether Darkness and enemies' squares are part of Foundry's movement cost (so the ruler counts them), where they were attached, and, if not, the cost-related names this Foundry has (the console line is plain text to copy).
- `game.twod12.shadowedEffects()`: world Effects that have the name of a compendium Effect, and what differs.
- `game.twod12.catalog` and `game.twod12.addHearing`: see the reference.
