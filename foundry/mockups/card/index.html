Foundry Action Card

Step 5 of the Foundry style plan. The Action card takes the book's look and the book's Structure together: the same card you read in the Paladin document, with what Foundry adds (the Use buttons, Your roll, what effects change) under it. Drawn with Smite (one Version) and Judge (two Versions, each a block). Dark on the left, Light on the right: with step 4's decision, each player's Foundry setting (Applications) picks one.

Today

Today's card (dark), with steps 2 and 3 already drawn in: the old Smite, old structure.

What changes, top to bottom:

  1. The frame: the book's flat tinted panel with a bronze rule above and below, in the place of the double frame.
  2. The Flavor goes to the top, in the serif italic, as in the book (today it closes the card under three diamonds).
  3. The traits: words in group order, separated by dots, a bronze line under them; no chips, no group labels (the book's decision). The automation rank stays at the right with its colors (grey, bronze, silver, gold, the Mithril rainbow, the Diamond blue).
  4. The Structure's lines: Distance and Target, then one labeled line each (Attack, Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, Special…), in the place of the Tier table and the Effects list. A Version is a block with its cost as the header.
  5. The Tier lines carry no color. The Tier colors (palette A) stay for results: the chat's stamp and ladder.
  6. The Use button (step 3's: outlined in the accent, filled on hover): in each Version's header; with one Version, at the end of the Your roll line.
  7. Your roll becomes a labeled line under a hairline: the Modifier, then Boons in the success color with a "+", Banes in the failure color with a "▼" (never color alone). The chips' boxes go.
  8. What effects change (Empowered, a bonus die) stays as a short note after its line, in the success or failure color.
  9. Glossary terms keep their tooltip and lose their color: a dotted underline.

Open on a sheet (under its row)

The row is the card's head, as today: the card adds no title. Durgan, Escalation 2, Empowered 1, Radiant Zeal active, Hindered; 1 AP left, so Judge's 1 AP Version can be paid but a 2 AP Smite can't (shown on the second card).

  • The Place Area chip becomes the same outlined button, in the Area line. The Ruler line (Designer mode only) stays as a small grey line at the bottom.
  • Not tried in Foundry. The text is the book's; the numbers on Durgan are made up for the picture.
