What changed from the first round

  • Single column at every width (the ladder on top, no side-by-side). At 420 px the Range column drops from the action rows, Frequency drops from the Escalation Actions (their name + Esc. pill need the room), and the green tickets move their badges (≠ targets, +1d6 Pi) into the expanded body.
  • Escalation Actions (Pinning Shot, Twin Shot, Vanish) are real Action rows (grip, arrow, name + “N Esc.” pill, cost chip, Range, Frequency, dark Use button), tinted by their Frequency as in the sheet. Passives are lighter rows with an amber edge and a tag (Active / Ready / Used). The rung number is the requirement, so no “needs N” text; a greyed row is one you can’t use now.
  • Granted tickets keep the stub; the Use button is blue (the granted button of v0.0.182) so it differs from the dark ones.
  • The same block sits above the list on the Favorites tab.
  • Colors are eyeballed from your screenshot (tint, chip and button colors, the red left edge); the real values are in the sheet CSS.
