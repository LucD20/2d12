W: fewer clicks for weapons

The use button of a [Weapon] Action shows the hand(s) instead of the "Which weapon?" window. Only the buttons that matter: one weapon that fits = the usual dice button (its tooltip names the weapon); a two-handed grip = one wide 2H button; two one-handed weapons = M (main hand) and O (off hand), a hand whose weapon can't be used for that Action greyed, with why. Fists count only when no held weapon fits (the Fists Basic Action is still there). Weapon Techniques and "Weapon used" Actions (Pummel, Leg Sweep) keep their one button.
