Hide by hand, in the HUD

A mockup: nothing here is in the system. Kaela's token is selected; the slices below are the right end of the HUD bar at real size (the right column at 240 px). Every button works, so the two layouts can be tried.

Two layouts for a creature's line. A, the word is the button: the mark, the name, then the state as one outlined button; a click turns Hidden into Unsensed and back. B, two marks: the name and its word as text, then the two marks side by side with the current one filled; a click on the other one switches.

Recommended: A. The state is read as a word in the same place it is changed, and with only two states a click can only mean "the other one". B shows which state is on more strongly, but its buttons are pictures alone, and without hover texts a player has to know the marks first. Both take one line a creature, and the Add line is the same in both.

What stays the same

  • The entry is a normal Situational entry (a bold word, a small tag, buttons) placed with Prone and Unloaded, under the urgent ones. It never pulses and never turns the tab gold.
  • No hover texts: the ✕ and B's marks carry an aria-label ("Remove Goblin Sneak", "Hidden to Orc Warrior").
  • The marks are the ones already chosen: a dashed ring is Unsensed, a half-filled disc is Hidden.
  • Whoever controls the token sees the entry and may use it, the GM or the player.
