The same in all three

  • A dock, not messages: it sits at the bottom of the chat, under the newest message, and never scrolls away. It is folded by default; each person unfolds it on their own client (remembered per client). It shows the creature whose turn it is; when the turn passes, it shows the next one.
  • The ⊟ Turn button takes the place of Foundry's Export Chat Log under the chat and hides or shows the dock completely (per client). Export Chat Log moves to the Journal tab's footer:
  • The unused AP line: "Kaela: Guard 8 (unused AP)", with "unused AP" linking to the book's Unspent AP (Chapter 2). It is a row of the dock, no longer its own chat message.
  • Advance shows while it happens: today the movement line is posted only when the movement ends. In the dock it appears as soon as the creature starts moving and its squares count up live (the pulsing dot), then it stays as "Advance 4".
  • Durations: at the start of a turn the dock lists the Effects that will tick at the end of that turn (the ones whose rounds count on this creature's turn, and its EoT ones), with "2 → 1" or "ends"; when the turn ends they turn into what happened ("1 round left", "ended"). No separate chat message for a tick or an end, since you said it should all go into the breakdown. If you still want one chat line when something ends (it drops off the dock at the next turn), say so.
  • Past turns: the dock shows only the current turn. Where should the past turns go? (a) nowhere, the Encounter Log keeps them; (b) the dock's ‹ › arrows to look back at earlier turns.
