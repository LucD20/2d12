A mockup (Lucas, 2026-11-20): the notes the system draws on the map while it asks something, today (redrawn from the code, v0.0.460) beside a proposal, with the HUD's Situational tab beside the map. Nothing in the system is changed. The goal is clarity before looks: at every moment the player knows
  1. what the map is asking of them now,
  2. which tokens or squares they can click,
  3. what each line and label means and what it costs,
  4. how to finish or cancel.
