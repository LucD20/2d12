Frightened is always of a source (the creature or object that applied it). While you see the source (Clear or Obscured to you), all your Rolls take 1 Bane, the Save that would end Frightened included, and every square that brings you closer to it is Difficult Terrain for you (Forced Movement ignores it). Out of your Sight (a wall, Darkness), the Bane and the Difficult Terrain stop until you see it again.
