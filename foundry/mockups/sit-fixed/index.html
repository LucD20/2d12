The Situational tab: buttons that stay put

A mockup: nothing here is in the system. Each picture is the right end of the HUD bar at its real size (the right column, 312 px wide, in the 150 px bar), with the system's own words and buttons.

The problem. A question in the Situational tab is a row that wraps: its sentence first, then its buttons after it. The area's sentence is rewritten on every mouse move, and the Line's Ties pair comes and goes in front of Cancel, so Cancel moves while the mouse is on its way to it.

The rule (A, recommended). Every entry is a frame whose parts have a fixed place: one title line, a text box of a fixed number of lines, then one row of buttons. In that row the main button starts at the left and the way out (Cancel, Pass, End movement, Give up, Don't move it) is always at the right end. Whatever changes (a count, a warning, the squares left) has lines of its own that are kept free while they are empty and cut with an ellipsis if they run over; a button that can only sometimes be used (Ties, Undo move, Take back a step, Charge) keeps its place and is shown off, never taken away; two wordings of one button (Advance instead / Evade instead) share one slot of one width.

A question asked on the map (an area, a pick, a movement, a Charge) takes the tab's whole height and is the first thing in it, so its row of buttons is the HUD's bottom edge and the way out is the HUD's bottom right corner, for every question. Anything else that arrives goes under it and cannot push it.

Every picture below was measured: within a kind, each button has the same position and size in every state (the dashed outlines are the first state's buttons, drawn on all of them).

10. The other way, B: the buttons in a column

The buttons stand in a column of a fixed width at the right, one slot a line from the top, the way out in the last slot (the same bottom right corner). The text has the left column and can be any length: nothing in it can reach the buttons, and no height has to be kept free.

B, the Line

B, the Charge

Recommended: A. It keeps the order every question has today (the sentence, the buttons, the way out last) and the full width for the sentence; the list entries (a Reaction, Prone) already look like it. Its cost is the Charge, which fills the tab to the pixel. B has room to spare for the Charge and never cuts a sentence, but the text is a narrow column beside a mostly empty one (a Line has two buttons, an Emanation one), and the stacked buttons look like nothing else in the HUD.

What the rule changes in what is shown

  • Shown off, no longer hidden: Ties Left / Right (the Line with no tie; the Charge already keeps their place), Undo move (before a move), Take back a step (before a step), Charge (no target, or too close), Hiding's Unsensed / Hidden (no new target), a creature already moved in a Push of several.
  • Kept, no longer removed: the Charge's pictures after a target is picked (the one picked is marked).
  • Moved: the line naming the keys goes above the buttons (so the buttons are the last row in every question); Hiding's Add line goes above its list; a Reaction's Pass and the Charge's Cancel go to the right end; Granted has one line a source.
  • Left out in the HUD: Forced Movement's small reminder of the rule (the Rules button opens it); the Charge's line of keys.
  • No new control, no new word.
