Draft 2, nothing built. The first draft reviewed against the system's style (STYLE-3), the research, Foundry and your HUD decisions of 2026-11-12, then against the rules. What changed and why is first; the pictures follow; the decisions are at the end. Drawn at 1480 px; scroll sideways on a small screen.

The decisions (my pick first; answer by exception)

  1. Health, AP, Guard and uses on the HUD (reverses 2026-11-12's "no Health, AP, Guard or uses"): yes, as your module; the Turn dock stays for the turn's log · no, as decided then.
  2. The party strip when nothing is selected: for everyone, the PCs' cards, Momentum at its left · GM only · off.
  3. Grouping: tabs by kind and columns by Frequency, Actions as ruled lines · today's flat row with categories.
  4. Height: 150 px with a fold to one 40 px line · 70 px as decided (fewer lines per column, more "+N more").
  5. Health: two thin bars, Vigor over Resolve, the Nonlethal part hatched · one Health Pool bar with the thresholds marked.
  6. The roll on each line: the Modifier and the Defense, the trait that limits it (Strike), the next use's Bane · only the cost.
  7. The pinned Actions (today's Quick slots): gone; the tabs show everything · kept as a first tab, "Pinned".
  8. The side panel: hands and conditions on top, a waiting Reaction or the NPC's notes, the hotbar · none (wider columns; hands and conditions move to the identity column).
  9. The Reaction alert: the chime, the ring and text on the map, the HUD's tab, each a setting, all on · fewer of them.
  10. The combat strip: the five changes (the HUD's AP marks, plain words, Bloodied / Strained / Down tags for everyone, Slowed struck out, Momentum beside the round) · as today.
  11. Resources by hand (Daggerheart Sleek): right-click AP, Rally or Escalation: +1 / −1 (the owner and the GM) · read only (the sheet changes them).

Kept from today: a click on a Version opens the roll dialog ("Skip roll dialog"), right-click the Action card, several tokens selected show their cards (as the strip), the pick bar's questions in the middle, the players list above the left end.
