The decisions (my pick first; answer by exception)

  1. The party strip when nothing is selected: for everyone, the PCs' cards · for the GM only · off.
  2. Grouping: tabs by kind (Actions, Reactions, Basic, Escalation) and columns by Frequency · today's flat row with categories.
  3. Health: two thin bars, Vigor over Resolve (the states are per threshold) · one Health Pool bar with the two thresholds marked.
  4. The roll on each tile: the Modifier and the Defense ("Weapon +1 vs AD") · only the cost.
  5. The Basic tab: all Basic Actions, the rare ones behind "+N more" · only the common five (Weapon Attack, Advance, Evade, Steel Yourself, Interact).
  6. The pinned Actions (today's Quick slots): gone; the tabs show everything · kept as a first tab, "Pinned".
  7. The hotbar in the HUD's right end: kept, under the side panel · Foundry's own place.
  8. The Reaction alert: the chime, the map ring and text, the HUD's tab, each a setting, all on · fewer of them.
  9. The side panel: the hovered tile's line, a waiting Reaction, the NPC's notes · no side panel (more room for columns).

What stays from today's HUD: one click on a Version opens the roll dialog ("Skip roll dialog" setting), right-click the Action card, several tokens selected show their cards (as the party strip), the pick bar's questions in the middle. Not tried in Foundry; the numbers are made up for the picture.
