Today (v0.0.393)Proposed

What changes

  • Traits as words ("Shot · Strike") with a bronze line under them, as on the roll dialog and the approved Action card. The filled blue and red chips go. This is on every chat card, attack cards included.
  • Trait words in the text ("[Ranged, Shot]") in ink and semibold, with no colored fill.
  • The card's dividers become plain hairlines, without the small diamonds.
  • End of Turn lines and the Turn panel: a flat surface with one thin line, no banded header and no rounded frame. The title is semibold.
  • The AP marks are the HUD's: bronze squares, a hairline, the Reaction diamond. The lavender dots were a leftover (STYLE-3 decided AP in ink, not blue). On a row, AP spent by that Action is a red outlined square.
  • Game words in ink: "+2 +R" and "Guard 4" lose their blue. The round tags ("1 rd", "ends") become outlined.

What stays

  • The Frequency square and word, the granted-use buttons (already outlined), the automation diamond's colors, the fonts (Source Sans 3 already loads; your screenshot matches mine letter for letter).
  • The light theme gets the same changes in its colors.
