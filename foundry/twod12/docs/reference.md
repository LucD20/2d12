# 2d12 System for Foundry VTT: Reference

How the `twod12` system works (the opening sections were last fully checked at v0.0.51; the compendium table, the tabs and the NPC sheet were brought up to date at v0.0.166; the Changelog at the end is current). The rules themselves are in the Core Rules book; this page covers what Foundry does with them. A short changelog is at the end.

## Getting started

- **Install:** unzip `twod12.zip` into `Data/systems/`. Start a **new world**: data from before v0.0.50 is not converted any more.
- **Compendiums** (in the system, so every world has them):

| Pack | Content |
|---|---|
| Classes and Species | Human, Dwarf, Elf; Paladin, Archer |
| Features and Talents | The Paladin's and the Archer's Features (Divine Power, Marked Quarry, Bow Mastery, their Picks and Escalation lists...) and the Talents that aren't Actions (Battering Charge, Brawler, Kickboxer...) |
| Actions | 48: the Paladin's and the Archer's Actions (Smite, Lay On Hands, Double Shot, Mark Quarry, Hunter's Focus...), Weapon Techniques (Reaping Sweep, Lock & Loose...) and the Actions that are Talents (Aim, Opportunity Attack, Cut Through...) |
| Weapons | Greatsword, Crossbow; Light, Medium and Heavy Armor |
| Effects and Conditions | Two packs: the effects the Actions use, and the Conditions (Empowered, Enfeebled, Hindered, Immobilized, Imperiled, Leaning, Prone, Quickened, Slowed, Vulnerable) |
| NPCs | Bestiary NPCs (a role and ratings, see the Builder tab): Skeleton Archer and Skeleton Swordsman (Minions), Goblin Skirmisher (Grunt), Roadside Bandit and Dire Boar (Standard), Skeleton Captain (Elite) |
| Player Characters | Human Paladin, Elf Archer |

- **Making a character:** create a character, then drop a Species and a Class on it (one window asks all the Class choices). Every character and NPC gets the Basic Actions automatically.
- **Editing the compendiums:** unlock a pack (right-click > Toggle Edit Lock). Edits live in the system folder, so a reinstall overwrites them. The source of the starting content is `tools/content.mjs`; `node tools/build-packs.mjs` rebuilds the packs from it.

## The character sheet

(v0.0.289, PACK SHEET: Lucas's design, `foundry/mockups/sheet-prototype.html`. The NPC sheet keeps the older layout, below.)

- **Header:** portrait, name, "Level N · Stage N · Species Class · Size" (the Level is changed on the Build tab). On the right: **Roll Initiative** while you are in a combat and haven't rolled (it is greyed with no combat), then "In combat · Round N". The eye is **Senses**.
- **Combat strip** (in combat): **Action Points** (amber pips available, hollow spent, a crossed pip for each AP Slowed took this turn; the teal diamond is the Reaction AP; click a pip to correct), **Guard** (all your Guard; "+N per kept AP" by Stage; click +1, right-click −1), **Escalation** (three rungs with your Escalation Passives, "+ Apex" on the third; the lit ones are reached; the hooks still free this encounter; a Momentum badge), **Hands** (a tile per hand, one wide tile for a two-handed grip; click a tile: Regrip, Release, Draw, Stow, with the cost on each line, free out of combat) and "Strikes this turn N / 3". Out of combat the strip is one line and the Hands.
- **On you:** a chip per condition and effect (red harmful, teal helpful; click to open it), Slowed with what it does ("Slowed 2 · regain 2 AP"), the Down state, named Wounds, Untrained armor. Drop a condition or an effect anywhere on the sheet.
- **Sidebar:** **Health** (Vigor and Resolve bars: solid lethal, striped nonlethal; BLOODIED / STRAINED tags; "N to Down"; click a bar +1 damage, right-click −1, Shift nonlethal, Ctrl 5; click the numbers to type them). **Down mode** (damage at the Health Pool): a red panel with the state from the book (Dying, Shattered, Desperate, Unconscious), "Dying Table rolled", **Keep fighting**, **Collapse** (Give up when Shattered; it asks first) and the **end-of-turn Body / Mind Check**. **Rally** pips. **Defenses:** AD, WD and the four Vs. an Attribute (13 + half). **Attributes** (click: Check; Shift-click: Save). **Movement**. **Modifiers · Save DC** (11 + the Modifier). **Wounds** with Roll Dying / Roll Shattered.
- **Tabs:** the Favorites star, **Actions**, **Effects**, **Items**, **Build**, **Mastery**, **Upkeep**, **Notes**.
- **Actions:** sections by Frequency (Granted and Escalation 3 only in combat; Once per round, Once per turn, Once per encounter, Once per day, At will, Reactions with Opportunity Attack). Columns: Action (click the row: its card opens under it), Cost, Distance, Vs. (a Defense, or a Save in short: "App Save"), and Use (a weapon Action: a weapon button per hand, "2H" / "1H"; Reactions teal). An Action you can't use now is faded with a dashed button; hover it for the reason. **Basic Actions** in four columns (Attack, Movement, Control, Utility); one you can't use is grey with its reason (No free hand, Not Prone...); Weapon Attack shows the At-will Banes of its next use. Right-click a row: Favorite, Send to chat, Edit, Remove.
- **Effects:** the conditions (their full text) and the effects on you (text, source, duration).
- **Items:** weapons (Damage by Tier, Mastery, Dropped / On you / In hand), armor (Armor value → AD, Ward, trained or not; Dropped / Carried / Worn), gear and coins; Generate Weapon, New Item.
- **Build:** a red banner when a card fills no slot (**Review**: keep or remove each); the Species and Class cards (Choices); a block per level with the slot each card fills; the Escalation picks; "Next levels" up to your next Stage; the Talent drop zones. The Level box is at the top; click a row to open its card.
- **Mastery:** a block per kind of weapon, a bar with the Technique (3), Expert (8) and Master (15) marks.
- **Upkeep:** Breather, Sleep (greyed during the 16 hours) and Rest, each asks first and posts a chat card; the Upkeep placeholder; the Wounds.

**NPC sheet (v0.0.291):** the same layout as the character sheet: the role chip, Stage and CV in the header, Post stat block (GM); the strip with AP, Guard, Elite Escalation (an Elite), This turn (Strike diamonds) and Senses; tabs Actions, Effects, **Builder** (the role, Level, and a clickable 5-step bar for each rating: the numbers are derived from the ratings), Loot (Give to a PC) and GM notes. The Basic attack's rating is set on its Action.

## Using an Action

Click its name on the sheet. The **roll dialog** shows the Action as it will roll:
- One button per **Version** (1AP, 2AP...) and a live **preview** of the card with the formulas resolved, the average damage and the chance of each tier (and of a Critical).
- **DC** and **Modifier**; **Favored** and **Doomed** (3d12, keep the two highest or lowest; Favored: any pair of the three is Doubles, three of a kind a Critical; both on is a normal roll); **Boons** and **Banes** buttons (left-click +1, right-click -1; v0.0.301: each is +1 / -1, they cancel, 5 or more left make the roll Favored / Doomed; the ones added by hand have no name).
- **Effect chips** for everything that changes the roll, checked by default when they apply: conditions (Bloodied, Strained, Prone), Cover, range bands, At-will uses, two-handed in one hand, Obscured, Hidden, Unsensed, Unconscious. Uncheck one to ignore it.
- **Damage line:** Reload shot (Favored roll and Empowered damage; the weapon becomes unloaded) and Empowered (every damage die one size larger; d12 becomes 2d6).
- **Forced tier** (testing): click a Tier in the preview to force that result; click again to undo. The card says "Tier forced".

**What Foundry works out for you:**
- **The roll:** 2d12 + the Attribute from the Action's traits (Body, Mind, Appeal, Instinct; the highest if several), or the Weapon Modifier for Weapon Actions and the Power Modifier for Power Actions.
- **Vs.:** the DC is 13 + half the first target's Attribute (rounded up), plus defense bonuses. "The attribute rolled" uses the one you rolled (Trip).
- **Weapon Actions** take Melee or Ranged from the wielded weapon, need a weapon with that property, and measure the range: far range 1 bane, maximum range 2 banes, out of range a warning.
- **Line of Effect and Cover:** with your token selected and a target targeted, Foundry draws lines from the center of your square to the target's four corners and counts those blocked by walls: 1 = Low (1 Field Bane), 2 = Medium (2), 3 = High (Doomed, v0.0.301), 4 = no Line of Effect; the token controls' **Line of Effect** toggle draws them, **Show flanking** draws who flanks whom (v0.0.302). A creature in the way gives Low cover. **Leaning** uses your best corner and lowers the cover against you by one level. Only Actions with a target; not area effects or Saves yet.
- **Perception is automatic.** When you target a token, the roll dialog works out how your token perceives it with your best sense, and how it perceives you: **Clear** (nothing), **Obscured** (your Actions against it take 1 bane; its Actions against you gain 1 boon), **Hidden** (yours are Doomed; its are Favored) or **Unsensed** (you can't target it, the GM can override as Doomed; its Actions are Favored). The chip or the Doomed/Empowered option says why ("Hidden: you only perceive the target by Hearing"). [Area] Actions skip all of it.
  - **Sight** uses the light at the target's brightest square and needs Line of Effect. **Light**: inside a light's bright radius is Bright, inside its dim radius is Dim; otherwise the scene's own light, from its Darkness level: Bright up to 0.30, Dim above 0.30, Darkness above 0.60 (world settings "Scene light", as light levels 0.7 and 0.4). The scene's settings show this under the Darkness slider. Darkness sources make a square Darkness.
  - **Hearing** gives Hidden for a creature that makes noise: it is in the combat that has started (and not defeated), it is not **Unsensed** (a creature that hides becomes Unsensed: the rules for hiding are not written yet, so the GM puts the Unsensed status on it), and it is within your Hearing range: 10 squares unless the character's **Senses** dialog says otherwise (0 is deaf). Walls don't matter. A token that is only heard shows to the players as a ripple. The GM's Hidden status counts as heard too, for a creature that makes noise outside the combat.
  - **Light overlay:** the light-bulb button in the token controls tints every square by its light level (Bright clear, yellow Dim, red Darkness), for whoever turns it on. It redraws when lights, tokens or the scene change; players only see it on squares they can currently see.
  - The roll dialog names what causes each option: "Target is Hidden (Doomed)", "Hidden (Favored)", "Target is Obscured (1 Bane)", "Obscured (1 Boon)", and likewise Imperiled, Unconscious, Darkness and Dim light.
  - **Senses** button on the sheet: Dim-light Sight, Dark Sight, "gives off no heat", and other senses with a range, what they give (Clear, Obscured, Hidden) and a condition (gives off heat; same ground, checked as the same elevation).
  - The GM's **Obscured, Hidden and Unsensed statuses** still exist as overrides (invisibility, magical darkness): they only make perception worse. Players see a Hidden token as a sound wave.
- **Unconscious:** all its Rolls are Doomed, except the end-of-turn Body Check of a Dying creature and Mind Check of a Shattered one.
- **Bloodied** adds 1 bane to [Strike] and [Weapon] Actions and to Body and Instinct Saves; **Strained** to [Magic] Actions and to Mind and Appeal Saves. Checks aren't affected.
- **Frequency:** At-will Actions take 1 bane for each use after the first in a turn; Round and Encounter Actions are dimmed once used; after 3 Strikes in a turn every Strike is dimmed. Nothing is blocked.
- **Requirements** are checked before the Action is used, where they can be: "Speed 3" (your current Speed, before any bonus the Action gives), "free hand", and "the target's Size is at most N higher than yours" (against your first target). **Reactions** have a teal "1R" badge; using an Action that isn't a Reaction outside your turn asks for confirmation.
- **Critical:** Doubles on a result that is already Tier 3, or a total 20 or more above the DC. Only Actions with a Critical row use it. Talents can add one (Opportunity Attack gives Weapon Techniques used out of turn the Critical "You disrupt the triggering Action before it happens").
- **General Effects** happen only on Tier 2 and 3 ("Effects (Tier 2+)"); on a Tier 1 they are dimmed and not applied. Actions with no roll apply theirs always.
- **AP** is spent in combat when the Action is used; with too little AP you are asked before going ahead.

## Chat cards

**Action card:**
- The title bar is the color of the Action's **Frequency** (At-will green, Round blue, Encounter red, no limit grey), with the cost badge and the category on the right. An optional italic **flavor** line, the traits as colored chips, then the Frequency line (Frequency ✦ target ✦ distance ✦ Vs.), Requirements and Trigger.
- **The roll** on one line (check, dice, modifier, total, vs DC); boon, bane and effect chips on a line under it when there are any.
- **The tiers**, labeled with the numbers for that DC (12 or less, 13-19, 20 or more; hover for "Tier N"). The tier you rolled is **green** ("Rolled"); a better tier reached with Doubles is **blue** ("Doubles"), and the rolled tier keeps its green. The Critical row sits under Tier 3, highlighted when it happens.
- The **odds** of each tier (testing; a client setting) under the tiers, then the **Effects** in a lightly tinted box.
- The **targets line** at the bottom, with a bullseye button to **retarget**: the card's targets become your current targets (or selected tokens), for the damage, Saves and Cleave that follow. Cards used with no target say "No target".
- **Roll Damage** posts the damage card; the Action card then collapses to its title and result (click the title to open it again).

**Description card** (click an Action's icon on a sheet, or open its item): the Action as written, one Version at a time; click the cost badges at the top to switch Versions. The same card shows in the item's window and under a row of the sheet.

**Damage card:** the same title bar ("Damage" as its badge, the Tier under it), a section per pool (Body, Mind, Choice) with a row per damage type, the **total** between two lines, the average and the chance of the total (testing), and the buttons: **Damage**, **Half** (rounds up), **Block** (no mechanics yet), and a **Lethal / Nonlethal** selector for damage with no lethality of its own. The bullseye retargets. Choice damage asks which pool when it is applied. Effects listed on the card are applied with Damage or Half.

**Save card** (the speech-bubble half of a Save button): the Action's color, the DC as its badge, the three tiers tinted red, green and blue, and a row per target with a small roll button named after the Attribute. Clicking it rolls for that target (only its owner or the GM), and the row shows the result (colored like its tier) and the damage taken, which is rolled and dealt automatically. With no token targeted, a Save card uses the targets of the Action card it came from.

**Talent and Feature cards** (sent to chat, or opened on the sheet) have a plain title bar, then the traits, Frequency and Requirements, the text, and what the item gives: the Actions it grants, its choice ("Choose 1"), its Talent slots and the Actions it modifies.

**Cards with no Action** (inline damage buttons, Initiative, Recovery, the tables) have a light title bar.

## Chat commands (inline buttons)

Type them in chat, item descriptions, effect notes and Action Effects. Format: `@Command[main value|key:value|key:value]`. Missing values are 0, except DC (13). `|label:Text` replaces the button text. Whoever clicks uses their selected token (each selected token gets its own dialog), or their assigned character.

**Names you can use:** Attributes `body`, `mind`, `appeal`, `instinct`. Traits `strike`, `melee`, `ranged`, `weapon`, `magic`, `divine`, `focus`, `handle`, `move`, `self`, `reload`, `twohanded`, `nonlethal`, `body`, `mind`, `appeal`, `instinct`. Pools `body`, `mind`, `choice`. Damage types by name: `acid`, `blunt`, `cold`, `electricity`, `fire`, `force`, `holy`, `pierce`, `psychic`, `slash`, `thunder`, `unholy`. Lethality `lethal` (default) or `nonlethal`.

| Command | Example | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `@Check` | `@Check[body\|dc:14\|adj:2\|boon:1\|bane:1\|traits:divine]` | Opens the roll dialog for that Attribute Check. `traits` turns on the effects that match. `sight:yes` makes it a Check that requires sight: the light where you are gives 1 bane (Dim) or Doomed (Darkness), after your Sight Senses. |
| `@Damage` | `@Damage[(1d4+1)[body,slash],1d6[mind,holy,nonlethal]]` | Rolls and posts a damage card. Instances separated by commas; `\|nonlethal` after the list makes all of them nonlethal. Text in quotes and effect items (`Item.xxxx`) can be added. |
| `@Save` | `@Save[appeal\|dc:13\|t1:1d6[mind,holy]\|t2:1d4[mind,holy]\|t3:0]` | A split button: the left half rolls the Save for the clicker (damage and effects of the tier are applied to them); the right half posts a Save card for the targets. A Save has its Attribute as a trait and remembers the traits of the Action it came from. |
| `@Template` | `@Template[emanation\|distance:2\|targets:enemies\|rounds:1\|difficult:yes]` | Places an emanation, burst or line (`@Template[line|distance:10]`: from your square, aimed with the mouse, Tab or the bar's button switches the tie side; preview follows the cursor; right-click or Esc cancels); the tokens inside become your targets. Options: `targets` (all, allies, enemies), `self`, `places` (keep it on the map), `rounds`, `expire:eot`, `difficult` (movement types), `difficultFor`. Squares grids only. `at:self` centers an Emanation on the user (no placing); `quick:yes` skips the dialog and uses the button's options. |
| `@Move` | `@Move[advance\|distance:speed]`, `@Move[evade\|distance:speed/2]`, `@Move[pushed\|distance:2]`, `@Move[pulled\|distance:2]`, `@Move[slid\|distance:2]`, `@Move[charge\|distance:speed\|min:3]` | Advance: free squares of movement. Evade: squares that don't trigger reactions, can't enter Difficult Terrain and can't be split. Slid and Reposition (`@Move[slid\|distance:2]`, `@Move[reposition\|distance:2]`, `\|reach:yes` keeps the target within your Reach): click the path square by square; Slid collides with what is in the way, Reposition stops before it with no damage and never enters Hazardous Terrain, and both end where the origin would lose Line of Effect. An Immobilized target is moved only by a roll that beats its Immobilized DC (a Save Action: the origin rolls 2d12 + its Modifier). Pushed: drag the target away from the user inside a 45° cone; hitting a wall or creature shows the collision damage. Pulled: drag the target toward the user inside a cone under 45° (the edge is out), stopping next to the user; the user's own square is a Collision with the user. A diagonal step never cuts a solid corner (a wall touching the corner point, or a Tile marked Solid), and a diagonal between two creatures is a Collision for Pushed and Pulled (Reposition stops there, no damage; for your own movement it is Difficult Terrain only between two unwilling creatures). Charge: a picker on the map (v0.0.218): click the destination; the straight line's squares are shown (blue passed, green end, red not legal), at least `min` squares, no Difficult Terrain, one tie side (Left / Right, Tab), and who is in Reach at the end. A shaded area shows how far the token can go; **End movement** drops what is left. |
| `@Condition` | `@Condition[prone]`, `@Condition[hindered\|rounds:2]`, `@Condition[immobilized\|eot]` | Applies the condition (the Effect item of that name) to the card's targets. |
| `@Rally` | `@Rally[1]`, `@Rally[2\|upto:yes]` | The clicker uses that many Rally Points (each removes ⌈Health Pool ÷ 4⌉ damage; you choose which damage when there are several kinds). |
| `@Guard` | `@Guard[1d8+mod]`, `@Guard[2d6\|self:yes]`, `@Guard[rally]` | Gives Guard (rolled when clicked). `mod` is the Modifier of the card's roll; `rally` is what one Rally Point of the creature gaining it heals. It goes to the targeted creatures on your side, or to you when you target none of them (`self:yes`: always you); each keeps the higher Guard. |
| `@Effect` | `@Effect[Compendium.twod12.effects.Item.xxxx]{+1 Defense for 1 round}` | Applies that Effect item to you or to the targeted creatures on your side ("you or one ally gain..."): the enemy you just hit is never chosen by accident. |
| `@Use` | `@Use[Weapon Attack\|count:2\|distinct:yes]`, `@Use[category:Weapon Technique]` | Lets you use other Actions as part of this one (Linked Actions): no AP unless `ap:N`, `boons:N`, a different target each use with `distinct`. They don't count for Frequency or the Strike limit. `nohandle:yes` ignores the Handle trait (Cut Through's Critical). `empowered:has(Battering Charge)` makes the damage Empowered (`yes`, or a `has()` condition). `source:Name` names the boon's chip in the roll dialog (by default, the Action whose card has the button). |
| `@Cleave` | `@Cleave[2]` | Target up to N creatures next to the original target and click: a damage card with your Attribute as damage, in your weapon's pool and type. |
| `@Reload` | `@Reload[weapon]` | Loads the weapon again. |
| `@Boon` | `@Boon[-1\|trait:strike\|rounds:eot]{Suppressed}`, `@Boon[1\|traits:ranged,weapon\|self:yes]` | Puts boons (or banes, with a negative number) on the next roll with those traits of the card's targets (or yours, with `self:yes`). It ends when used or at the end of the creature's turn (`rounds:2` for longer). The name in braces names the effect; without it, it is called "1 Bane on the next Strike" and so on. |

**Distances** can be expressions, rounded up: `speed`, `body`, `mind`, `appeal`, `instinct`, `level`, `weapon` (Weapon Modifier), `hp` (Health Pool), `rally` (damage a Rally Point removes). Example: `@Move[evade|distance:speed/2]`. **`has(Name)`** is 1 when the creature has an item or effect with that name (spaces and case don't matter), otherwise 0, so a bonus can depend on a Talent: `@Move[charge|distance:speed+2*has(Battering Charge)|min:3|label:Charge]` lets the Charge go 2 squares farther only for a character with the Battering Charge Talent. An Advance or Evade tag can end with `|terrain:ignore`: for as long as that free movement lasts, the creature may move through creatures (it can't end in one) and Difficult Terrain costs nothing extra, on the ruler too (Cut Through's Setup).

**Create Button** (Token Controls, GM): builds a Check, Damage, Save, Template, Move or Rally button from a form and puts it in the chat box (and the clipboard).

## Making content

**Action Generator** (Generate Action on an Action, a Talent or a Feature): AP, Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3 and Effects fields, with a live preview. Separate parts with commas:

| Text | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `1WB` | 1[W] to Body (`2WM` = 2[W] to Mind) |
| `1d6MHo` or `1d6MH` | 1d6, Mind, Holy. First letter the pool (B Body, M Mind, C Choice), then the type: two letters (Ac Bl Co El Fi Fo Ho Pi Ps Sl Th Un) or the one-letter shorthand (B Blunt, C Cold, H Holy, M Psychic, P Pierce, S Slash) |
| `AtB`, `AtSl` | [At] to Body (with a type) |
| `"Prone"` | text for that tier |
| `0` or `-` | nothing |

In tiers and Effects, these turn into buttons: `Advance 5`, `Evade Speed/2`, `Pushed 2`, `Cleave 1`, `2Emanation`, `2Burst`, `DC 14 Body Check`, `Appeal Save: 1d6 / 1d4 / 0 [M] Holy`, `uses 1 Rally Point`, `1d6MH` (a damage button). Other text stays as written.

**Items:**
- **Action:** Category and Level (shown on the right of the title; a category that starts with "Talent" makes it a Talent), Prerequisites (for Talents), "Modifies other Actions" (for Talents that change other Actions, such as Opportunity Attack), Frequency (Default, None, At-will, Round, Encounter; Weapon Actions default to Round), Targets, Distance (`reach`, `self`, `weapon` or text), Vs., Area (such as `1 Emanation within 10 sq.` or `Line 10`, shown where the Distance would be, with a **Place Area** button on its cards), Setup (what happens before the dice, buttons allowed), Requirements, Trigger, Reaction, Flavor (an italic line under the title of its cards), Traits, and **Edit Versions**: per Version the AP, No Tiers, damage Instances and text per tier, a Critical text and the Effects.
- **Weapon:** Properties (barehanded, two-handed, reload, melee, ranged), damage per tier and type, ranges, reach, and its Weapon Techniques (**Create Weapon Technique**).
- **Effect:** boons and banes (+1 / −1, they cancel; each setting can name them Field, Pressure or Support: up to 2 of each name on a roll, v0.0.301) (on the creature's rolls, on rolls against it, or on Saves against Actions with given traits), Speed changes, Bonus Damage for the next matching Action, what ends it ("Ends after an action" with traits), a duration, and **Grant Action Use** (free uses of an Action, shown in Granted). **v0.0.186, general automation** (on Effects and on a Feature's or Talent's passive): Boons and Banes and Bonus Damage can be **only the first matching Action each round** and **only against the creature it marked**; Bonus Damage can say `[Mod]` (the Action's Modifier); **Ignore a Bane** (one range Bane less, the Banes for a [Ranged] Action while Engaged, the Bane for a target Engaged with someone else) and **Rally Points** (each Rally Point heals a formula more, for the creature and its allies within N squares) and (v0.0.187) **Effect each turn** (the creature gains an Effect at the start of each of its turns: Killer's Focus). Bonus Damage shows as a chip in the roll dialog that can be unticked. An Action's **Marks the target** box makes the creature it targets the marked one. A passive's Bonus Damage works on every matching Action (an Effect's is used up). **v0.0.207 (editability sweep):** **Chat message** (at the start of an encounter, at the start of each of its turns, or when the creature it marked goes Down: a text to its owners or everyone, `{mark}` for the marked creature, and a button that uses an Action for N AP as a free use: Marked Quarry's reminders), **Weapon property** (the creature's Fists, or all its weapons, don't have a property: Brawler, Non-threatening) and **Starting Mastery** (choose one weapon with a property: it starts at N Mastery; the Mastery tab asks: Sharpshooter). An Action's **Weapon used** says which weapon a [Weapon] Action uses: its weapon, only the Fists (Pummel), or the Fists even with no free hand (a kick: Leg Sweep).
- **Talent:** Category (v0.0.186: Combat, Mobility, Magic, Skill; shown "Combat Talent 1"), Level, Frequency, Theme, Flavor, Traits, **Prerequisites as rows** (a Talent, a Level, an Action with a trait, an Attribute at a value, or Other: not checked; all of them or any one), the Actions it grants, a **Choice** (options to pick from when it is added), and **Modifies other Actions** (an Action, category or trait; always or out of your turn; a Critical and Effects it adds).
- **Feature:** Flavor, Talents granted (slots), Actions granted, and Defenses (a bonus vs an Attribute, optionally only for some traits).
- **Class (v0.0.185):** Category, Armor Training, Flavor, Health multipliers, Rally, Speed modifier, the Weapon and Power Modifiers (two Attributes each), the **Repertoire** (Actions and Features, by Frequency) and the **Escalation** Passives and Actives. **Edit levels…** opens the **Levels window**: per level, a list of sections (drag to reorder): **Items I drop** (Granted, or a Choice of N), **Attribute increase** (pairs), **From the Repertoire** (a Frequency, Pick N) and **Talent** (Pick N, optionally one category). Each section says what the player sees. Dropping the class on a character opens the **class window** for level 1 (then each level up to the character's); raising the Level opens it for each new level. Nothing is added before you confirm; Cancel puts the Level back. Lowering the Level asks, then removes what the higher levels gave.
- **Species:** attribute choices, Species Health, Speed, Size (1 to 7: Tiny, Small, Medium 3, Large, Huge, Gargantuan, Titanic). An NPC's numbers come from its role and ratings (the Builder tab).

## Combat

- **Initiative:** the Initiative button asks for an Attribute (Instinct by default) and rolls for the creatures that haven't rolled (v0.0.273); its window has a Rolls / Skips chip per creature beside "another Attribute" (v0.0.274: a player's creature skips by default, its player clicks its portrait). Creatures are grouped by token disposition (Friendly, Neutral, Hostile, Secret, each with its own color) and the groups alternate; ties go to the higher Attribute, then to Friendly.
- **AP (pack R, v0.0.243):** 4 AP and 1 Reaction AP at the start of a turn (Minions 3 AP; every NPC has the Reaction AP). The Reaction AP (the diamond pip with an R on the sheet, the strip, the tracker and the ticker) pays only for Reactions and is paid first; the rest comes from AP kept from the turn (or the turn's own AP). Using an Action spends its AP; moving a token spends AP as an Advance (one AP per Speed of movement). Every change is posted to the chat.
- **Escalation (pack R):** a level from 1 to 3, never spent: 1 when the encounter starts (2 with **Momentum**: the tracker's button before the encounter, or the world setting; the encounter uses it up, so does a Rest), +1 at the start of each round after the first (not while Unconscious), +1 once an encounter on a Critical on one of the creature's Actions and once on the class's hook (the "Escalation hook" setting of Kindled Wrath and Thrill of the Kill; a system line says it), back to 1 at the end. - **Reactions (pack RX, v0.0.272):** a Reaction whose Moment is set (the Action's Details: Moment, Within, You too, Used on) is offered by itself when its moment comes: before a damage lands ("a Strike would damage an ally", "an ally takes damage", "a creature would go Down"), after a Strike ("a Strike is made against an ally"), after a movement or a Stand ("an enemy moves closer", "an enemy moves or stands up"; not an Evade or a Forced Movement; once a turn per Reaction for the same mover). **One chat message per moment** lists every Reaction it triggered: a row per creature and Reaction with Use / Pass for its owner, the GM's **Skip all others**, and an **auto-skip timer** counting down (world settings: on, 1 minute; click the clock to pause it). ## Conditions you can drop on a token

Drag these from the Effects compendium onto a token (or use their buttons): **Vulnerable 1, 2 and 3** (Actions against the creature gain that many boons; only the highest applies) and **Imperiled** (a creature that barely avoided a deadly hazard: all its Rolls are Doomed, plus Vulnerable 3; at the start of its turn it gets back to safety, Imperiled ends and it regains 1 less AP; the effect is removed automatically).

**Bloodlust** (from the Talent's Action) gives your [Melee, Weapon] Actions 1 boon and makes the roll dialog's **Empowered** option start checked, with 1 bane on Saves against nearly any Action. Any effect can do the same: the boons and banes setting has a checkbox for "Matching Actions are also Empowered".

## Health and rest

- **Health (pack R, v0.0.243):** the Health Pool is Vigor + Resolve: the class's high and low values (the player puts the high one on Vigor or Resolve: the line under the Health, with a swap button; Vigor by default), the species' bonuses (the Human's +3 goes with the high value unless the line says a side), the class's Stage steps at Stages 2 and 3, a floor of a third. The Attributes don't change the Health. **Rally Points** = the class's Rally + the Stage; each heals a quarter of the pool. The Modifiers are +1 at Stage 3 only (never above +4).
- **Bloodied** at Vigor damage equal to Vigor, **Strained** at Resolve damage equal to Resolve (both from damage, shown in Conditions). They have **no effect by themselves** (pack R): a system line says when a creature becomes Bloodied, Strained or Down, and cards use them ("when an ally becomes Bloodied", "against a Strained target").
- **Down** when damage reaches the Health Pool: Unconscious, Desperate, Dying or Shattered depending on the damage. Taking damage while Down rolls on the Dying or Shattered Table (a tie of dominant damage asks you). Each table roll adds a Wound of its kind, and each Wound of that kind adds 1d6 to that table.
- When damage drops below the Health Pool, the creature wakes up: Unconscious, Desperate, Dying and Shattered end.
- **Steel Yourself** (the Basic Action) can't be used while Down or Unconscious; Rally Points can still be used through other Actions, such as Lay On Hands.
- **Guard** (v0.0.181; v0.0.183: a blue ring on the Health donut, a blue segment on the token bar, and the Guard button under the Donut / Classic switch: click +1, right-click −1, Ctrl 5 at a time): a buffer. Damage applied from a card goes to the Guard first (Body or Mind alike), after any damage reduction; what the Guard takes isn't damage you take (no thresholds, no triggers, not in the damage log). It doesn't stack (the higher value is kept) and ends when the encounter ends. `@Guard` buttons give it (Smite 3 AP, Divine Surge, Bulwark of Faith).
- **Rest** (sheet button): a day in a safe place. Removes all damage, restores all Rally Points, and rolls a Body Check and a Mind Check to remove Wounds (Tier 1/2/3 removes 1/2/3).
